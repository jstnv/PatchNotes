IMPLEMENTATION
Final resolved ProjectState Graphics, Sound, Technology and Design scores are used after all production synergies. The project-saved selected Genre ratios are compared with the authoritative Primitive genre catalog; Theme is ignored. For positive total, actual shares are 100 * score / total. Deviation is half the sum of absolute share differences from the four target percentages. Modifier is max(0.75, 1 - 0.01 * max(0, deviation - 6)). There is no bonus. For absent legacy identity or zero total, deviation is recorded as 0 and modifier as 1.0.

INSERTION AND ROUNDING
The original production-quality, Scope Completion, Bug Multiplier and variance calculations are unchanged. Their combined value is clamped to 0..10 as the pre-Genre Review. Genre Fit multiplies that clamped, still-unrounded value exactly once. The product is clamped to 0..10 and rounded with the existing one-decimal half-up function. ReviewResult retains pre-Genre value, exact deviation and modifier for verification and future presentation. Manual fixture constructors retain neutral optional defaults. A committed ProjectState ReviewResult remains one-shot and Gameplay reconstruction reuses it; no second multiplication or reroll occurs.
