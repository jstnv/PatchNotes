Rule and state ownership
- Accepting the one-shot Ironclad offer creates the authoritative RunState-owned ContractState and credits exactly 40,000 cents through RunState cash. Acceptance advances zero cycles, consumes zero redraws, and does not touch ProjectState or released-game sales. The ContractState has a committed-upfront guard. A second acceptance cannot create ownership or pay again.
- ContractState completion numerator remains 4 x min(Final Scope, 12) + sum over four Cores of min(Final Core Score x 2, 12), denominator 96. Completion remainder = floor(200,000 x numerator / 96) cents. Total reward = 40,000 + remainder cents. At numerator 0/48/96, total is 40,000/140,000/240,000 cents. At numerator 4, remainder is exactly 8,333 cents and total 48,333 cents. Overproduction is capped before payment. The $400 is inside the fixed 240,000-cent Publisher Investment, never added on top.
- Hand one commits no additional Contract cash. Hand two credits only the computed remainder via RunState.complete_productive_action, before its one shared productive-cycle/calendar/earned-sales settlement boundary. ContractResult records immutable upfront, completion payment and total for the completion view. All existing two-hand card, priority, and redraw mechanics remain unchanged.
- Acceptance preflights signed-cent overflow before creating ownership. During the acceptance transaction, RunState blocks observer signals until ownership and exact cash both commit, then emits cash and Contract notifications. An unexpected cash failure removes the new ContractState; callers see no accepted offer. Completion still uses the existing central preflight and commit boundary: failed or overflowing actions preserve cash, cycle, sales, redraws, and ContractState. Repeated callbacks and phase reconstruction reuse the paid state/result and cannot pay either portion twice.
