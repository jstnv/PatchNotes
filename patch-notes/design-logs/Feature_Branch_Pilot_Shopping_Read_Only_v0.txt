Status: partial design evidence only. No later Feature card or Store behavior was added or approved.

Authority and exact conflict
Read current To Do List branch handoff, cumulative authority, Primitive card ledger, current Feature Store ledger/source, first-Studio economy and the 6,300-path publisher sampler. The To Do List says a later Store node should cost one productive cycle, while the current Feature Store purchase code and earlier explicit Studio Feature Store rule make purchases zero-cycle. Both interpretations were modeled as sensitivity arms; this trial cannot settle that conflict. Branching Dialogue has no supplied card definition, prerequisite, phase, price, or play cost in the accessible queue/ledger, so its requested gameplay comparison is blocked. Later Feature play costs are also TBD. The proposed four cards are not in the purchasable ledger and must not enter ordinary draws yet.

Candidate shopping bound
Used 6,300 identical first-game paths and their owned Primitive Features, current Ironclad $400 acceptance plus completion remainder, exact Month 1 sales/settlement, and hypothetical $75 bill with zero/one staff. For each of six two-card combinations and the four-card pilot, tested missing-parent acquisition and pilot base prices. The four unapproved candidate prices/effects from the queue are Background Music (Music parent, $950, Sound +3/Scope 1), Sub-Areas (Levels, $1,700, Graphics +3/Design +2/Scope 2), Boss Battles (Enemies, $2,800, Graphics/Sound/Technology +3 each/Scope 3), and Character Classes (General Combat plus Save Files, $1,700, Technology +2/Design +3/Scope 2). The full four-card set costs $7,150 before missing parents and would add eight printed Scope. The trial allows at most one missing Primitive parent to be bought as the currently modeled $450 reserve, and Save Files at its current $2,200 Store price. Paths needing more than one missing Primitive parent are marked prerequisite-gated rather than silently granting multiple reserves. No familiarity discount is assumed, giving an upper-cost bound.

At current zero-cycle Store timing and zero staff, the Background Music/Sub-Areas pair meets that prerequisite bound in 52.89% of paths and is fully affordable in 47.90% of all paths. Charging one productive cycle per later Store node lowers affordability to 46.81%. The full four-card pilot meets the prerequisite bound in 13.62%, but is affordable in 0% at either Store timing. Current Store with no pilot purchase is a 100% zero-cost control. All pair combinations, one-staff cases, detailed blockers, shopping costs and cash outcomes are in the summary/raw file.

Boundary and next step
This is a purchase feasibility simulation, not a card-draw/Review playtest. It cannot establish pilot Core balance, effect on Scope completion, familiarity discounts, or whether Branching Dialogue belongs in the set. The existing card schema exposes primary and one secondary Core stat, while the proposed Boss Battles has three Core effects; a design-approved representation is needed before faithful hand/synergy simulation. Resolve the zero-versus-one-cycle Store purchase rule, later Feature play cost, multi-parent purchase/familiarity semantics, and exact Branching Dialogue definition; then run a matched Godot draw-by-draw Game 2 trial before implementation. Do not infer that the four-card set is viable merely from its printed G6/S6/T5/D5 scores.
