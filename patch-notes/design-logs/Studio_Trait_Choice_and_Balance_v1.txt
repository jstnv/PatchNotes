CHOICE RULE
At studio creation, the player manually selects exactly one background trait: Family Funding, Cult Following, or Publisher Connections. The three are mutually exclusive; no random/default assignment or stacking. The separately proposed Genre specialty is an optional second type of choice, not a second company background trait. The main-menu implementation must eventually require a background selection before creating RunState and show its actual benefit; do not add inert choices before effects and dependent systems are locked.

TRIAL INPUTS ONLY — NOT IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORITY
Family Funding: +$300 initial cash, above the existing $5,500. Cult Following: +200 initial studio fans. Publisher Connections: +15% cents on the first completed Ironclad Contract, capped naturally at +$360 on its $2,400 maximum payout. Matching Genre specialty: +10 launch Awareness on a game of the chosen Genre. All eight Genre IDs come from the authoritative roster. Family and Publisher bonuses never change Review or fan totals; Cult fans affect Awareness through the proposed saturated 150 × fans/(fans+300) curve; specialty does not alter the locked Genre Fit Review modifier. The model uses the prior candidate 5/10-neutral fan gain/loss curve, with zero-loss and half-loss sensitivity. No cash is spent from projected sales before actual earning and month-boundary settlement.

LEGAL FIRST GAME (20–23 STARTING SCOPE; n=1,440 PAIRED PATHS)
No-trait median Month 1 units 187 (p10 73, p90 380). Candidate Cult +200 fans raises the median to 224, with 60 launch Awareness from the proposed saturated formula; Family and Publisher leave units at 187. Matching +10- Awareness Genre specialty alone raises median units to 194. These are Month 1 projections; modeled cash only becomes spendable at the existing settlement boundary.

With the separate $75/month bill, one $100 employee, two $25 raises, and current zero-fee course assumptions from the prior payroll experiment, no-trait median cash after Game 1 is $760; Family is $1,060, while Cult and Publisher remain $760 at that point. After the two Contract hands, median cash is $3,555.01 control, $3,855.01 Family, $3,734.54 Cult and $3,858.44 Publisher. Publisher median first-Contract payout is $2,357.50 versus $2,050 control, and it arrives only at Contract completion. The separate payroll/bill numbers are themselves unimplemented prototype inputs, so these are stress tests, not actual current-game bankruptcy rates.

The same lean Game 2 path has modeled shortfall 0.14% control, 0% Family, 0.07% Cult and 0% Publisher. Adding a separate optional $1,700 Store-node cash/cycle stress produces shortfalls 77.29% control, 58.06% Family, 58.12% Cult and 57.08% Publisher. No branch eligibility or card effect was invented for that optional-node stress. By policy, control optional-node shortfall is 90.2% conservative, 81.9% ordinary, 59.8% optimized; Family 75.8/61.0/37.3%, Cult 83.1/62.7/28.5%, Publisher 76.7/59.4/35.2%. Thus the best cash outcome changes with performance and timing; none of these policy heuristics represents human skill.

BELOW-20 OPT-IN (n=1,080)
The optional $1,700-node shortfall is 14.44% control, 6.57% Family, 9.72% Cult, and 7.50% Publisher. Lean Game 2 has no modeled shortfall in this cohort. The much lower node shortfalls mainly reflect less initial Feature spending; this cohort must not be combined with the legal 20–23 design-target start when tuning bonuses.

FAN AND GENRE SENSITIVITY
With the proposed 15%-per-review-point loss below Review 5, the 200-fan Cult start survives the first weak release in all 250 common trajectories, but falls from 200 to median 104 fans after the first release and median 10 after ten sampled releases. With half-strength loss, median fans after first/ten are 152/51; with no loss, 200/200. The first-release sold-unit benefit remains, but long-run trait value changes sharply with the not-yet-locked loss rule. At the legal first game, 100/200/300 starting fans yield mean Month 1 units 237.50/253.38/263.75, respectively, versus 211.91 control. A Review of exactly 5.0 gains and loses zero fans in every sensitivity.

The optional +10 matching-Genre Awareness produces mean legal first-game units 218.78 versus 211.91 control; +5 and +20 produce 215.35 and 225.73. The simulated player chooses a specialty matching Game 1's Genre and later games match at sampled Genre frequencies; this is an intentional-choice upper bound for the first game and a limited approximation thereafter. It is separate from the background trait in every paired case.

VALUE SENSITIVITY AND DECISION
With the optional $1,700 node, Family +$150/$300/$450 has legal-start shortfalls 68.06/58.06/48.06%. Publisher +5/+15/+25% yields median after-Contract cash $3,659.48/$3,858.44/$4,064.95. The tested +$300 Family and +15% Publisher are near each other after Contract, but Family arrives earlier while Publisher varies with earned completion. Cult's sale advantage can overtake either in optimized runs, yet may decay after poor releases. No universally dominant option is proven, and no exact values should be locked from this model alone.

LIMITS, TESTS, AND HANDOFF
The sampler keeps the same first-game policy actions across traits; extra Family cash does not cause the policy to buy or play more Features. The later-game cohort uses expanded Primitive supply; later Store Feature play costs are undefined. Fan saturation, gain and loss are candidate curves, not runtime systems. Publisher Connections' trial cash multiplier does not implement early-offer access or change eligibility. The payroll/bill and course timeline is a prior prototype. Only Month 1 sales are modeled; no Month 2+, hiring, real salary, durable save/load, contract repeats, or publisher reputation is inferred. The current model's low Review distribution needs actual play traces.

Script assertions cover eight Genres, Review-5 fan neutrality, exact +$360 maximum Contract bonus, paired unchanged units for cash traits, +$300 exact Studio cash, and exact first Contract bonus cents.
