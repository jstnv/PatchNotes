Presentation decisions
- Five focusable lane buttons switch the visible tree without changing the run. Nodes retain the existing owned, available, needs-cash and locked states. Details and tooltips show actual ledger phase, Scope, Core values, prerequisites and prices.
- Every branch places its root below its direct child, with an upward arrow. The three-Gameplay ownership gate is a label below Difficulty Levels, not a new card or discount parent.
- Independent roots are side by side within a lane and the tree scrolls horizontally. Lane buttons make wide forests navigable at 1152x648 and 900x600. Selecting a different lane clears detail selection; purchase and reopening preserve the selected node and current lane/scroll. A lane's horizontal/vertical offset is restored on return.
- Visual theme remains the existing Studio palette. Existing owned-pool totals remain visible above the lane navigation.
