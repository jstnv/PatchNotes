Status: sensitivity evidence only. Month 2+ sales, campaigns, payroll and bills are not implemented or locked.

Authority and method
Read To Do List follow-up, cumulative authority, the V1 campaign log, current Review trace summary, Godot scaled-Awareness and exact Month 1 sales code, RunState settlement/Contract code, and the 6,300-path publisher sampler. Current Godot first-game automated Review p10/median/p90 anchors are cautious 0.6/1.0/1.6, ordinary 1.9/2.6/3.3, optimizer 2.6/3.6/4.9. A Review 9.0 setup is synthetic, prompted by the player's later-game human observation; no 9.0 source trace was supplied. The historical older sampler's low Review distribution is not substituted for these current anchors.

Candidate later-month equation: Month 2 organic Awareness = 50% of launch Awareness + tested 0%, 15%, or 30% of the separate 200-point organic launch discovery baseline. Later monthly organic Awareness multiplies by 0.25 + 0.055 × Final Review, flooring the scaled value to four decimals; monthly units use the current scaled-Awareness sales numerator without Month 1's +200 term. This is why Review 1.3, launch Awareness 100, neutral market gives exact Month 2 units 23/37/51 for 0/15/30% baseline carryover. Existing Month 1 arithmetic stays unchanged. Campaigns cost $100 and one productive cycle, target one release for one month, and add either V1's 10% of launch Awareness or a fixed +10 Awareness points, divided by (1 + prior accepted campaigns). Campaigns act in the first release-age cycle of the target month. A matched control uses a neutral productive cycle in the same slot.

Full-calendar replay
Replayed 420 identical sampled first-game starter/action/market/Contract/Game-2 paths across both half-month alignments for each of 12 Review/Awareness anchors, three carryovers, two boost definitions, and single/repeat/first-dormant policies against matched no-campaign controls. Ironclad now pays the locked $400 at acceptance and floor($2,000 × numerator / 96) at completion before the same-boundary net sales transaction. Each arm tracks 24 month boundaries with earned/settled exact cents, provisional $75 bill, one $100 salary, and Game 2 action costs. The optional $1,700 node and zero/two-staff sensitivities use subsets. Full per-boundary cash percentiles and first-shortfall rates are in the summary; paired raw boundary vectors and representative line-item traces are in the compressed result.

At 15% discovery carryover and fixed +10 campaign, the single campaign's median 24-boundary cash difference from its matched no-campaign control is -$37.06 for current ordinary median Review 2.6 (p10/p90 -$51.05/-$30.07), +$25.87 for optimizer p90 Review 4.9 (p10/p90 -$9.09/+$39.86), and +$123.77 for synthetic Review 9.0 (p10/p90 +$67.83/+$158.74). Campaign shortfall is 1.43%, 0%, and 0% respectively in the one-staff/no-node arm. At Review 9.0, median first zero-sale month is 21; a campaign at that dormant month gives a median +$130.77 net cash versus a matched neutral action. Repeating every month accepts 23 campaigns but loses a median $1,488.81 over 24 boundaries; the pure marginal model shows only the first two Review-9 campaign months profitable, so no sustained profitable repeat loop appears.

The matched 750-Month-1-unit pair matters: Review 4.0 with Awareness 325 receives a median -$2.10 from fixed +10, while Review 7.0 with Awareness 100 receives +$74.83. V1's 10%-of-launch boost instead gives the marketing-heavy Review-4 game a much larger one-shot gain because 32.5 points are added rather than 10. Fixed points favor actual Review quality without using launch marketing as campaign reach. At 15% carryover, the neutral-market Month 2 base for current ordinary median Review 2.6/Awareness 109 is 78 units; at 0/30% it is 50/106. Monthly cash, cumulative units, exact 70%-share revenue, first dormancy, $50/$100/$200 costs, and ±5 percentage-point retention tests are preserved in the two summaries/raw files.

Limits and recommendation
All later-month sales and expense rules here are candidates. The full replay retains the prior Game 2 Review/Marketing proxy, not an actual second release result. Simulated negative balances mark first affordability failure; they are not an implemented financing option. A single fixed-point campaign is not economical for ordinary low-Review first games, while it can help a strong or revived game. The 15% baseline carryover and fixed +10 boost merit a narrower later-game human trace trial; neither should be locked from this model. Preserve the current Month 1 sale/settlement code and defer fanbase, payroll and campaign implementation until the finance boundary and player-facing design are resolved.
