PATCH NOTES — DESIGN/ALPHA REDRAW CLASS PARITY READ-ONLY TRIAL v1
2026-09-27 | Source revision ba616515ed1b039fd0da7cf03f73288fd7ccd8e4
Status: simulated only. The current Design/Alpha same-type redraw code was not changed.

Authority and method
Read current To Do List redraw-class request, cumulative authority, shared redraw/priority log, current Godot Design/Alpha draw and redraw code, card ledger, Review/Beta Marketing/sales rules, and repository state. Replayed 600 paired legal 20–23 purchased-Scope first-game paths (200 each cautious, ordinary and optimizer; Sound-focused and mixed subsets) with the same starter roster and seeded draws. Current arm preserves Feature-to-Feature and Pass-to-Pass redraw. Candidate arm chooses Feature or Pass at equal weight when both legal classes exist, then selects a legal definition under existing weighting; if only one class remains, it falls back to that class. Initial draws, Beta, Contract, finite Feature exhaustion, renewable Passes, four-redraw cap, +1 per productive cycle, zero-cycle redraw, and one-card replacement remain unchanged in the simulation. It is a Python policy model, not human play or exact Godot PRNG parity.

Measured redraw behavior
Current: 5,201 attempts, 5,198 success, three rejected, 31 Feature-to-Feature and 5,167 Pass-to-Pass; no class changes. Where both classes were available, a Feature was the result only 1.20% of the time because almost all selected old cards were Passes. Parity: 5,200 attempts and success, 1,098 Pass-to-Feature, 32 Feature-to-Pass, 28 Feature-to-Feature and 4,042 Pass-to-Pass. Given both legal classes, Feature result was 50.47%; 1,434 redraws exercised the one-class fallback. Candidate selection excludes the immediately replaced definition and still reserves visible finite Features. No cash or calendar blocker appeared in either arm.

Gameplay outcome
Cautious policy never redraws and is unchanged. Ordinary policy gains mean +0.065 Review, +0.28 Scope and +7.245 Month 1 units from parity; median Review difference is +0.1, with wide p10/p90 -0.6/+0.7. Optimizer policy loses mean 0.10 Review and 11.825 units; median Review difference is -0.1, with p10/p90 -1.0/+0.7. Mean Sound Feature plays change by only +0.02 in ordinary and zero in optimizer, including Sound-focused cohorts. Synergy counts and Feature-play costs shift modestly but neither strategy consistently improves. Equal class odds reduce Pass-only redraw outcomes, yet do not by themselves prove relief from the Sound-specialization meta. The ordinary policy's additional Scope has a cost; changes are not free score.

Decision and validation
This run supports the class-parity UI intent and a clear one-class fallback, but not a balance claim that parity fixes Sound access or Review. If the player wants the requested feel change implemented, retain one-card replacement, no immediate same-definition repeat, finite exhaustion, shared redraw accounting, and unchanged initial/Beta/Contract draws; then verify current and proposed distributions in live Godot under scarce Sound supply. A separate design decision should address whether a Pass fishing pattern is desirable. No gameplay rule was locked by this analysis.
