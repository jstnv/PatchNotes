2026-09-27 | Source revision ba616515ed1b039fd0da7cf03f73288fd7ccd8e4
Status: design evidence only. No employee, course, salary, or perk was implemented or approved.

Authority and method
Read current To Do List trial roster (Production Specialist, QA Analyst, Contracts Specialist), cumulative Implementation Discoveries and Locked Rules, Employee Production Reward Counterfactual v1, QA Analyst counterfactual, current card/market ledgers, production/Beta/Contract/Review/sales source, and the clean starting worktree. Replayed 600 deterministic Python paths: 200 legal 20–23 starter Scope, 200 below-20 opt-in, and 200 expanded all-Primitive paths. Each path retains the same owned cards, production hands, Beta actions, Review variance, market, Contract draw seed, and Game 2 supply across no-employee, each specialist, all three two-person pairs, and three-person combined arms. This is automated policy evidence, not human play or Godot PRNG parity.

Results in the legal first-game cohort
- Production matching Pass+Feature qualifying Design hand: 60.5% opportunity. If the specialist is hired, permanent plus six-cycle temporary discounts save $64.06 on average in Game 1; median $89.50 among all paths. Fixed actions leave Scope and Review unchanged. The script separately records gross-cost failure that a discount might make affordable, but does not secretly add that hand.
- QA one productive Search followed by a later productive Debug: 40.5% opportunity under the tested action mix, versus 8.0% for the rejected two-Search trigger. Marketing-first Beta gives only 9.5% QA qualification. The proposed extra Debug fix averages 0.075 Bug per path; extra post-award Search discoveries average zero in these short first-game Beta schedules. Mean Review improvement is +0.002/10; no material 5.0 or 7.0 crossing. Current all-four-Marketing specialization is included in the Beta replay.
- Contract Specialist with ordinary one-redraw-per-hand policy: zero qualifying first hands, hence zero additional redraws or payout effect. Deliberately spending up to four redraws in hand one qualifies in 12.5% of legal paths; the +2 restoration supplies 0.25 actual extra redraws per path on average, but paired payout gain is zero in this 200-path sample. The present build has only one Ironclad Contract, so career-long repeat value has no live source.
- With the provisional $75 bill, $100 monthly salary per employee, and two $25 course raises per employee, the optional $1,700 Store node makes modeled Game 2 cash shortfall 4.5% with no employee, 57.5% with Production, 65.5% with QA or Contracts, and 100% with all three. These are counterfactual paths continued below zero to locate first failure; negative cash is not legal gameplay. With no courses and no optional node, all single-specialist legal paths remain nonnegative, but one specialist's payroll still exceeds its usual direct cash benefit by Game 2 release. Game 2 sales are not credited before release.

Trigger and finance semantics tested
Only successful committed hands qualify. The revised QA trigger needs ordinary Search discovery and ordinary later Debug repair; bonuses clamp to remaining Hidden/Known Bugs. Production discounts are capped at actual Feature play cost, $60 permanent and $80 temporary per project. The temporary window starts after the qualifying hand and counts the next six successful productive cycles; redraws and failed attempts count zero. Contract redraws use the shared maximum of four and restore after the normal +1 at hand one's productive boundary. Contract Features remain free. Ironclad retains a $400 once-only acceptance advance and floor($2,000 × numerator / 96) completion remainder. Zero-cycle, zero-fee hires were modeled either before first Pre-Development or at Studio; payroll starts only while hired. A course costs $0, advances one cycle, and raises salary by a provisional $25 from the same boundary in the main arm. Both first-bill and payroll-entry timing remain open.

Sensitivity and decision
Across all 600 paths, 50% permanent cap $40/60/80 and temporary rate 10/20/30% six-cycle cases are in the summary. The default $60/20% combination saves $51.49 on average across all cohorts; even $80/30% saves $56.13. The old two-Search QA challenge is a poor fit. The Contract redraw reward needs a different opportunity or a repeatable offer source before it can carry a salary. Do not approve a salary-supported three-person roster from these values. Test hire, first-bill, course-raise, insufficiency, and alternate Beta policies with real UI traces before implementation. No Generalist, SideStreet offer, Promotion, or passive score was added.
