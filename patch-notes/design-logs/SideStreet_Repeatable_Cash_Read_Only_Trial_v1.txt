Status: design evidence only. No SideStreet offer, second Contract history, payroll, or later sales was implemented or approved.

Authority and method
Read current To Do List cash-only SideStreet handoff, cumulative Implementation Discoveries and Locked Rules, publisher progression, Ironclad guaranteed-payment trial, 6,300-path publisher cash/payroll sampler, Contract and sales source. Reused identical 6,300 sampled first-game paths for all $800/$1,200/$1,600 maximum SideStreet cash caps, both half-month alignments, early/late ordering, zero/one/two staff, zero/two courses, and lean Game 2 with/without an optional $1,700 later Store node. Each SideStreet offer has a distinct deterministic draw seed and local finite-Feature exhaustion. The trial reuses the existing two-hand Primitive Contract numerator, pays floor(cap cents × numerator / 96) once upon hand-two completion, and charges no Contract Feature play cost. It does not use the separate unapproved $2,160/Promotion alternative. All SideStreet lifecycle and staff rules are hypothetical.

Measured completion and payout
First SideStreet numerator p10/median/p90 = 52/76/94; second = 56/78/94, from separate draws. At cap $800, first payout p10/median/p90 = $433.33/$633.33/$783.33 and second = $466.66/$650.00/$783.33. At $1,200, first = $650.00/$950.00/$1,175.00 and second = $700.00/$975.00/$1,175.00. At $1,600, first = $866.66/$1,266.66/$1,566.66 and second = $933.33/$1,300.00/$1,566.66. Exact cents use integer floor; no cash is paid on offer browsing or acceptance in this trial.

Cash and reachability
With SideStreet 1 completed before Game 2, one staff, two courses, and the optional node, first shortfall is 29.51% at $800, 15.63% at $1,200, and 6.51% at $1,600. Median cash immediately before Game 2 Feature play is $1,567.78/$1,911.98/$2,261.61 respectively; median end cash after SideStreet 2 is $991.24/$1,689.66/$2,391.86. At $1,200, first failures are SideStreet 2 hand 1 (240), Game 2 (701), and Game 2 Feature play (44) among 6,300 paths. Early no-staff/no-node has no shortfall at any cap. Completing Ironclad and SideStreet 1 only after Game 2 leaves the early funding gap: 81.40% shortfall in the one-staff/two-course/node arm at every SideStreet cap, because that reward arrives too late. The optional node is therefore not automatically affordable at $1,200 or even $1,600.

The candidate one-offer-per-distinct-release lifecycle would allow Ironclad plus SideStreet offers linked to releases 1 and 2 to reach Starwave's existing two-release/three-completion count on nonnegative paths. The script identifies those distinct hypothetical IDs and reports both counterfactual three-completion paths and paths that stay nonnegative; it does not claim Starwave is currently reachable. Current runtime has only the one-shot Ironclad Contract and no SideStreet offer or committed additional Contract history. A repeated callback, release re-registration, or duplicate ID must never create a second payout in future implementation; that invariant remains an implementation requirement, not a runtime test of this proposal.

Timing, limitations, and decision
Ironclad remains $400 at acceptance plus floor($2,000 × numerator / 96) at hand two. Direct completion payouts are applied before that hand's month-boundary settlement, matching RunState's productive-cycle ordering. Earned Month 1 sales, $75 monthly bill, $100 salary/staff, and $25 raise/course are combined at each modeled boundary. Hire and course cycles, first bill, payroll-entry, course-raise timing and negative-cash handling are candidates. Main optional later Store node spends $1,700 and one cycle per To Do candidate; the zero-cycle current Store behavior and Game-2-sales proxy are separate sensitivity arms. Main cash horizon gives Game 2 no post-release sales (lower bound); it does not model Month 2+. Negative balances are counterfactual first-failure markers, never playable credit. A $1,200 cap remains a plausible test candidate, not a lock. The cash cap cannot repair rewards delayed until after the intended purchase.
