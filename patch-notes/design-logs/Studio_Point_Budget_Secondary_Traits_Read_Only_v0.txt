PATCH NOTES — STUDIO POINT-BUDGET SECONDARY TRAITS READ-ONLY TRIAL v0
2026-09-27 | Source revision ba616515ed1b039fd0da7cf03f73288fd7ccd8e4
Status: candidate balance evidence only. No secondary trait, point budget, fanbase, payroll, or new studio creation UI was implemented or approved.

Authority and method
Read current To Do List point-budget and secondary roster, cumulative authority, Studio Trait Choice and Balance v1, publisher/Contract/Review/Genre/market logs, and live code. Preserved the locked manual exactly-one background choice (Family Funding, Cult Following, or Publisher Connections) and separate optional Genre specialty. Enumerated all 24 legal combinations of up to two positive traits and one negative trait under the proposed four-point allowance, two/three-point positive prices, one/two-point negative refunds, and $50 per unspent point up to $300. Replayed each on 840 paired source paths: legal 20–23 starter Scope, below-20 opt-in, three production policies, and two calendar alignments. The main candidate cash path has a $75 monthly bill, one $100-salary employee, two $25 course raises, and an optional $1,700 Store node. All of those money/time values remain unapproved.

Candidate traits tested
Student Loan refunds two points but costs $25 at each of 96 monthly boundaries, beginning with the first boundary after run creation. Expensive Lease refunds one and adds $15 to each monthly bill. Unknown Name refunds two and subtracts 15 launch Awareness points from each of the first two releases. Resourceful costs two and reduces the first eligible postlaunch Store-node purchase in a project by $100. Lean Production costs three and saves 10% of Design/Alpha Feature-play cash, at most $100 per project. Studio Buzz costs three and adds 10 launch Awareness points per release. The older background numeric candidates (+$300 Family, +200 Cult fans, +15% separate first-Contract cash Publisher) and optional +10 matching-Genre Awareness were only scenario inputs, not locks. Lean savings are integer-dollar exact in the current Primitive play-cost model; Store familiarity interactions remain untested.

Results and exploit checks
For Family Funding with the optional node, zero secondary modifiers yields a 26.79% modeled first shortfall and median $456.14 at Game 2 release. Student Loan alone raises shortfall to 37.02% and reduces median release cash to $259.74. The no-future-sales bound for its remaining 96 dues is median -$1,843.87; that bound omits later revenue and ordinary future bills/payroll, so it is a debt stress test rather than a forecast. Two refunded points used only as cash supply at most $100 immediately; four $25 dues erase that advantage.

Unknown Name is an early cash exploit in this low-Review sample: Family Funding plus Lean and Unknown Name gives a 23.45% shortfall and median $510.03 at Game 2 release, versus $474.89 for Lean without a drawback. The median first-game sales penalty is only seven units, while the refunded points can fund a larger immediate conversion. This does not establish long-run dominance: Game 2's Awareness penalty has only a Game-1-Review/Marketing/market proxy because the cash horizon stops at Game 2 release. Resourceful alone ties no modifier on median cash when the optional node is bought: its $100 Store saving is offset by $100 of foregone point conversion, before one-cycle calendar effects. The full summary reports each background, every legal build, optional-node on/off, first shortfall stage, cash, units, and 0/1 Store-cycle sensitivities.

Decision and open rules
Do not lock the candidate four-point budget or any trait numbers yet. Unknown Name's two-point refund is too easy to cash out in weak early-game paths; the 96-month Student Loan is much heavier than its short-term refund and requires explicit insufficiency handling. Test a smaller Unknown Name refund or a more consequential, clearly explained downside, and a shorter or differently priced loan, against real Game 2 sales and fan effects. Resolve trait compatibility, background-bonus cap, additive Awareness order, familiarity order, hiring/course/bill timing, save/load, and one-time conversion guards before implementation. No background is stacked with another in this trial.
