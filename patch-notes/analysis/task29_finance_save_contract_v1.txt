Atomic checkpoint
Persist only between complete actions, outside _productive_cycle_in_progress/_committing_cycle_cash/_publishing_cycle and UI animation transaction locks. Required commit includes direct effect (ProjectState or ContractState), exact cash, central cycle, redraw refill, all title earning/settlement, due and paid rent, finance journal, publisher/contract completion IDs and ownership/familiarity changes. Never save one portion as authoritative ahead of another. Presentation tween state, open menus and report selection are transient; reconstruction cannot execute callbacks, settle income, pay rent, refill redraws or mint payout IDs.

Restore validation
Validate old base-state invariants; deep-copy typed finance data; replay the entire action journal from initial financing; compare every monthly row/obligation/payment/transaction; require last_cycle and cash_cents to equal RunState and aggregate unsettled_sales_net_cents to match title entitlement less settled. Missing or corrupted history must display unavailable and cannot silently disable rent for a named Studio. Current runtime rejects productive actions with invalid named-Studio finance provenance. Future migrations require explicit version policy; no schema repair or old save invention is authorized here.

Shortfall and exactly-once boundaries
First rent aftercycle2, then each productive even cycle. Direct cost must be affordable before that action's receipts. All income fits64bit before rent. Settle earned sales before bills. Pay oldest debt first; partial payments leave explicit arrears without negativecash. While arrears exist, a productive action must clear all old/new due rent or reject completely. Positive external receipts service arrears without time; passive navigation and eligible launch remain free. expected_cycle rejects stale callback replays; contract/release ID guards remain authoritative. Rent due uniqueness derives from strictly sequential committed cycles, not UI events.

Deferred lending extension
No loan balances, approved score/terms or borrowing actions are implemented. Future loans need typed loan IDs, origination cycle, original/remaining principal, fixed total interest, exact-cent installment schedule/firstdue, paid/unpaid components, immutable one-time-startup eligibility flag independent of payoff, missed-payment evidence and checked payoff transactions. Keep Student Loan trait/employee payroll separate. Those future fields and values require the approved lending design, not inference from this foundation.
