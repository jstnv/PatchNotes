## Finding

On the retained private cycle-528 pair (`docs/codex/findings/performance-audit-20261103-v1/long-legal-528-final-v1.json`, SHA-256 `73f71b28a2265bda007ee2e0e83bfd6f12638cbf28eef1334e68049d517c4a87`), one cold headless serial trace parsed JSON in 1.61 s and validated the player in 17.94 s. Each of the eight rivals validated in 15.08–20.43 s, with no errors. These owner timings are serial, not a direct estimate of the game's parallel wall time. The debug trace's printed release count used the wrong payload nesting and must not be treated as a release count; the fixture contains 52 player releases and 30–31 releases per rival.

A separate cold player decomposition measured DTO decode 644 ms, finance validation 546 ms, and Fanbase validation 16,968 ms. The latter includes its own cold sales checks and cycle-by-cycle replay. After the replay, all 52 sales-record validations took 66 ms with warmed proofs. Scanning every player release's historical Begin receipt took 20 ms; reconstructing Market facts for individual cycles 96, 264 and 528 took 8.66, 6.07 and 7.58 ms respectively. These are local stage samples, not end-to-end/device-wide claims. Evidence: `.codex-godot-temp/performance-trace-20261104/trace.log` and `trace_detail.log`.

## Isolated candidates

The second also skips a repeated current-record validation only for private replay records returned directly from checked `ReleasedGameSales.create`/`next_cycle`, or exact duplicates of already-checked closed records. Public `plan` still deep-copies and validates; saved records are still validated before replay; numeric/order checks and the final exact ledger comparison remain.
