## Evidence that bears on pay

The [bounded comparison](2026-10-06-benefit-payroll-comparison-v1.md) used legal native card/action/sales routes and counterfactual exact-cent payroll. It predates live employee payroll, Bank loans, active traits and future Store/Fanbase/publisher packages. These are deterministic sampled paths, not player success rates.

The $10→$50 median final-cash difference is $640 across the 24 arms, consistent with sixteen additional $40 payroll differences; it is a cost difference within those modeled paths, not an employee return on investment. Their minimum cash low was $0 at both $10 and $50. At $75/$100, the first recorded extra failures were unaffordable Feature plays at cycle 42. Immediate $100 hiring after zero-cash long first games failed some fixed-action paths; waiting for actual Contract receipts/settled sales preserved all 32 at $10. Hiring remains optional, and a Contract is not a prerequisite.

The benefit evidence is mixed. Bundled planning was used in sampled routes but had zero median Game-2/Game-3 Review change, with both gains and losses. The two-employee sample sometimes saturated at the same number of useful adjustments as one employee. No human deliberate-choice trace establishes what the option is worth. Do not set wage by attributing a nearby settlement windfall to the employee, or by requiring every hire to earn back its cost in direct cash.

That creates a visible recurring choice while retaining all sampled one-employee chronology-correct paths at the tested hiring opportunities. It is a test target, **not a final salary or instruction to change runtime now**.

At $50, a one-person bill is $550/month including base rent, or $565 with the active $515 Expensive Lease trait. Bank capacity subtracts scheduled payroll once: versus the $10 trial, $40 more wage reduces the positive monthly installment capacity by $10 under the approved divide-by-four formula, all else equal. A loan is a separate financing choice, not proof that wages are self-supporting. At 19 due months, the pure fixed-schedule difference from $10 to $50 is $760; the implemented [three-release route](../../findings/2026-10-07-employees-implementation-v1.md) paid 19 $10 bills, but it has **not** been replayed at $50, so its final cash and actions cannot be asserted for that wage.

## What must be settled before final wage approval

1. **Representative income and choices:** Keep the working lifespan coefficients fixed while capturing actual overlapping-title earned and settled net sales, short/long first games, and a deliberate human or visible-information Production-use route. Optional use frequency and useful decisions matter more than a single Review or cash average.
2. **Store spending:** Price, fee and legal access for any first-era candidate node used in the route must be explicit. Current [Task2 findings](../../findings/2026-10-07-task2-store-comparison-v1.md) show that early purchasing and paid plays can block later work; candidate prices/fees remain OPEN. Do not reserve wage headroom by silently dropping an owned card or inventing future Store income.
3. **Other live obligations and income:** Test actual $500/$515 rent, active traits and Bank bills; use no-loan and separately chosen-loan paths. Count only implemented Ironclad/SideStreet receipts. Crown/Neon advances, Fanbase on main and future publisher/Store packages may be added only after their own rules are live or as labeled sensitivities. Payroll takes its approved place between rent and bank.

The entire later-era catalog and every publisher are not prerequisites. A stable early-game shopping path, current obligations, and representative settled portfolio sales are enough for a bounded wage decision.

When the surrounding economy slice is stable, compare no hire with delayed $100 hire at $10/$25/$50/$75 per month; retain $100 as an upper stress reference only if the lower arms do not separate. Keep the same legal specialty, seed, initial cash ledger and pre-hire actions. Use both the genuine $5,500 legacy fixture and current no-trait $5,700 creation separately. At each matched calendar boundary, record the date and source of every earned/settled title receipt, direct Contract receipt, rent/payroll/bank due and paid cents, cash low, arrears/credit, first blocked action, owned Store access/play, next-game funding, and training/use/Review decisions. Censor an unaffordable arm at its first genuine failure; do not continue with negative cash. A changed draw or production choice breaks downstream fixed-action identity, so report those routes as adaptive outcomes. Test $515 Lease and chosen Bank debt as separate strata; do not pool them with no-trait/no-loan controls.

**Decision gate:** Prefer $50 if it remains a meaningful optional hire across ordinary and long legal paths without making a loan or Contract mandatory, and if deliberate use gives a credible strategic choice. Prefer $25 if $50 materially pushes otherwise viable next-game/Store actions into arrears or makes the option routinely unattractive. Keep $10 temporarily and revisit the benefit if neither higher wage has a useful player-facing tradeoff. Consider $75 only with stronger repeatable use and ample settled-cash evidence. Do not call any amount final from the old counterfactual grid alone.
