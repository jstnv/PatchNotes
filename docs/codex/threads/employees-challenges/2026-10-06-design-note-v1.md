## Authority and current baseline

**LOCKED design:** Employees are Studio-owned specialists that persist across projects and should create meaningful choices in existing production, Beta, Contract, or redraw actions. They generally enable strategies rather than award passive/free Core score. Generalist is the stated possible exception: Core contribution through a committed existing action may be considered, but its trigger, size, and even inclusion are **OPEN**. A completed challenge grants an immediate, permanent employee-owned benefit; any temporary effect needs an explicit project or productive-cycle window. The Production Specialist's established trigger is the first **successfully committed Design hand** with a Core Pass and at least one Feature matching that Pass's **printed primary or secondary Core**, once per employee per project. Failure, unaffordability, reconstruction, and duplicate callbacks do not grant it. No employee or course is currently playable. Sources: [employee design](../../design/employees-courses.md), [cross-system decisions](../../DECISIONS.md), and the local Implementation Discoveries archive cited in the previous thread intake.

**LOCKED economy / ACTIVE lifespan baseline:** Base capital is **$5,500**. Current trait-selection creation separately issues a once-only **$200 financing receipt** when four points remain unused; a genuine current-flow neutral case can therefore start at $5,700. These are different ledgers, not interchangeable starting cash. Rent is **$500 per completed calendar month**, every two productive cycles. A first release within roughly **14 development cycles** is a pacing target, not a hard cap; track total run cycles as well. The working lifespan and campaign coefficients are **ACTIVE/TRIAL** but are the fixed comparison baseline here. Launch pays nothing; Month 1 earns over two subsequent productive cycles, and only the actual 70% net entitlement settles at the applicable boundary. Concurrent releases accrue together. Cash, rent, typed obligations, credit, and rollback follow the central finance transaction order. Sources: [economy](../../design/economy-banking.md), [lifespan](../../design/game-lifespan.md), [current state](../../CURRENT_STATE.md).

**ACTIVE/TRIAL planning value / OPEN employee economics:** A [Drive design-archive pass-off](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1SlwomB3riLH9qm0maT-4Ixhg9YSbLwUD88abW3NvBwM/edit) explicitly chose **$10 per in-game month** as the interim Production Specialist payroll planning value, conditional on later lifespan and reward review. It is therefore a real provisional handoff, **not a final salary or implemented charge**. Local Tasks 8/16/18 also screened $50/$75/$100 monthly salary, $0/$100/$200 hire fees, zero/one hire cycle, first payroll at the boundary crossed by hiring versus a later boundary, and course tuition $0/$75/$150, one course cycle, and $0/$25 monthly raises. They are alternative shadow arms. Course availability, completion timing, salary effective boundary, first bill, stacking, dismissal/rehire, and unpaid-payroll recovery are all **OPEN**. The finance system has a typed payroll hook, but only rent produces live bills. Do not promote a simulated combination to a rule. Sources: [employee design](../../design/employees-courses.md), local [Task 8 archive paragraph](../../archive/drive-task-queue-2026-10-06.txt), and [balance evidence](../../findings/current-balance-evidence.md). ## Small dependency set before wage/reward approval

1. **Finance transaction policy:** Decide payroll producer, due order relative to actual sales settlement and rent, first payday, typed arrears/credit handling, and recovery from insufficient cash. Use the existing exact-cent ledger and no-negative-cash rule. No loan package is needed to run a no-loan employee comparison.
2. **Actual production and Store access:** Use current specialty roster, finite Features, printed Core matching, retained candidates, primitive purchase/play fees, and one-cycle later purchases. For any proposed new Store node, settle that node's price, play fee, and legal draw access before claiming employee value from it. The entire future 97-node/era catalog need not be finished.
3. **Publisher cash timing:** Keep live Ironclad/SideStreet receipts and contract cycles in native routes. Crown/Neon advances and Promotion remain modeled candidates; include a separately labeled sensitivity only if a decision actually depends on them. Do not make staff viability rely on unimplemented payouts.
4. **Representative release income:** Reuse current monthly ledger and a legal short/long first-game route, including overlapping releases and a strong later Review route if captured. Fanbase, active trait effects, loans, and full era progression can be held absent or shown as separate sensitivities; they are not prerequisite systems for the first wage screen.

Hire/tuition must be checked against spendable cash when committed. Any proposed cycle cost advances every released title and can cross rent/payroll and sales boundaries. Wages should be revisited **after the surrounding economy is polished enough to supply stable ledger rules and representative traces**, while employee benefit usefulness can be studied now. There is no final salary recommendation in this note.

## Production Specialist benefit to test — not approved

**Candidate: bundled synergy planning.** After training, once per project, the employee may attach one *valid changed* Design/Alpha priority adjustment to the next successfully committed production hand that contains a printed-Core Pass/Feature match. Show the proposed new priority and the qualifying visible cards before commit. If the hand succeeds, change priority within that same atomic action and consume the employee's project use; the hand retains its ordinary one-cycle cost and ordinary productive-cycle redraw refill. The adjustment adds **no separate cycle**, no Core points, no free card, no pool reroll, and no second redraw. Failed/canceled/unaffordable hands change neither priority nor the employee use. The training hand could activate the permanent capability immediately, but redemption would require a later hand; exact same-hand eligibility is **OPEN**.

This turns the older standalone free-priority waiver into a decision tied to an actual synergy hand and avoids its lost-refill artifact. It is only valuable when the player would have changed priority and can arrange a legal matching hand; that is the hypothesis to test, not a claimed outcome. Compare it with no employee, an ordinary one-cycle priority change, the old standalone zero-cycle waiver, and the old curated redraw as a weak reference. Record how often the player declines, qualifies, uses it, saves a cycle, preserves or changes redraw choices, and improves later hand options/Review/cash. If meaningful use remains rare, revise the benefit before assigning wages. Do not add passive Core score; reserve any Generalist exception for a separate ruling.

## Matched comparison design

Use reproducible current-source legal routes with the same specialty, owned cards, market inputs, seeds, and initial cash ledger for each no-hire/hire/course arm. Capture a **short first game** (target 12–14 development cycles), a **long first game** (16–20 or a legal 22-cycle stress route), and early versus post-Game-1 hiring. Include Game 2/3 with two concurrent releases where legal. Start with fixed-action pairs to isolate fee/payroll/calendar effects, then allow an adaptive visible-information policy to actually exploit the benefit. Once a choice changes draws or timing, report diverged paths and common-calendar comparisons; do not pretend downstream hands are identical.

For each arm, report successful/failed actions; qualifying and redeemed challenges; useful changed priority, redraw availability, chosen hands, Scope/Core/Review; development and total cycles; Store purchases and actual draw/play access; Ironclad/SideStreet timing and receipts; each release's age-month earned net and separately settled cash; monthly rent and candidate payroll/tuition bills; cash low point, first unaffordable action, arrears/credit, cash at launch and matched calendar boundaries; and whether the next game or Store purchase remains affordable. Attribute a boundary windfall to its actual sales/publisher source, never to a nearby employee action. Separate genuine $5,500 no-trait and $5,700 current-preview ledgers. A no-hire baseline must be playable; hypothetical payroll arms that would fail are censored at the first shortfall, not carried as negative-cash routes. Report per-route distributions and concrete decision traces, not only end-cash averages.
