Their assignment counter starts at zero and advances once for each committed action while they remain assigned, including actions when they are in the first two positions. First-two staff offer a half-step every action regardless of that counter. A rank change while the employee remains assigned neither resets nor pauses the counter; if they move to a later position, its current parity determines whether they offer a half-step on that action. Removing Research duty resets their counter; reassigning starts at zero. Save and Continue preserve the counter without advancing it.

For clarity, a newly assigned third employee has counts 1 (no step), 2 (step), 3 (no step), 4 (step). If that employee moves into the first two before count 3, they offer a step on count 3 because of their rank; if they move back to third for count 4, they offer a step because count 4 is even. A committed action still counts when that employee's offered step cannot be paid, is stopped for bill protection or has no queued target. A rejected action counts nothing. No skipped step is banked.
