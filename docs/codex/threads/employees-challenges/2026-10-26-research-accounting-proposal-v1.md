## Pricing choices and recommendation

**Recommend integer half-units of Research progress.** A Feature requiring `A` normal Research actions has `2A` units. One manual Research action gives up to two units **to the current FIFO head only**; unused manual capacity does not jump to another Feature. Each eligible staff member gives one unit. Staff units then walk the entries that were already queued when the action began. This preserves current one-action/no-staff behavior and the selected staff-only FIFO carry. A manual action on a half-finished one-cycle head pays for and completes only the remaining half.

For a full-action installment with nominal unpaid amount `N` cents, split its two units into `N1 = floor(N/2)` and `N2 = N - N1`. At the start of each committed Research action, freeze that Feature's earned familiarity percentage `d`. Quote unit 1 as `floor(N1 × (100-d)/100)` cents. Quote unit 2 as `floor(N × (100-d)/100) - floor(N1 × (100-d)/100)` cents. If the halves occur on different actions, recompute **only the still-unpaid unit** at its later action's discount; never adjust the first payment. This cumulative rounding keeps a full two-unit action exactly equal to today's full-installment quote at an unchanged discount. The nominal `N` for a multi-action Feature remains today's per-action `FeatureResearch.payment` allocation, including its final-action odd-cent remainder. Zero-priced units may still grant their actual progress.

Independent rounding of two discounted halves can make the combined price one cent cheaper than today's full action, so the user selected parity with the cent assigned to the second half. Delaying all payment until the second half contradicts the pay-at-each-half-step ruling. **Selected Resourceful boundary:** the single up-to-$100 opportunity applies after familiarity to the unit that completes the Feature, and is capped at that unit's remaining charge. It is claimed only on successful completion. Resolve completions in manual-then-staff FIFO order, so the first qualifying completion consumes the available opportunity before a later same-action Feature; that exact same-action ordering remains a recommendation. If the final unit costs less than $100, the unused difference is not refunded against an earlier paid unit, as the user confirmed.

## One quote, one action, protected bills

**Recommend a single immutable Research quote and one productive finance action.** Snapshot opening cash, calendar cycle, current queue and discounts, Resourceful claim window, employee assignment/order/cadence, finance ledger revision and the next cycle's earned/settled sales. Plan the mandatory manual progress and its Store charge first. If that normal productive-cycle plan fails, reject without any Research progress or cadence change.

Treat its projected payment **for each bill** as the baseline. Walk eligible staff units in order. For each prefix, quote its incremental Store charge and replan the **combined direct Store debit** from the same opening snapshot. Include a unit only if opening cash can pay the combined direct debit, the normal cycle plan accepts it, and no bill's projected payment falls below its manual-only amount. Stop at the first failed unit; do not try a later employee or a cheaper later Feature. Newly earned/settled sales may service bills under existing rules, but cannot finance the Store debit. Do not reserve bills that are not yet due. If the mandatory-only plan itself leaves a new bill partially unpaid under current rules, optional staff still cannot reduce what that plan would pay it.

Commit the selected prefix atomically: one calendar cycle, one market/sales/finance settlement and one `store` direct debit equal to the sum of the manual and committed staff charges. Itemize each Feature/unit payment under that action; grant ownership and child unlock on its final unit, with card supply only for future projects. A rejected or stale quote changes no queue, cash, finance, Resourceful claim, employee cadence, sales or save state. Recomputing the proposal at confirmation must produce the same complete quote, not just the same total cents.

## Staff order, cadence and controls — candidates

**Selected opt-in Research duty in the Employees roster.** Keep specialty buffs separate. Hiring alone cannot trigger additional Store spending; existing and newly hired staff start unassigned until the player opts them in. A zero-cycle toggle and named Store preview are recommended UI details. Automatic contribution by every hire was considered and not selected.

**Selected:** resolve assigned staff and their eligible paid half-steps in hire order, including the first two full-rate positions. Leaving Research duty resets that employee's cadence; Studio Continue preserves duty and cadence while assigned. The counter starts at zero, advances on every successfully committed active Research action while assigned, and offers a later-ranked half-step on counts 2, 4, 6, and so on; it keeps advancing through first-two rank and rank changes. First-two staff offer a half-step on every action. Count even if that employee's offered unit is unaffordable or the pre-queued FIFO is exhausted; an unused opportunity is not banked. Rejected Research actions count nothing. [Cadence phase ruling](2026-10-26-research-cadence-phase-decision-v1.md). **Recommend, still OPEN:** use stable employee ID for any otherwise ambiguous hire-order tie; omit a separate reorder control; make the duty toggle a zero-cycle roster event.

**Recommend a Store preview before commitment whenever a staff unit can apply.** Keep the existing Research button, but show mandatory cost, possible staff cost, actual affordable/bill-safe staff prefix, affected queued Features, one calendar cycle and any due-bill impact. A confirmation from that preview commits the exact fresh quote; stale state returns to an updated preview. With no contributing staff, the existing one-click path and quote can remain. The Employees roster shows assignment and third-plus cadence. This is a candidate interface contract, not an approved layout.

## Save and acceptance handoff boundary

**Recommend a versioned Research receipt model**, preserving old `research1` entries and finance actions on Continue. New receipts identify the one composite action and each queue entry's unit index, nominal cents, familiarity percent, Resourceful saving, paid cents and completion order. The sum of itemized charges must equal that action's single Store direct debit; a unit cannot be paid twice or credited after the Feature is owned. A new employee roster event records duty and cadence, with old saves mapped to unassigned/zero. Strict validation must accept historical full-action receipts and new multi-entry same-cycle receipts without rewriting old finance history. This is a schema requirement, not a proposed code patch.

A later source-pinned implementation handoff should verify: exact no-staff parity; one- and two-staff same-action carry; a partly researched head; third-plus cadence over two actions and Continue; order changes by assignment; queue exhaustion and zero-cost units; both changed/unchanged familiarity and odd cents; Resourceful on first versus later completion; manual rejection; first unaffordable or bill-unsafe unit; due rent/payroll/Bank/education and settled sales; no negative cash; one calendar cycle; exact ledger/itemization, duplicate/stale rejection, prior-save preservation and separate-process cold Continue.
