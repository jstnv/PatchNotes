## Production findings

Eight primary base pairs per reward, compared with their no-hire controls:

| Arm | Uses across horizon | Median Review Δ G1 / G2 / G3 | Median cash Δ at cycle 36 | Complete horizon |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary paid priority change | 23 | +0.5 / 0 / 0 | +$552.935 | 7/8 |
| Standalone zero-cycle waiver | 24 | +0.5 / 0 / 0 | +$365.035 | 8/8 |
| Bundled planning | 24 | +0.5 / 0 / 0 | +$365.035 | 8/8 |
| Curated same-class redraw | 28 | 0 / 0 / 0 | $0 | 8/8 |

Cash medians can be half a cent because they summarize pairs; every playable ledger value is an integer cent. Bundle Game-2 deltas ranged −0.5 to +1.3, Game-3 −0.9 to +0.3. At cycle 36, bundle cash changes ranged −$1,619.51 to +$2,502.91. A changed priority is consequential and can hurt a route.

The standalone and bundle controls are identical when the same adjustment is made at the same qualifying hand. That isolates the waiver's calendar/refill tradeoff; it does not demonstrate a superior bundled UI. Paid adjustments add one cycle and its normal refill, potentially settling sales and charging rent. The zero-cycle arms preserve the hand refill but receive no separate priority-action refill. Twelve first-use pairs shared the visible hand and selected cards with their controls, had identical immediate Core gains, advanced one cycle and preserved normal refill. Later paths adapt after priority-dependent replacement draws diverge.

Curated used its separately sampled same-class second option, consumed a normal redraw and improved immediate printed utility in only 6/28 uses. Later Review/cash medians remained zero. This supports deferring curated redraw as the first reward.

Two-employee sensitivity, synergy seed 1104: early bundled route used only three adjustments across the horizon, the same count/outcomes as one employee; long bundled route used six and changed first Review from 5.5 to 6.1, with identical later Reviews 7.7/7.1. This is saturation evidence, not a roster cap or a reason to launch two employees together.

Optional activation compares expected first-replacement utility from public owned/remaining definitions and visible Core/Scope deficits, with no peek at a replacement. Run-start optional arms used seven adjustments and declined six. The **post-Game-1 ownership** arms used three and declined six; no Game-1 challenge or reward was credited. Game-2 Review deltas were 0/−0.5/0/0; Game-3 0/0/0/+1.8. A heuristic utility improvement does not guarantee a stronger release. Native typed-bill probes: $505 cash pays $500 rent and $5 of the first $10 wage; one/two/three employees leave $5/$15/$25 payroll outstanding. Each case loses five credit points once for Payroll, not once per employee. A genuine receipt of arrears plus $1.23 clears exact balances and leaves $1.23; original due dates, partial-payment history and late status survive. Recovery at the opening boundary produces no second old-period penalty. Native Payroll age tiers remain prototype 5/10/15; rent and Payroll category penalties add independently. No extra late fee or credit forgiveness was modeled. Bill byte reconstruction passed; durable save/restart remains a separate missing runtime.

Following-month raises use the **expense month**. Zero-cycle course at cycle 14 belongs to month 8: same-month raise due 16, following-month due 18. Productive completion at cycle 15 or 16 also belongs to month 8: 16 versus 18. Zero-cycle enrollment **after** cycle 16 belongs to month 9: 18 versus 20. Already-issued bills retain their original amounts.

For one employee, a $25 raise adds $300/year; two add $600/year. It changes a $10 wage to $35. This recurring cost quickly exceeds $75 tuition, so tuition affordability alone does not settle course balance. Across the 28 early/long control and run-start reward financial paths, post-Contract hiring with $10 wage/$100 fee, $75 tuition/$25 raise retained 26/28 when enrolled immediately versus 27/28 after Game 2. The course benefit itself remains unspecified and supplies no invented income in these overlays.

An extra course cycle is productive calendar time. Base early/synergy 1104 at common cycle 24: immediate $75 course/next-month raise leaves $2,224.55 with either zero or one course cycle, but the one-cycle path has reached original action cycle 23 instead of 24. Delayed enrollment leaves $2,399.55 and original action cycle 24. Ending cash at later calendars cannot establish a course advantage.
