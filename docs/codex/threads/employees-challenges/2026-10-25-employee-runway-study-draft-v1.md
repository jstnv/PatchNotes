## Study question

Can a real player fund one employee after Game 2 and a second employee later, while receiving a useful trained benefit, without losing the next release to monthly payroll? Separately, what would it take to fund both immediately after Game 2? Do not collapse those into one pass/fail claim.

## Bounded matched arms

Pin the then-current source, exact initial state/RNG and legal action policy. For the same short and longer first-game configurations and at least the two existing seeds, compare: no hire; QA after Game 2; Production after Game 2; both immediately after Game 2; QA after Game 2 plus Production after Game 4; QA after Game 2 plus Production after Game 5. Use the existing rich quality prefix and at least one ordinary shorter prefix. The after-Game-4/5 arms are legal **player timing choices**, not changes to hire eligibility. Do not grant training, Bugs, redraws, cash or Features by fixture.

The fixed quality policy never used Production's trained bundled-planning benefit. Add a bounded, publicly observable policy that can choose that benefit when legally useful, and log each trigger/use. Track QA mixed and pure challenge progress from actual committed Beta hands, including both raise dates and any Swap use. Keep each arm's actions and deviations explicit so a better score is attributable to employee play rather than an unrelated policy change. Preserve failed commands and incomplete challenge progress.

## Required report and decision boundary

Report, by arm and seed: hire/course spend; first and raised paydays; monthly earned net sales versus settled cash and any lag; rent, payroll, unpaid bills and other due obligations; Feature/Research costs; lowest cash at every action and month end; release cycles, Review, employee-benefit uses and overlapping release income; first failed legal action; six-release continuation and Game 7 Begin affordability. Keep Contract/Borrowing proceeds in separate columns and exclude them from recurring support. Check exact replay and Studio restore for any arm proposed as evidence.

An **immediate-dual pass** requires the actual dual arm to fund both payroll streams, both earned QA raises where reached, legal play and the next game. A **staged-hire pass** requires actual later hiring, prospective bills and sustained continuation; a salary-only overlay or cash-rich Studio snapshot cannot pass. A staged pass would support a proposal to revisit the existing exposure gate, **not** automatically change it or enable normal Employees. If neither passes, provide the measured monthly shortfall and compare only narrow candidate progression/recurring-income levers under a new design review before changing accepted sales or wage values.
