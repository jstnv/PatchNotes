## Question, hypotheses and cases

Can a first 1977 Action release's **visible four-Core shortfalls against its recorded standards** direct paid first-era Store research that raises Games 2–4 toward 8.0 without losing the fourth historical release or a played January 1980 project? H1: one targeted card improves the next game's relevant Core and Review over the no-Store control. H2: a second card broadens the pool and improves quality further, but its additional Research cycle and cost may delay or remove a release. Nulls include no changed actual play/Core, Review regression, unaffordable quote/bills, fewer releases, no January WIP, illegal command or timeout. No 8.0 minimum, direct Core grant or Review clamp.
