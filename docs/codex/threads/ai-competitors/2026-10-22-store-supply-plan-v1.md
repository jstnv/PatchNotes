# Rival Store/Core supply experiment plan — 2026-10-22

**Status: pre-run OPEN design experiment.** Owner: AI Competitors. Canonical gameplay and trial numbers are unchanged. Source is current `main` at `0132c4dab398a2a94dbeae3caa68a36505fbfa4f` plus its preserved dirty working tree; the named private variant will freeze actual bytes and manifest before use. Prior frozen-target results do not establish a Store effect.

## Question and hypothesis

Test whether Wayfinder Works can legally research one current-live later Feature at its first completed Studio boundary, draw and play it during the next project, and improve real resolved Core/Review while staying solvent. The preselected node is **16-Bit Music** (`16_bit_music`, Store base 170000 cents, named parent `8_bit_music`, initial Wayfinder extra). A purchase that never reaches a hand is a supply failure. A changed Review with no improved Core is not proof of the intended mechanism.

## Cases, start and policies

Use fresh, source-matched founding recipes, active two-rival world, Action/War player route and seeds **4417 and 4418**. Each seed's A/B/C arms start with equal founding/player/rival state digest and typed RNG. Every arm advances through ordinary legal player commands and the same active market transaction. Do not carry a Studio envelope, RNG state or checkpoint across different source revisions. Preserve the rival's initial resources, archived games and legal Design/Alpha/Beta hands.

- **A, current policy:** Wayfinder's current Primitive synergy selector, immediate next project after its first release.
- **B, Feature-first control:** same first-release prefix, then switch Wayfinder to the existing Feature-first Design/Alpha selector, immediate next project. Beta policy, phase exits and hand count remain current.
- **C, legal Store case:** same first-release prefix, then at Wayfinder's first Studio boundary use current `get_feature_research_quote(16_bit_music)`; if admitted under the experimental screen, issue actual `admit_research`, re-quote, then actual paid `research`. After ownership, use the same Feature-first policy as B and begin the next project. No grants, skipped months, injected scores, modified hand counts or market coefficient changes.

For C, require `unlocked`, `can_admit`, and spendable cash covering quote down payment plus remaining due plus already known outstanding dues. Label the experimental held next-project allowance **B = 0 cents**; this is a feasibility lower bound, not a production AI liquidity rule. Record the exact quote, finance/outstanding snapshot and acceptance of each action. Abort that C arm as **INCOMPLETE/blocked** when the screen or a legal command fails, rather than changing the seed, node or budget. Follow ordinary settlement after Research. A→C estimates the net effect including the extra month/cash; A→B isolates selector loss at the same boundary. B→C is not a pure inventory effect because Studio has no neutral productive wait action.

## Measurements and fences

Record per-arm initial and first-release-boundary state/RNG, Store quote/down/due, known dues and cash before/after Research, ownership, Feature candidate appearance/draw/play, actual four Core categories, Scope, Bugs, Review, launch cycle, Fans, arrears, active market exposures, policy decisions, wall/actual CPU time, and checkpoint bytes. Compare second Wayfinder release to its first and to aligned A/B arms; report player exposures separately. Require legal world advancement, source identity, exact command/result/state tape and representative fresh-process replay. Check Studio-only Continue on one successful C route. Retain all rejected, incomplete and timed-out outputs.

Bounds fixed before running: 180 player commands, six player releases, 48 committed cycles, two hands per rival phase, 12 zero-cycle policy steps, 240 seconds each import/world process. Stop on source drift, initial or first-boundary mismatch, illegal Store command, finance failure, replay divergence, or missing required Store event. Do not extend seeds, bounds or node opportunistically. A legal but unaffordable target is a finding about this proposed first acquisition.

## Disposition rule

Only a reproducible positive, solvent **net** A→C result with owned-and-played Feature evidence can justify a dynamic Store policy handoff. Otherwise record the failure mode and next targeted question. This study does not approve `B`, a shopping rank, entrant rules, market coefficients, leaderboard, population CPU budget or final challenge balance.
