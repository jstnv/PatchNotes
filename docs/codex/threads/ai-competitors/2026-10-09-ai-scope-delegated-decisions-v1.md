## First-slice acceptance shape

The AI must be able to explain a selected productive action from the actual candidates, current project state, owned supply and spendable cash. An invalid or unaffordable choice cannot consume a card, RNG draw, Feature research installment, cash or calendar turn. Each actual hand's outcome must match player resolution for identical inputs, including zero-cycle redraw and phase-entry behavior. The Store planner is governed separately by [delegated shopping decisions](2026-10-09-feature-shopping-delegated-decisions-v1.md); its deterministic planning ceiling is **only** an acquisition aid, not permission to award Core or a Review.

The old primitive competitor ledger and Beta intel cards need a source-matched migration/interaction review. Retaining frozen old results does not imply maintaining two live rival systems in new games. The market's effect on Reviews remains a separate **OPEN** design topic.
