## Interpretation and remaining work

The eight-cycle hard reserve protects the declared cash plan without anticipating income, but at these two starts it blocks the cheapest qualifying Feature and therefore all other candidates ranked after it under the held 1/1 plan. The one-cycle guard preserves the locked quote-plus-Research-dues check and an experimental `B` at the ownership boundary; it permits acquisitions that may need later revenue or legal early exit. Neither is an approved AI policy. Review the [candidate choice and alternatives](2026-10-11-g2-reserve-window-review-decision-v1.md) before implementation. Exact `B`, hands, due snapshot for debt/employees, whether any already-known receivable can be counted, horizon/replanning, F5 adapter, F6 prerequisite progression and CPU bounds remain **OPEN**. Maintained canonical verifiers and latest-source native parity were not run because gameplay was not changed. The only runtime warnings in completed probe logs were local root-certificate-store warnings; v1 parse failures are explicitly retained.
