# G4 benefit-aware current-hand choice — predeclared study, 2026-10-18

**Status: DESIGN experiment.** The user delegated the next AI Competitors design review. This is a private test of current-project hand choice, conditional on the unapproved v5 exact quote prototype. It does not approve the quote interface, numerical AI objective, final hand counts, a CPU limit or canonical gameplay.

## Question and hypothesis

The rejected 2026-10-11 candidate ranked printed Core/Scope with an arbitrary cash floor; a cheapest-quote probe also reduced first Reviews. Test whether a deterministic benefit rank using the *current Genre's active Review targets* and visible Scope can improve first releases while retaining cash and an actual second-project opportunity. H1: among preflight-available current four-card hands, a no-variance Review proxy after printed Core and Scope additions will produce stronger Reviews than the token-first reference and cheapest-affordable negative control. H2: any first-project gain may be offset by cash and later-project opportunity; measure both rather than assuming benefit.

## Measures and stopping conditions

Measure accepted/rejected commands, chosen Design/Alpha tokens and quotes, Review and four Core scores, Scope/required Scope, known/remaining Bugs where observable, release cycle, direct hand costs, quoted next cash, minimum transaction/launch cash, arrears, Fans, actual earned and settled sales, and `policy_and_observation_us`. Compare first-release Review and cash against both controls by recipe; compare second release only within the declared Action/4417 continuation. A first-release projection or quote is not earned sales. Use state/RNG-compatible fresh-process replay of representative arms; preserve each arm's own tape.

Stop and retain the failure if initial state/RNG differ within a matched group, the policy mutates state/RNG on observation, a selected quote disagrees with actual legal receipt, a command is rejected, or a fresh-process replay differs. Use failure reduction when an action/invariant failure is reproducible. A tentative policy worth *further* design review must legally complete all declared routes, avoid arrears and materially improve first Reviews over both controls without a severe second-project cash/Review regression. No success threshold here approves balance. If results are mixed, reject a universal rule and keep the objective OPEN.
