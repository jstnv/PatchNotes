# One Action rival: paid Store gap progression — finding, 2026-10-27

**Status: bounded DESIGN evidence; no adoption handoff or TODO entry.** A legal, paid after-Game-1 Action Store policy raised Game 2 Reviews in both declared seeds when it bought the largest visible Graphics/Technology gap card. Buying both tested cards yielded one **8.4** historical title (seed4417 Game 2, cycle39), but seed4423's Game 2 fell from the one-card 7.7 to 7.1. Compared with the no-Store 36-Scope control, one or two Research cycles also shifted some fourth releases out of pre-1980 history and into genuinely active January 1980 Beta work. The two-card policy is a useful **OPEN candidate**, not a general rival rule or an 8+ guarantee.

## Actual paid policy and retained failure

At the next Studio after Game 1, the policy reads that title's committed `review.cores` and `review.standards`, compares nonnegative Graphics and Technology deficits, and breaks a tie toward Graphics. Seed4417's Technology gap8 beats Graphics gap5, so it first chooses **Scripted AI** (Technology5/Scope2, owned Enemies parent). Seed4423's Graphics gap7 beats Technology gap0, so it first chooses **Animated Sprites** (Graphics5/Scope2, owned Sprites parent). The two-card arm then buys the other child. Admission required the fresh legal quote, cash for down + remaining cost + a $1,000 known-bill buffer, and a legal paid Research action. The live frozen-source quotes were Scripted AI **$950 down + $855 due = $1,805** and Animated Sprites **$850 down + $765 due = $1,615**; the second paid Research cycle is real. Both cards were actually drawn and played in Game 2 whenever owned, and again in each later project whose Feature phase completed. These are old-source Store definitions; current queued Store graph/upgrades must be reconciled before any adoption.

The v8 `full_pool` 4417 control completed, but both v8 Store arms stopped at c17 after the same Game 1. [Their logs](sims/one-action-store_one_gap-4417-20261027-v8/prelude.log) show a private GDScript `Array`→`Array[StringName]` assignment error before any Store command. They are retained as **probe failures**, not Store affordability or Review outcomes. V9 changed only that typed-array construction and ran all three arms in both seeds under one frozen source.

## Recommendation and remaining OPEN work

Keep the Store-gap purchase concept **OPEN/TRIAL**. The one-card policy improved Game 2 in both seeds, but reduced Game 3 in one and delayed a fourth title in the other. The two-card policy proved a **legally paid 8.4** is possible in 1978 for one Action rival, while the other seed worsened Game 2 compared with one card and neither produced a fourth frozen title. The next design question is an adaptive **one-versus-two purchase gate** based on first-game quality, calendar slack and a whole-project quote, then the typed c72→January 1980 projection to verify those Beta projects become timely live releases. Rebaseline against current Store parent/upgrade and era rules before implementation. Historical standard generation, eight-Genre distribution, market impact, CPU population and old-save migration remain OPEN. No canonical gameplay, TODO, `.codex-godot-temp`, player save, commit or push was changed.
