## What the policy actually did

The candidate maximum was `max(2,ceil((Design+Alpha)/3))` Beta hands. The first two remained ordinary. An extra hand needed visible Search+Debug, positive possible Bug reduction under a public hidden-Bug range proxy, and normal payable Beta command execution. The final v6 enumerated at most 35 four-card hands, picked the best eligible hand by that proxy, and took a legal exit if none existed. No redraw, cash grant, Review formula or card rule changed. Five **distinct accepted** extra Beta hands in v6 all selected Search+Debug, across four projects; one project took two extras. The [audit script](2026-10-24-beta-qa-opportunity-audit-v1.py) deduplicates repeated post-cycle policy observations by accepted sequence. One extra hand was on a project that already had zero actual remaining Bugs at launch; the proxy tests possibility, not hidden truth.

v1/v2/v4 were complete legal **no-op** worlds (same 10 titles, Reviews and player outcome as control). Diagnostics showed v2 had five of ten exit deals with visible Search+Debug but zero payable preflight results. The actual cause is the world boundary: `RivalWorld` asks the policy again after a productive rival action, while that RunState has advanced to `world.cycle+1` and the world clock has not yet committed. `RunState._plan_productive_cycle` asks for a further cycle; the market provider correctly rejects it until the next world turn. v4's exact Beta finance kind/project ID still returned empty at this boundary. v6 carries the visible choice across the boundary and performs the exact Beta preflight when clocks align. Its direct world-clock access is a **diagnostic private adapter**, not an approved implementation design.

## Recommendation and remaining design work

Do not queue v6 or change the current Beta rule. First test whether the existing two Beta hands can select Search+Debug more effectively **without adding months**. After that, consider an extra hand only when a visible Bug-risk signal and expected Review gain justify the publication delay, with a release-density/player-sales guard. An unknown hidden Bug count cannot be treated as guaranteed positive. Keep exact Bug-risk estimator, proportional coefficient, hand-count ledger for redraw/host-playtest, affordability timing, population CPU/save limits and adoption criteria **OPEN**. This finding does not approve a balance target or claim release readiness.
