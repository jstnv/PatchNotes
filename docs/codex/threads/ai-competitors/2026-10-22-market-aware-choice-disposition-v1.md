# Rival market-aware choice disposition — 2026-10-22

## Decision

**Do not adopt the frozen-target-only hand selector as a standalone challenge improvement.** The [bounded finding](2026-10-22-market-aware-choice-finding-v1.md) matched four legal control/candidate worlds from identical state and RNG. The candidate received the actual frozen target array, including five raised Arcade projects in each Action route and an uneven benchmark lift after a legal player 7.8, yet made no different four-card choice across 526 rival plays per arm. Review, release cadence, player market pressure and final gameplay state matched. The candidate remains an **OPEN private proposal**, not queued canonical gameplay.

The existing two-rival Primitive synergy selector and active market effects remain **ACTIVE/TRIAL**. The persistent rival foundation, real legal hands, frozen market project basis and sales saturation remain as implemented. No number, hand count, market coefficient, CPU/population budget or balance threshold is approved here.

## Alternatives retained

| Option | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Adopt frozen-target scoring now | Defer. It aligns the proxy with Review input, but produced no challenge gain in the declared worlds. Revisit when the candidate action space or cost screen changes. |
| Add hands or tune market lifts immediately | Defer. Those alter cost, cadence or player pressure without identifying the rival quality bottleneck. Keep values **OPEN**. |
| Test actual Store/Core supply before a full dynamic shopping AI | **Recommended next design probe.** It isolates whether better available Features can lift real Core/Review under legal finances, then informs the exact G2 planner and richer-pool hand policy. |
| Choose later Genre/Theme or add entrants now | Separate **OPEN** slices. These can widen market exposure but will not establish that current rivals make strong games. |

The next Store probe must account for a current policy coupling: `RivalTrial.uses_synergy_trial()` stops using the adopted selector after a rival owns any non-Primitive Feature. A simple buy/no-buy comparison would mix inventory gain with selector loss. The [next probe brief](2026-10-22-store-supply-probe-brief-v1.md) specifies a same-boundary Feature-first control and legal admission/Research boundary, while keeping the extra Research cycle as an explicit unresolved comparison limit. It is design preparation only; no new simulator version or implementation handoff has been approved by this record.
