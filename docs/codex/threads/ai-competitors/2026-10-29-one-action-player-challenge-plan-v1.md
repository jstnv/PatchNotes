# One Action player challenge comparison — pre-run plan, 2026-10-29

**Question.** Does the legally published Action/Fantasy rival game observed in the v28 world change an actual new player's first game's Review and launch sales, compared with a matched world where that rival has not published?

**Hypothesis.** The published game raises the frozen Action Core targets at the player's Begin and depresses same Genre/Theme sales while its market fact is live. The player action sequence and player starting state/RNG must otherwise match. This is a one-rival mechanics test, not evidence of a challenging eight-rival opening.

**Cases and legal start.** Both arms restore the same source-matched c2 player/world checkpoint from v28's 1977 legal prelude, seed 5607, `full_pool`, midpoint first standard, trial $25,000 rival founding receipt, ordinary player opening cash/owned Features, and three frozen historical games. The publication arm continues the rival's legal full-Scope Beta project to c6 release and idles through c8. The control arm idles the same active rival from c2 through c8. The player advances idle calendar in both arms until c8. Require exact equality of encoded player Studio checkpoint and RNG before Begin. Require publication arm one live c6 Action/Fantasy fact and control zero. Reject otherwise.

**Player policy.** Begin the same Action/Fantasy title in both arms, then use identical legal command decisions based only on the player's own project hand (one fixed deterministic finite policy, no purchase or artificial funds). Consume legally playable Features and normal phase exits; use ordinary QA in Beta and launch when legal. Record command tapes or per-step choices and require equality up to any market-caused difference. No change to canonical gameplay. A project that cannot reach launch within the bound is retained as incomplete, not called a pass.

**Measures.** Source/candidate/fixture identities; pre-Begin player state and RNG; each world's Market fact/targets/factor; project frozen market details, hand/Core/Scope/Bugs, release Review, units and revenue, cycle of first sale; any rejected commands, default/arrears and rival releases. Check the historical records stay frozen. Compare both arms at equal world cycles. Run a fresh process from a saved c8 Studio/world checkpoint if the first run succeeds.

**Bounds and stop.** At most 12 rival cycles from c2 to c8, four rival zero-cycle actions per world boundary, 120 player commands and 32 post-Begin player cycles per arm, 240 seconds per process. Stop on source mismatch, unequal c8 player states/RNG, illegal command, missing publication fact, failure to capture/restore or non-matching replay. Retain failures and do not substitute seeds or budgets. Trial +72 rival sales/index clock and market coefficients are inherited from private v28; they are not approved integrated rules.
