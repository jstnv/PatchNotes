## Current-rule reconciliation

- A Beta Marketing card adds **Marketing Output to the project**, which enters launch Awareness and later sales calculations. It does **not** deposit money in the studio bank account. A `playtest_rival_games` Insider card currently pays $1,000 cash per selected card as Beta operating income. The actual Beta hand command and RunState settle that income, calendar and bills; an AI forecast cannot spend unearned future sales.
- Every Beta hand is one productive cycle. Two cycles complete a month; ordinary rent is $500, with actual trait, payroll, Bank and education obligations determined by the studio's finance ledger. A free legal phase exit or launch does not add a productive cycle. The rival world gives at most one productive action per rival per player cycle, so extra Beta work delays publication and can reduce title density.
- The present trial policy exits Beta after two hands when exit is legal. Design and Alpha have a separate **LOCKED** visible-Feature exhaustion rule. No Beta duration proposal changes that requirement or grants cash, Core, Review, or hidden-Bug knowledge.

## Proposed decision structure

1. At each legal Beta decision, evaluate the *next actual visible hand* and the project opportunity left. Continue only if that hand has a defensible expected QA or Marketing benefit relative to launching now and the lost release cycle. Do not use the project's hidden remaining Bugs as AI input. Insider cash may count only when it is guaranteed by the selected legal hand; speculative future draws and sales do not count.
2. Treat spendable cash as a **ceiling on optional Beta time**, not a reason to keep producing. Forecast the next hand and the full contemplated Beta horizon from current cash, already-due bills and the studio's known future dues at real month boundaries. Preserve a separately stated post-launch operating/project reserve. Do not initiate a Bank loan to make an optional Beta hand affordable; a studio with an existing loan still counts its scheduled payments. Exact reserve and horizon are **OPEN**.
3. Reassess after each committed hand and monthly settlement. If another hand fails the ordinary legal cash/calendar preflight or the prudent no-new-loan runway screen, take the legal Beta exit. If exit is unavailable, report the ordinary blocked state rather than conjuring a release. Cash can allow a longer Beta for a wealthy studio, but cannot make renewable Marketing an unlimited farming loop. A finite marginal-value or opportunity stopping rule is required; its formula is **OPEN**.
4. Preserve a distinct project ledger: actual Design/Alpha Feature spend and hands; actual Beta hands; Marketing Output; Insider cash receipts; known due payments; and cash left at launch. Displaying a `Marketing budget` as bank cash would misstate the live rule. The earlier full-project runway ledger remains the proposed source for estimates, with its unknown prices and draw duration explicitly incomplete.

## Existing-behavior screen, not a new candidate run

Post-hoc read-only extraction from the frozen two-hand control [seed 4417](sims/rival-proportional-beta-control-4417-20261024-v1/world-report.json) and [seed 4418](sims/rival-two-hand-qa-4418-control-20261025-v1/world-report.json) found 20 actual `request_exit` decisions, ten in each legal Action/Fantasy world. The **choice observations** showed cash from **$1,800.00 to $12,025.90**. At $500 ordinary rent, even the lowest cash would nominally cover three further month-end rents before other spending, so a `cash ≥ next rent` screen alone would distinguish none of these exits. **Correction after reading the harness:** a `rival_choices.cycle` is the world cycle *before* the rival step, while `rival_progress.cycle` is captured *after* each completed world step. A same-numbered progress row is therefore an earlier boundary, not the exit decision. Its empty bill list or hidden remaining-Bug value cannot establish the exact exit-time state. An exit-aligned typed finance/QA snapshot is still required. This is a descriptive screen over prior runs, not a predeclared causal experiment or proof that a particular extra hand improves Review.

The prior [raw proportional study](2026-10-24-proportional-beta-finding-v1.md) changed two to 3–4 Beta hands, yielding eight rather than ten rival titles by player cycle 56 and mean Review 4.96 versus 5.00 in one matched world. The later [QA opportunity candidate](2026-10-24-beta-qa-opportunity-finding-v1.md) produced nine versus ten titles and mean Review 5.06 versus 5.00. The [same-duration mixed-QA study](2026-10-25-two-hand-qa-finding-v1.md) did not improve mean Review in either of two seeds. These findings reject those exact candidate selectors and show that cash feasibility, QA selection and lost publication time must be assessed together. They do not reject the user's funded Beta direction.
