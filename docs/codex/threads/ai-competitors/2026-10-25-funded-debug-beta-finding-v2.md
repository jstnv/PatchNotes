## Recommendation and limits

Do **not** hand off this $1,000/known-Bug extension for canonical adoption. It helped each directly affected game, but triggered only two of twenty projects, yielded no new 6.0+ rival release, and player sales moved in opposite directions. Cash should remain a constraint on a valuable QA opportunity, not the objective for staying in Beta. Next design work should estimate the marginal value of a visible Search+Debug hand (including unknown hidden Bugs as a range, without reading them) against one-cycle publication delay and known dues. Then test a larger population and debt/low-cash legal fixtures. Existing Bank loans, active education, payroll, arrears, broader Genre/Theme choices, long campaigns, and a measured CPU limit are **NOT RUN** here. Passing simulations do not approve balance.
