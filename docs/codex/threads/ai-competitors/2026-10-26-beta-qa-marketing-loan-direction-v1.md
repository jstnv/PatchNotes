# Rival Beta work and borrowing — decision, 2026-10-26

**Status: USER-SELECTED qualitative direction. Exact AI policy, numerical values, implementation and balance remain OPEN.** This record supersedes the no-new-loan restriction in the [earlier cash-funded Beta direction](2026-10-25-cash-funded-beta-direction-v1.md) and its restatement in the [full-Scope/QA priority decision](2026-10-25-full-scope-qa-marketing-decision-v1.md). The hard [full-Scope release gate](2026-10-26-rival-full-scope-hard-release-gate-decision-v1.md) and [whole-Feature-supply phase gate](2026-10-26-rival-complete-feature-exhaustion-phase-gate-decision-v1.md) remain in force.

## Decision

1. Once a rival legally reaches Beta, it should actively work there when another **useful legal hand** can be funded. Rank **QA before Marketing**. Search may reveal Hidden Bugs and resolves before Debug; Debug removes Known Bugs. The AI must use its visible state and a bounded estimate of undiscovered Bugs, never inspect hidden Bugs or future draws. A legal mixed hand may deliver QA and Marketing together.
2. If no useful QA opportunity is available, use a worthwhile Marketing opportunity when funded. Marketing Output increases launch Awareness; it is not cash in the Studio account and cannot pay the next bill. Do not leave Beta merely because a fixed two- or three-hand count was reached. Reassess after every hand and monthly settlement. Cash runway supports more Beta work; it is not permission for an infinite renewable-Marketing loop. The finite marginal-benefit/publication stop remains **OPEN**.
3. **Consider borrowing proactively** before dropping a valuable Beta opportunity for lack of cash. Use only an exact quote the current Bank actually accepts, count *net* proceeds after arrears recovery, and project every scheduled payment alongside rent, payroll and education dues. Borrowing should support a credible remaining project and operating runway; it must not invent cash, bypass Bank eligibility or force a loan for every Studio. The quote amount, term, debt-service reserve, minimum expected QA/Marketing benefit and loan-versus-launch comparison are **OPEN**. An accepted loan is an ordinary Studio liability, not a Review bonus.
4. If neither cash nor a legal, serviceable quote supports a worthwhile hand, use the ordinary legal Beta exit and launch only after the full-Scope gate. If legal exit is blocked, retain the actual blocked state and report it. A live project may not be rescued by synthetic Scope, Bugs, income or Review.

## Bank reconciliation and opening implication

The current [Bank2 rules](../../design/economy-banking.md) require two completed positive settled-sales months and sufficient capacity for an ordinary quote; existing Bank installments reduce that capacity. A one-time three-rent recovery facility can be available to a Studio whose cash is below three rents, including one with existing Bank debt. It carries ordinary interest and repayment. Qualified proceeds may first service overdue bills. The old hard overdue-bill and single-active-loan restrictions in historical Bank text were superseded by Bank2. The [live quote code](../../../../patch-notes/scripts/finance/bank_loan.gd) and finance ledger validate exact quotes; the AI should not predict eligibility from cash alone.

Frozen pre-1980 archive games do not earn post-start sales or Fans, so an opening rival's archived titles alone cannot create the two live settled-sales months for ordinary borrowing. Initial Beta lending will often be unavailable unless a genuinely eligible recovery quote exists. Historical opening resources and any legal pre-start finance provenance must be explicit; do not treat the archive as live sales.

## Why this needs a candidate test

The [single synthetic all-Features case](2026-10-26-all-features-cash2000-beta3-one-case-finding-v1.md) gained a third Beta hand and rose from 5.5 to 6.8 Review, with Bugs falling from 11 to 7, but its middle hand delivered Marketing and no Bug reduction. It had no loan and is not a cohort result. Earlier longer-Beta studies also lost publication count in some worlds. Neither result approves a fixed hand allowance, borrowing amount, score lift or launch delay.

Next private simulator study: freeze a real rival-world control; predeclare seeds, starting cash/sales/loan eligibility, policy, horizon and command/release/time limits. Compare current Beta policy, QA-first funded extension, and the same extension with **only legally available** Bank quotes. Include a no-sales opening Studio, an ordinary eligible Studio, a cash-poor recovery-eligible Studio, and an already indebted Studio. Record exact quote/acceptance and net proceeds, QA/Marketing actions, Bugs, Review, cycle of release, release count, cash, arrears and debt service, player market exposure, Studio-only Continue and fresh-process replay. Verify matched legal starting state and RNG or regenerate legal prefixes when rules differ. Retain rejected and incomplete cases. CPU/save cost and the finite stop remain **OPEN**. Canonical gameplay and TODO adoption require a separate source-pinned handoff.
