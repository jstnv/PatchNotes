## Interpretation and next design gate

Current `RivalWorld.initialize` requires every rival cycle equal the player's; `RivalWorldCheckpoint.snapshot` and restore enforce it again. `RunState` derives January 1980 from cycle zero, and `StudioCheckpoint` validates `studio_finance.last_cycle == completed_run_cycles` along with dated transaction, research, employee and Fanbase provenance. Thus a legal prefix cannot simply be placed in a fresh January world by changing one cycle or project field. The result is a **current representation blocker**, not an assertion that a revised system cannot support pre-start projects.

Recommended next prototype: a **versioned pre-start tape and one-time opening-state normalization** for new saves, with the tape and opening ledger used as validation inputs. Generate legal past hands in a private scratch session; explicitly map pre-start dates and settled costs into a typed January opening snapshot; validate project/hand/RNG against the tape, then persist the resulting world exactly once. The normal live calendar and shared scoring should remain unchanged after creation. This requires a named provenance schema and tests for finance, project target freeze, first post-start legal hand, cold Continue, corruption rejection and old saves. A global per-studio cycle offset is an alternative, but touches every calendar, market, release-age and checkpoint comparison. Both are DESIGN/OPEN, and neither is implemented or proven here. Do not skip pre-start cycles for free or assign Scope/Review directly.
