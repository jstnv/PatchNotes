# 2026-10-09 decision: frozen pre-1980 incumbent history

**Status: LOCKED qualitative G1a choice; OPEN cohort content and numbers.** The user selected the recommended **frozen historical archive** for incumbents' pre-1980 games. Those titles are visible prior market history, not active post-start sales titles. The designated incumbent opening game remains its first actual release on or after January 1980, made through legal Design, Alpha and Beta work under the [revised opening rule](2026-10-09-opening-rule-revision-decision-v1.md).

At the January 1980 run start, each incumbent receives one explicit authored snapshot of cash, Fans, completed Feature ownership, familiarity and any other applicable persistent state. Do not replay pre-run launch proceeds, monthly sales, Fan changes, research payments or news. Historical titles earn no new sales or Fans after run start and cannot trigger following-month high-Review news. The snapshot explains the head start without fabricating transactions on the shared calendar.

Pre-1980 titles need a distinct typed historical date/release representation. Current ordinary release/checkpoint state is tied to nonnegative post-1980 run cycles; do not encode a past title as cycle zero or reinterpret it as a new January 1980 launch. Its displayed Review and other authored history must be marked as historical, not as a post-start result from unplayed hands. All new post-start rival releases still require actual legal hands and ordinary frozen Review scoring.
