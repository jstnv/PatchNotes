# 2026-10-09 delegated design decisions: rival world, calendar and news

**Status:** **LOCKED first-slice qualitative contract; OPEN thresholds, entrant schedule, exact AI choices and CPU limits.** The user delegated subsequent small design choices to the recommendations while requiring other choices to be logged. ## Selected choices and alternatives

| ID | Selected recommendation | Other choice logged | Reason and limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 — Shared progress | For each **successfully committed player productive cycle**, give every active rival one opportunity to commit one legal productive action on that same central cycle. Failed player actions, browsing, redraws, loading and zero-cycle transitions give no rival productive turn. | Progress rivals per frame, per menu visit, per player project, or by replaying missed time on load. | The calendar already measures productive time; this avoids time being minted by navigation or Continue. A blocked rival can record an idle turn without gaining Core, cash or time. |
| W2 — Zero-cycle work | A rival may make the bounded legal setup/choice/phase-transition/launch steps needed around its one productive action. Those steps do not grant a second productive action or a second calendar tick. If a bounded decision cannot finish, retain a safe state and idle/defer rather than inventing a result. | Allow unlimited redraw/transition loops or replace a hand with direct Core/Review credit. | Actual hands and finite supply remain authoritative. The bound and CPU budget are **OPEN** until profiling. |
| W3 — Money and Fans | Each studio owns exact-cent finance, earned-versus-settled sales, due costs, release-age accounting and a separate Fan ledger. Existing applicable player formulas are the baseline source; current **ACTIVE/TRIAL** coefficients do not become final competitor balance approvals. No launch forecast, release registration or same-cycle future settlement funds the current action. | Give rivals monthly grants, instant launch cash, projected Fans or a shared player ledger. | A persistent studio's later choices must follow its actual resources. When it cannot legally pay, it takes another legal action or idles; recovery/retirement policy stays **OPEN**. |
| W4 — News | Stamp a stable event identity from an **actual frozen qualifying rival release**. It becomes due at the start of the following calendar month and is delivered once at the first safe player-facing point after that boundary, before further player input. If several events are due together, show one ordered digest by release month, studio ID and release ID. | Notify at launch, interrupt an unresolved hand, or open one modal per simultaneous release. | This preserves the user's following-month timing and avoids duplicate/spam presentation. The Review threshold and final UI copy remain **OPEN**. Pre-1980 historical titles do not generate fake January-1980 next-month alerts. |
| W5 — Save and replay | Persist rival identities, resources, active hands/phase, per-studio random state, release/finance/Fan accounting and pending/delivered news with a versioned committed-Studio checkpoint. Continue restores state rather than recomputing past rival turns. An unsaved player mid-project rollback also rolls back unsaved rival progress and news. | Recreate rivals from a run seed or infer delivered news from visible history on load. | Exact-once settlement and alert delivery require saved identities. Older bytes and frozen player releases need explicit compatibility handling; no silent migration. |
| W6 — Later entrants | Found entrants only on a committed calendar boundary, with stable IDs and the player-valid Studio creation structure available on that date: permanent specialty, legal traits, starting cash and Store access. They begin without an incumbent's historical head start. | Spawn entrants on menu open/reload or copy an incumbent inventory. | The event is reproducible and respects the structural lock. Entry trigger, cadence, selection, population, survival and later-era adjustments remain **OPEN**. |
| W7 — Market record | Register every legal rival release in the shared world history with frozen Review and launch context. | Add an immediate competitive Review or sales modifier from the record. | The market's effect on Reviews remains a **separate OPEN design topic**; this record selects no modifier. |

## Transaction and UI boundary

One central calendar commit must account for all studios without double-charging rent, double-settling a title or allowing a rival to spend income that has not settled. The exact source-level ordering of rival actions, release registration, monthly earning and settlement is **OPEN** until a focused implementation design pins the existing transaction path. A newly launched rival title cannot earn its first Month 1 units on the same productive boundary that creates it. Invalid rival choices preserve cash, Feature supply, random state and world history just as rejected player actions do.

The news record stores release ID, studio ID, frozen one-decimal Review, release month, due month and delivery state. A qualifying release during a month is not displayed immediately, including when the player is in Design, Alpha or Beta. At the next month boundary, the first safe player-facing presentation can appear without changing the unresolved hand. A saved delivered ID is never replayed on Continue; replay after an unsaved rollback follows the restored timeline.
