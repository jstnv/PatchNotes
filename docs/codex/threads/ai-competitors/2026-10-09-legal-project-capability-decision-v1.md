For Feature shopping, represent the four-Core mix that the rival's [owned, project-eligible Feature pool](2026-10-09-owned-feature-capability-decision-v1.md) **could contribute within one legal project**. Respect ordinary Design/Alpha phase eligibility, finite Feature use and legal hand/card limits. Do not sum every owned card's printed Core as if the entire catalog could be committed in one game. This is potential capability, not the Core already produced by a past release.
