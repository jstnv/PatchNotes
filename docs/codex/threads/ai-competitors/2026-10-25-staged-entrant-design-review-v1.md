# Staged rival entrants — design review, 2026-10-25

**DESIGN recommendation; entry trigger, dates, resources, cap and Theme policy remain OPEN. No gameplay approval.** The [delegated world rule W6](2026-10-09-rival-world-delegated-decisions-v1.md) already fixes the important boundary: found later Studios only on a committed calendar boundary, with stable IDs and player-valid creation resources/traits for that date, and no incumbent historical head start. This review narrows the next private experiment after the [publication study](2026-10-25-publication-capacity-qa-finding-v1.md) and [same-calendar Beta choice study](2026-10-25-same-calendar-beta-choice-finding-v1.md).

## Why entry is the next lever

The current two-incumbent world publishes ten rival titles by player cycle 56 in both studied seeds. The same-calendar Beta diagnostic improves one title past 6.0 and rival sales while leaving player units unchanged. A different four-active-Studio opening creates twenty rival titles per seed and lowers player units by 56/52 under current Beta, but starts with a different population, weak extra Studios, and all-Fantasy Themes. That is a capacity signal, not approval of four day-one incumbents or a population cap. A delayed entrant can add actual titles without rewriting Reviews or market coefficients; its first launch, survival and calendar effect must be measured, not presumed.

## Alternatives and recommendation

| Candidate | Upside | Cost / reason not selected now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Authored calendar admission pilot (recommended for private study)**: a fixed, disclosed boundary table admits at most one legally funded new Studio per entry event, subject to a measured save/CPU cap. | Deterministic, easy to replay, independent of poor rival Reviews, allows a clean two-incumbent prefix before entry. | Exact dates, resources, max count and survival still need evidence; a fixed event may overfill a market in a long campaign. |
| Recent-title-gap admission | Responds to actual market pressure. | Risks automatically spawning more Studios after bad AI releases, conflating population with quality; window, cap and feedback stability are unmeasured. |
| Four active Studios on day one | Produces twenty titles by cycle 56 in the prior private study. | The tested cohort is weak, uses provisional pre-1980 snapshots and is already rejected as an opening package. |

Recommend the authored calendar pilot **only as an experiment**, not an approved entrant policy. First keep the current two-incumbent prefix exact through the proposed event. At that boundary, found one new Studio with an explicit player-valid date-specific recipe, no prior active sales/Fans/history, no free Feature grants or simulated launch; include its actual finance and hands from then onward. Add a second entrant only in a separate version so its marginal CPU/save and market effects are identifiable. Entrants should choose a distinct permanent specialty; a separate Theme-choice candidate should use public recent-market counts rather than automatically cloning Fantasy. Do not combine that Theme candidate with the first entry test, because the prior worlds showed Theme variety is absent and its independent effect is unmeasured.

## Current-source feasibility and required prototype boundaries

`RivalWorld.initialize` accepts only Studios already at the same run cycle with no releases. It has no committed mid-world admission API. `RivalWorld.advance_committed_player_cycle` requires every registered rival to be synchronized before advancement. `RunFactory.create_fresh_session` creates at cycle zero, while the finance ledger and Studio clock must agree. `RivalWorldCheckpoint` can serialize up to eight Studios but currently **rejects an empty historical archive** for an ordinary rival. A legitimate later entrant has no incumbent pre-1980 archive, so a typed founding-cycle/entrant distinction and validated empty archive must be added in the private prototype and tested through save/restore. Thus merely adding a new session to `world.studios` midcycle, editing its clock, fabricating old titles or inserting a simulated release would evade W6 and the save contract. The private pilot needs a single atomic boundary operation: validate the date, recipe, stable ID, funding source and no-duplicate cap; create a new legal RunState **and finance ledger** at that cycle; bind its market provider; register the next-cycle policy; and roll back all world/RNG/finance changes if any step fails. Cold Continue must restore the entrant exactly once. The permanent max-eight codec guard is a technical upper bound, **not** an approved population target.

Do not use a pre-created dormant Studio as a fake entrant: it would either accrue rent for months before founding or dodge monthly liabilities while already existing. Do not give entrant Studios incumbent archive titles or early Store inventory. Funded/contingent/blocked runway categories from the [finance shadow study](2026-10-24-qa-frequency-runway-finding-v1.md) should be reported, but a conservative full-pool cash forecast cannot reject a legal project automatically; 16/60 observed completed later projects failed that proposed screen.

Predeclare two independent legal fresh seeds beyond the repeatedly used 4417/4418 pair if the runner is extended, plus the old pair for comparability. Compare the two-incumbent control and one-entrant candidate at equal initial state/RNG and identical player/rival prefix **through the entry boundary**, then label post-entry worlds as different-population comparisons. Measure actual completed entrant releases, 6.0+/7.0+ Reviews, Genre/Theme mix, player launch snapshots and earned units, entrant cash/arrears, calendar/transaction count, right-censored titles, checkpoint bytes and representative process CPU/wall. Include a rejected/duplicate admission and failure reduction, exact fresh replay, Studio-only Continue before and after entry, and a one-event cap. Stop on import/source drift, illegal founding, clock divergence, save mismatch, incomplete player route or timeout. Retain every failed case and do not change event timing or funding after seeing results.

Acceptance for **further design review**, not implementation, requires legal one-time entry and saved replay, at least one real entrant launch by the common boundary, no lost incumbent titles at that boundary, and evidence that the added titles change the player's actual market snapshots or units without unacceptable stalls. Exact event date, Studio recipe, lower funding bound, population limit, Theme policy and CPU target remain OPEN until a pre-run plan pins trial values and a source-matched study tests them.
