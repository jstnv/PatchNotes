# AI Competitors market pressure shadow study — predeclared plan, 2026-10-17

## Question, authority and hypothesis

The [user direction](2026-10-17-market-pressure-direction-v1.md) asks real AI releases to raise future four-Core Review standards and Genre/Theme crowding to affect sales. The [neutral index](2026-10-17-market-index-decision-v1.md) is queued separately. This study tests **CANDIDATE/TRIAL arithmetic only**, before any canonical market gameplay or checkpoint change.

**Hypothesis:** A deterministic month-start snapshot over actual released titles can (a) keep current weak rivals from raising Review standards, while allowing a genuinely strong same-Genre other-studio title to raise specific Core targets, and (b) distinguish Genre-only, Theme-only and pair crowding in sales without same-month action-order effects. The current two-rival route will produce nonzero sales pressure but no Review lift. These are structural properties, not a claim that the numerical penalty is desirable.

## Cases and legal starts

1. **Existing-rule control:** Fresh `market-shadow-control-v1` private variant copied from current canonical source without edits. Run `2026-10-17-foundation-smoke-v1.json` (one fresh Action player Studio, no traits, seed `4417`, finite 2/2/2 Action/Fantasy legal route, 50-command/one-release bound), then the maintained `verify_rival_trial_activation.gd` focused route (fresh Action player Studio seed `4417`, two January trial rivals, 20 player productive idle cycles, current rival legal policies, two releases per rival expected). This is the exact current-rule outcome.
2. **Shadow trial:** Derive `market-shadow-trial-v1` from that control version. Add only a pure read-only candidate market helper and verifier instrumentation in its private `project/`. Run the same simulator config and 20-cycle focused verifier with fresh output/profile and test port. Candidate calculations must not mutate world, games, RNG, sales or checkpoint. The source revisions differ, so compare declared legal starts/outputs directly; do not feed them to the same-source paired reporter.
3. **Synthetic edge table inside the trial verifier:** Explicit release rows at a month-start boundary exercise no competitor, Genre-only, Theme-only, pair, self-exclusion, own-studio sales cannibalization, old release expiry, current-month exclusion, other-studio Review gate, missing Core, and cap/floor. Synthetic rows are formula unit controls, not legal Studios or population evidence.

## Fixed trial policies and arithmetic

- World cycle0 is the first January half. A snapshot for even cycle `s` contains actual releases with `release_cycle < s`; release at any half of a calendar month first appears at the following month's even boundary. Presence window: `1 <= s - release_cycle <= 12` cycles (six calendar months). Pre-1980 archive excluded. Per-title pressure excludes itself; same-owner other titles count for sales.
- Review target trial: for each project Genre category, start with the current `PrimitiveReviewCalculator.GENRE_TARGETS` target. From the latest snapshot take other-studio, same-Genre titles with frozen Review `>= 70` tenths and valid four-Core output. Candidate category lift is `min(8, max(0, floor((max_qualifying_core - base_target)/2)))`; no qualifier gives zero. This category maximum is independent per Core. The selected threshold/divisor/cap are **OPEN TRIAL inputs**, not approvals.
- Sales factor trial: classify other recent releases into mutually exclusive Genre-only `g`, Theme-only `t` and same-pair `p` buckets. `factor_bp = max(7000, 10000 - 600*g - 300*t - 1200*p)`. Pair weight exceeds either one-field weight and their sum. Shadow month units are `floor(current-rule month units * factor_bp / 10000)`; ledger and settlements are not changed. Factor is held for the two earning cycles of one release-age month in the proposal; this shadow verifier can show snapshot/factor at each observed boundary but cannot prove saved age-month reuse.
- No rival AI policy, Genre, Theme or hand allowance changes in this study. Historical rival archive never supplies a market row. No year-based replacement or fabricated entrant.

## Measurements and comparison validity

Record branch, HEAD, dirty source/variant identities, source delta, Godot hash, exact commands, process exits/errors, legal cycles/release IDs/Review/Genre/Theme/earned units, player control outcome, candidate snapshot IDs/eligible rows, bucket counts, factors, four target vectors, shadow units and assertion results. Require control and trial to start from the same fresh recipe/seed and legal policy under their own compatible source; equal seed by itself is insufficient. The shadow helper may change only observation/debug output, so actual control/trial releases, cash, Fans, checkpoint digest and RNG must match. A mismatch invalidates causal comparison and stops adoption. Never reuse an old Studio envelope across mechanics identities; use Studio-only Continue within each version.

## Limits and stopping

One seed (`4417`), one player policy, two current trial rivals, 20 world cycles, at most 50 player control commands, no additional releases beyond those reached legally, 120 seconds per process. Run into unique `sims/market-shadow-*` folders and preserve rejected/incomplete/timed-out cases. Stop on variant provenance drift, import/runtime failure, incomplete legal route, missing release, source mutation or failed invariant. Keep the failed output and diagnose without silently changing seed, budget or policy. If successful, replay the independent player control in a fresh process, and repeat the focused rival verifier in a fresh profile to check reproducibility. This limited shadow study cannot validate active-game integration, checkpoint migration, UI explanations, rival strength, final coefficients or player-facing balance.
