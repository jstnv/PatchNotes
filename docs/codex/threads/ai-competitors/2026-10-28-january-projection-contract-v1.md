# January 1980 rival projection contract — design proposal, 2026-10-28

**Status: OPEN architecture proposal; no gameplay adoption.** This narrows the next private simulator version after the [c72 capture finding](2026-10-28-one-action-january-projection-finding-v1.md). The selected legal-opening direction calls for a 1977–79 simulated career, frozen old games, and an active project at new-save creation. Its exact money, first-standard numeric transform, eight-Genre tapes and population cost remain OPEN.

## Approach choice

The source has two calendar domains: the player's January 1980 local cycle 0, and the prelude rival's January 1980 local cycle 72 (`START_YEAR=1977`, 24 cycles/year in the private source). **Recommend an explicit per-Studio calendar offset of +72** in a typed world admission record. A world advance from player c0 to c1 advances the prelude rival from c72 to c73; physical year/month must agree at every boundary. This preserves the rival's existing finance, Store purchases, Fanbase, project age, release and RNG history for validation. It requires world policy, market and checkpoint code to use physical date or mapped world cycle where they currently assume equal run-cycle integers.

The alternative is to create a fresh c0 Studio and rewrite each old finance/Fanbase/Store/project ledger into c0 history. That would touch more causal records and create a new way for save validation to disagree with the actual 1977 tape. Keep that alternative documented, but use the offset first in a private variant. The v15 probe's attempted direct insertion is correctly rejected and should remain a negative control.

## Required data and invariants for the private candidate

1. A certified prelude capsule names the originating variant/source identity, seed, immutable command tape digest, Studio ID, c72 checkpoint digest, development/hand digest, original RNG digest, calendar offset, and first-release standard source. Its archive contains every pre-1980 title exactly once with Studio/release IDs, title, release date, Genre, Theme, Review, four Core and its original sale/Fan close totals. The source release must be found in this archive and must recompute the saved target and effective boundary.
2. Admission checks both calendars resolve to January 1980, the active project ID is absent from the archive, and the active project retains its already-frozen market target. Asset, debt, Fans, owned Features, cash and RNG match the c72 certified checkpoint. The player starts c0 under existing rules. Projection does not replay or charge a January cycle.
3. At admission, pre-1980 titles become closed historical entries. Their exact last prelude totals remain available to checkpoint verification, but they have no post-start sales, Fan transactions, active Market saturation or news. New 1980 releases alone enter the live world release index. A later leaderboard may display archive titles by explicit design, without making them live economic records.
4. On each advance, compare mapped run-cycle and physical month/year before and after both player and rival actions. A cold Studio-only Continue reconstructs the offset, closure and archive from versioned saved data, rejects altered archive/core/source/offset/old-sales totals, and reaches the same next state and RNG as an uninterrupted run. Old saves without a prelude remain on their existing path.
5. Do not suppress old sale records by mutating their totals or deleting them from the owner ledger. `StudioFanbase.validate` reconstructs the complete old path, and current world restore requires every owner title in live history. A new closure field and separate historical/live indexes need explicit schemas and validators; an offset alone is insufficient.

## Private validation gate

Use the existing 5607 Beta and 5613 Design c72 capsules as first cases, with the same actions, policy and source. Before a new run, freeze commands/limits and record any rule delta relative to v15. Accept the private candidate only if both produce a c0 world, the first player month turn leaves old sale/Fan totals fixed, no old title appears in live saturation/news, a legal rival continuation can finish its active project, and cold Continue plus tamper tests pass. Retain rejected cases. Then regenerate from a current canonical source with legal starting prefixes before quality/CPU claims. No January adoption handoff or TODO queue until this gate and representative eight-Genre population evidence exist.
