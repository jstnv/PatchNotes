# Next AI competitor probe: legal Store/Core supply (brief v1, 2026-10-22)

**Status: OPEN design study, not a gameplay or numerical ruling.** The [market-aware hand finding](2026-10-22-market-aware-choice-finding-v1.md) found no changed rival choices despite real raised targets. The next useful question is whether a legal, funded later Feature actually reaches a rival's hands and improves Core/Review enough to justify a dynamic Store planner. This brief fixes the causal comparison before any private variant or run; an execution plan must pin current source and legal starts when the probe begins.

## Proposed bounded comparison

Start from one source-matched completed first-release Studio boundary with the current two-rival world, actual cash, Fans, owned Primitive Features, ledger and RNG. Select **one already unlocked, unowned current-live Store node** before running; Recorded Sounds is a candidate only if its parent and exact current quote are legal in that prefix. Do not grant ownership, cash or an artificial Review. Requote admission and perform ordinary `admit_research` and paid `research` commands. Screen spendable cash against the current conditional completion quote plus known dues through Research, with a separately labeled experimental held next-project allowance `B`; fail closed or defer when that screen cannot be computed or funded. The exact due arithmetic and `B` are **OPEN**, so this probe cannot authorize a production shopping policy.

Compare three fresh, legal branches from that same prefix:

1. **A — current immediate play:** no purchase, current Primitive synergy selector.
2. **B — same-boundary Feature-first:** no purchase and the current Feature-first hand policy; isolates loss of the synergy selector at the original Studio boundary.
3. **C — legal purchase, Feature-first:** admission/Research and ordinary next project, which reflects the real current policy fallback after non-Primitive ownership. Compare A→C for the **net** player-facing outcome, A→B for selector loss, and B→C only with the added Research cycle, cash and dues explicitly labeled. A fixed-input, non-applied hand-supply analysis may help explain actual draws; it is not a fourth legal world or an outcome claim.

The current Studio command surface has no neutral productive `wait` or `idle` action. A post-launch `campaign` is legal but changes cost, Awareness and sales, so it is not a clean time-matched no-purchase control. Do not invent a skipped month or declare B→C a pure inventory effect. The execution plan must decide whether the net A→C question is useful despite this limit or design another **legal** time comparator before running.

Keep rival hand counts, phase exits, market coefficients and player policy fixed. Regenerate legal prefixes if canonical source has changed; never load a checkpoint across revisions. Record chosen Store quote/due snapshot, each accepted/rejected action, Research cycle and cash, Feature ownership versus actual appearance/draw/play, four resolved Core categories, Scope, Bugs, Review, release cycle, arrears, Fans, rival/player market exposure, source/RNG identity, exact fresh replay, and per-world CPU/checkpoint bytes. Bound commands, releases, cycles and process time before the first run, retain incomplete/failed arms, and stop on a legality, source or replay fence. A Feature that is bought but never drawn is a supply failure, not evidence of quality improvement.

Only a positive, solvent, reproducible **net Store effect**, with actual ownership and draw/play evidence, would justify the next source-reconciled dynamic G2/F5 shopping and richer-pool synergy/cost study. This three-arm design cannot by itself identify a pure inventory effect against a perfectly time-matched no-purchase world. It would **not** approve a numerical `B`, later Feature ranking, entrant rules or final challenge tuning. The old G2 private candidates predate the active market and are historical evidence, not compatible checkpoints.
