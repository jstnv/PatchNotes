## Implemented structural slice

The canonical [RivalWorld](../../../../patch-notes/scripts/market/rival_world.gd) owns an ordered registry of independent scene-free `CampaignSession`s, one world cycle and frozen records for actual live rival releases. A caller supplies studios and explicit bounded decision policies. On each verified committed player productive cycle, each rival receives at most one productive action through the existing campaign command resolver; legal zero-cycle setup/phase/launch work is bounded, and an idle or blocked studio advances its own `RunState` calendar once. Rival `RunState`s retain their own cash, finance, Fans, Feature state, release sales and RNG. World records copy the ordinary release's Review; they do not roll or modify Reviews. Launch occurs after that cycle's settlement, so a newly registered title has no same-boundary sales.

The layer does not select a roster, grant historical resources, choose a Store Feature, define AI hand counts or choose a high-Review news threshold. It is not attached to `Gameplay` or the player checkpoint. Normal new games and Continue therefore do not yet run this world. This is **PARTIAL implementation of the foundation**, not completion of item 22. No market-to-Review effect was added.
