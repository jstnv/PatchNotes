## Why the full projection remains blocked

`RivalWorld.initialize` presently requires each rival's run cycle to equal the player's cycle and its released-game list to be empty. `RivalWorldCheckpoint` independently requires the same cycle across owner runs and world, then requires every owner release in live world history. Both c72-to-c0 attempts are rejected as expected. Merely changing the world cycle would not make the Studio, project, finance, Fanbase, release ages and RNG a valid January 1980 state.

The existing `RunState._plan_sales_cycle` iterates all owned released games. One normal c73 action gives old titles +64/+239 earned units in the two cases; zero immediate Fan delta is only a one-cycle observation, not proof of Fan closure. The Fanbase validator reconstructs sales across the prior calendar. Removing old records or zeroing their totals would break that provenance. A typed historical close and epoch mapping must preserve the pre-1980 asset/finance/Fan result while preventing old titles from entering post-start sales, Fans, active saturation and news. The world codec must validate the archived owner releases separately from live releases. The market target source must survive projection and cold restore with its first release as evidence, while an already-started project retains its frozen target.

**Recommended next private revision:** build a versioned c72→c0 projection contract before another score study. First create a certified capsule with exact pre-1980 archive, active Studio/development, historical-source and closure provenance; then map calendars while preserving state/RNG and only put post-start releases in the live market. Test c0 world admission, January month turn, at least one continued project release, Studio-only cold Continue, tampered archive/source rejection and no old-title sales/Fans/saturation. Rebaseline against current canonical Store and rosters before widening to eight Genres and measuring CPU. Keep candidate capital and midpoint numeric transform **OPEN**.
