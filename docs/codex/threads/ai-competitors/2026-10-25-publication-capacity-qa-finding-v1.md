# Rival publication capacity and QA quality — paired private finding, 2026-10-25

**COMPLETE bounded DESIGN/TRIAL study; no adoption.** Four active publishers raised the number of actual rival titles and reduced player sales in two source-pinned exploratory worlds. The already rejected optional third Beta Search+Debug hand improved some individual Reviews, but delayed one title in one seed and increased player units in **both** four-studio worlds. The predeclared combined challenge screen failed. Keep the present two-incumbent cohort and two-hand Beta **ACTIVE/TRIAL**; entrant schedule, QA policy, title-density goal and CPU/save limits remain **OPEN**.

## Recommendation and remaining design work

Treat **publication capacity** and **per-title quality** as separate design levers. Preserve current two-hand Beta while studying a legal same-calendar QA value rule against Marketing/Insider alternatives; the prior simple selector also failed, so it must use better public evidence rather than a raw Search+Debug preference. Separately design a staged, funded entrant schedule that can create actual overlapping releases and broader Genre/**Theme** choices without four incumbent Studios on day one. Test any combined proposal on fresh seeds and low-cash/debt cases, with source-matched player market snapshots and representative long-run CPU/save measurements. The two-seed result rejects this particular combination and does not approve a four-Studio cohort, 20-title target, QA threshold, population cap or balance values. No adoption packet or shared TODO implementation entry is warranted.
