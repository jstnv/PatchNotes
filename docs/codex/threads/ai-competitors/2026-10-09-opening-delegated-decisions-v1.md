## Reconciliation with current systems

January 1980 is the shared origin, with two productive cycles per month. The stronger opening position comes from separate authored resources and owned Feature access, not a hidden Review modifier. A prior title may be visible in world-market history on day one; it does not qualify for following-month news as a newly released game. Incumbents still select legal Features and resolve actual Design, Alpha and Beta hands for O1. Their opening results are frozen at launch like player results.

The current Store, Genre, finance, Fanbase, Review and checkpoint rules may change during other work. The proof must use a source-matched snapshot when the implementation handoff begins. The current 14-development-cycle reference is a pacing target, **not** a legal ceiling. Because ordinary project advancement has no finite project-cycle cap, a finite search assumption cannot stand in for the player-maximum proof. The work may need a mathematical bound plus focused legal-route verification; an incomplete search cannot certify the guarantee.
