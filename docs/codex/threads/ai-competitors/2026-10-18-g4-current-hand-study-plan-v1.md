## Authority and question

**Private engineering/design study, not an AI policy or balance approval.** The selected finite, legal rival-project direction remains in force. The previous printed-Core/Scope rank and $2,500 post-spend floor were rejected as adoption rules: the same-count comparison had inconsistent Review changes and sometimes spent more, while longer routes produced low launch cash. This study asks whether an exact read-only quote for each visible four-card hand can be supplied before the next action without changing state or RNG, and what a cheapest-affordable negative-control choice does under current rules. It does **not** decide a value objective, reserve threshold, or final hand counts.

Hypotheses: (H1) observation-only quoting leaves a finite control route's starting/final state, RNG, actions, Reviews and finance unchanged; (H2) the cheapest-affordable choice may retain more cash but is unlikely to establish a Review challenge by itself. Either outcome is informative. A failed or incomplete route remains evidence.
