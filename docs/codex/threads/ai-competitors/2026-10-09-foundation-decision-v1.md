## Ruling

1. Multiple rival studios persist on the same January-1980-origin calendar as the player. Each has its own permanent Genre specialty, traits, cash, Fans, Feature ownership/research, active project and release history. Their resources and outcomes are not borrowed from the player or regenerated for each player project.
2. Rival projects use actual legal Design, Alpha and Beta hands under the shared project rules. Their chosen and committed work produces Core, Scope, Bugs, Reviews and releases; a final Review cannot be an unrelated launch roll. The AI's choices inside legal hands remain OPEN.
3. A small incumbent cohort already exists in January 1980 with previous market titles and stronger Feature pools. Each designated incumbent opening game must receive a Review **strictly higher than every legal player first-game Review**. The player first game cannot reach 10.0 under starting Feature access, cash and rent. These are design constraints to verify, not authority for an arbitrary Review bonus or a fixed rating roll.
4. Later studios use the existing studio-creation framework; project Genre may differ from permanent studio specialty.

## Boundaries
