## Question and hypothesis

Can an incumbent begin at January 1980 with completed, prerequisite-consistent owned Features, empty Research/familiarity, a visible typed pre-1980 archive and explicit starting cash/Fans, then make its first **live** release through ordinary legal Design/Alpha/Beta hands without replaying archival income or Fan gains? Hypothesis: a separate immutable archive and opening-snapshot field can coexist with the live RunState release/finance/Fanbase path. Injecting historical titles into ordinary `released_games` would violate source-matched age, settlement and Fan provenance; that is an excluded design, not an experimental arm.

The current trait-backed January start should be $5,850 and 40 Fans, but actual recorded values control. Cash/Fans remain exactly the legal trait-backed values rather than unjournaled arbitrary funding. Research queue and familiarity begin empty. No historical sale, Fan, Store payment or news is replayed into 1980.
- Candidate controls reuse the ordinary baseline recipes under the changed source. Compare their readable outcomes and command/result traces to current behavior; mechanics source hashes differ, so do not present them as a same-rules paired report. For positive candidate cases, use the same seeds/policy/command bound as controls but recognize the intentionally different starting owned pool/RNG consumption after dealing. No rating superiority or launch-cash causal effect is inferred from equal seeds alone.
- Negative candidate cases: reject an archive year of 1980; reject a historically owned `branching_nodes` without its `save_files` parent. Retain the invalid-initial results rather than adjusting the fixtures. Extra Features must be known, unowned by the ordinary starter roster and parent-complete. Old title IDs must not collide with live releases.

## Measurement, commands and stopping

Capture initial January cycle/calendar, cash/Fans, exact owned IDs, empty queue/familiarity, archive fields, initial finance actions and release count. After the first actual release, capture accepted Design/Alpha/Beta commands, live Core/Scope/Bugs/Review, cash/arrears, live release count, archive count/identity, finance/Fanbase and sales history, Studio checkpoint availability. A post-release Studio continuation must restore archive bytes, then legally make one more release without treating the archive as active. Replay one positive case and one continuation in fresh processes. Verify negative rejections and ordinary-control parity where supported.

Each positive or control case targets one **live** release and stops at 60 commands or 240 seconds per import/run. Continuation targets two total live releases with 60 further commands and the same timeout. Seeds and budgets are fixed; no favorable substitution. Stop after two positive seeds, two controls, two negative fixtures and one continuation, regardless of Review. Keep all failed/incomplete/timed-out cases. Use failure reduction for an unexpected recorded command rejection; invalid initial recipes have no command tape to reduce.

## Authority and limits

Locked: persistent separate rivals, actual legal hands, frozen visible pre-1980 history, first actual post-start release as the opening game, specialty as first project Genre, no hidden Review bonus, no universal strict Review ordering. The candidate Action fixture's names, title years/Reviews, trait loadout, $5,850/40-Fan start and three extra Features are **OPEN examples**, not a chosen incumbent cohort. The private archive schema must preserve old player checkpoint bytes in a later adoption-ready revision; this bounded prototype does not claim migration or a world scheduler. G2/G3 shopping, G4 policy/entrants, G5 news/performance and market effects on Reviews remain separate.
