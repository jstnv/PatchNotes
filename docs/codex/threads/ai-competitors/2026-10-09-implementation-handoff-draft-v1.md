## Proposed implementation work packages, after their gates

1. **Shared legal resolver and parity.** Separate data-only deal/resolve operations from animated phase controls, preserving current player behavior. Use the same seven-candidate/four-selected Design/Alpha and applicable Beta eligibility, redraw, finite Feature, Pass, priority, synergy, cost, Scope, Bugs, QA and Review rules for rivals. Test identical input/state parity and atomic rejection, not just a final rating sample.
2. **Persistent world and calendar.** Give each rival stable identity, legal Studio/trait/Store state, active project and per-studio RNG. On each committed player productive cycle, resolve at most one rival productive action and bounded zero-cycle work, then perform shared calendar earning/due processing exactly once. No per-frame, menu-open or load catch-up. Keep exact-cent separate ledgers and actual earned-unit Fan accounting; launch creates no spendable cash.
3. **Store planner.** At Studio before the next project, use the frozen finite Genre-independent Core planning envelope for owned, project-eligible Features. Select best overall equal-proportional-gap legal mix, balance a pre-target tie, target the largest positive gap with fixed Core tie order, and require target and conditional overall-fit improvement. Screen legal current admissions and require current spendable cash for the conditional completed quote plus known dues through Research; prefer earliest available node era and lowest current conditional completed quote, with stable further ties. Complete FIFO head before a new AI admission, then requote each payment. Defer when no candidate qualifies or search is incomplete.
4. **Opening history and entrants.** Add distinct typed frozen pre-1980 title records and coherent explicit incumbent starting assets without replaying historical payouts or allowing old titles to accrue post-start sales/Fans/news. Generate designated on/after-January-1980 opening games through actual legal work and pass G1a. Create entrants only from committed calendar events with legal contemporary Studio starts; exact event and population policy comes from G4.
5. **World record, news and save.** Store every actual rival release with frozen Review and market context, while keeping any market-to-Review effect out of this slice. Queue qualifying news for the following month and deliver an ordered digest once at the first safe player-facing point. Version checkpoint state for rivals, RNG, finance/Fans and pending/delivered event IDs; preserve old checkpoint bytes and frozen player results under an explicit migration rule. Retain old primitive snapshots for historical compatibility, not as fake persistent studios.
6. **Integration and acceptance.** Verify multi-title monthly earning/settlement, Fan boundaries, Store payments, redraw/hand legality, calendar zero-cycle exclusions, checkpoint Continue/rollback, deterministic replay, exactly-once news, old-save compatibility and representative long-run performance. Separate automated evidence from rendered/exported interaction and user playtesting. Record exact results and blockers in a dated implementation log before any completion claim.
