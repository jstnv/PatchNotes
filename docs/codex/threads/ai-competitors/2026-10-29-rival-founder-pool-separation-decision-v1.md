# Rival founder pool separate from the player — 2026-10-29

**USER-DIRECTED DESIGN correction; canonical implementation OPEN.** The user wants AI rivals to retain the original, larger genre-oriented Feature pool, approximately the old **27 printed Scope** Action start. The player's newer 12-Scope founder nerf must not automatically reduce rivals. This supersedes any design assumption that rivals should inherit the player's current founding roster simply because both use the same Studio factory. It does not authorize changing the player pool, granting all 27 Primitive Features, or assigning a final exact roster to all eight rival Genres.

## Current-source diagnosis

The [rival-only opening census](2026-10-29-opening-rival-review-census-finding-v1.md) observed first live Reviews 0.9–1.9 in two seeds. `RivalTrial.create_world` calls `RunFactory.create_fresh_session`; that calls `RunState.create_studio_with_traits`, whose default `StudioSpecialties.preview` is current founder v5: **12 printed Scope** per Genre. Trial Arcade/Action receives `music` (2 Scope), `dialogue` (1), `secrets` (1); Wayfinder/Adventure receives `sprites` (2), `8_bit_music` (1), `music` (2). `FeatureStoreCatalog.future_supply` removes retired `music` and `8_bit_music`, so each actually starts with **14 phase-eligible printed Scope** for a new project. The current trial Studio policy immediately Begins its next project and has no Store-purchase branch. `PrimitivePredevelopment.REQUIRED_SCOPE` remains **30**. Thus even ideal Feature use from the frozen owned supply cannot reach full project Scope; its maximum Scope multiplier is **14/30** before Core fit, Bugs and variance. `PrimitiveReviewCalculator` multiplies production rating by Scope completion. This proves a material supply constraint, but the exact Review-point contribution is **not isolated** without a matched pool-only counterfactual.

## Intended design interpretation

- Use a **rival-specific 1977 founder roster** at approximately the historical pre-nerf per-Genre scale and Core identity, while keeping player new Studios at 12 Scope. The historical v3 reference is Action **18 Features/27 Scope**, Adventure **17/22**, and the other six **21–25 Scope**; “27” here is **Scope for Action**, not 27 owned Primitive Feature IDs. The older synthetic ceiling granted all 27 Primitive IDs (a broader pool) and is not the selected solution.
- The existing selected January 1980 opening goal remains **at least 35 owned, phase-eligible Scope per rival** after legal 1977–79 Store progression, so the full-Scope release gate can be met. A 27-Scope January pool alone is below the current 30-Scope project requirement. Cash, bills, purchases and actual hand play still require legal receipts; no rating clamp or free January grant follows from this direction.
- Literal historical v3 Audio IDs cannot simply be copied into new v5 Studios: Sound Effects, Music and 8-Bit Music are retired from **future project supply**. Author an active, parent-consistent replacement roster preserving each Genre's intended Core/Scope profile and old-save provenance. Exact eight membership lists, 1977 capital, paid Store path, January projection, roster version and resulting Review distribution are **OPEN**.
