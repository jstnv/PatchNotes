## Hypothesis and cases

Holding the same development allowance and 1/1 Design/Alpha envelope for every legal node from one Studio decision will yield one identical owned-pool baseline Core/gap vector across the candidate screen. Actual Store admission, Research, quote and due checks remain separate. A candidate that cannot leave the held allowance after Research and known dues must defer rather than recompute a cheaper baseline.

## Bounds and stopping conditions

Use the v3 bounds unchanged: 500,000 attempted transitions, 100,000 retained states, 2,500,000 dominance comparisons and 10,000 generated hand tuples per phase. Each Godot process has a 240-second timeout. Stop ranking a seed if its owned baseline defers, its source identity fails, or any completed candidate produces a different owned baseline under the held plan. Retain every rejection, defer and timeout. No 2/2 or 3/3 budget increase, new node, extra seed, or numerical hand choice follows from this study. Run candidate checks with isolated profiles and outputs; preserve `.codex-godot-temp`, canonical gameplay and player saves.

## Interpretation and handoff boundary

Passing the invariant would support a candidate-independent **snapshot protocol**: freeze plan inputs once at Studio; keep a fixed development allowance for the whole screen; use candidate-specific price and known due obligations as separate affordability checks; recompute only at the next genuine Studio decision after state change. It would not prove that 60,000 cents or 1/1 hands are desirable, that all rivals can fund such a reserve, that Store progression is healthy, or that G2/G3 can enter canonical gameplay. Numeric allowance, hand counts, broader liabilities/traits, due windows, CPU limits and F6 progression remain **OPEN**.
