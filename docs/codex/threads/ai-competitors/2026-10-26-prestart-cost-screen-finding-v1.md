**Comparison warning:** the cost probe uses player ID `prestart-cost-<seed>`, while the earlier shadow probe used `prestart-shadow-<seed>`. `RivalTrial.create_world` derives rival seeds from the player's run ID and rival ID. Thus equal numeric seed and identical gameplay source do **not** give equal rival starting RNG or release results across those two studies. The differing 4417 Reviews and dates are not a policy regression or a valid paired comparison. The cost table is internally consistent; future paired work must keep the full player recipe/identity fixed or regenerate legal prefixes.

## Design consequence and open work

The two-rival, six-cycle legal advance and payload size do not by themselves justify a precomputed-template architecture. Try the simpler per-save legal tape first, with an explicit 1979 calendar origin and typed January finance/market provenance, then measure the actual conversion and cold save/load cost. The present data exclude those costs, more rivals, historical UI, authored thousands-Fan/bankroll/35-Scope resources, full-Scope/QA revisions and player challenge. Quality remains inconsistent: one 6.2 title among the four first releases in the two declared seeds, the others 4.4–4.9. No 8.0+ **live** release or usual 5.6–6.2 first-live distribution was established. This does not change the separate candidate of visible frozen 8.0+ historical titles. No TODO/adoption handoff is justified by this cost screen.
