## Exact rule

1. For every AI-controlled Studio—including opening incumbents, later entrants and a former player Studio while AI-controlled—the committed project Scope must satisfy `current_scope >= required_scope` **before Alpha can finish into Beta and again when Beta finalizes or the title launches**. Use the project's actual frozen required Scope and legally committed played Scope. Owned printed Feature supply, forecast Scope and a warning confirmation do not satisfy this check.
2. No cash shortage, exhausted draw pool, passage of time, missing Feature, desire for publication frequency, low Review forecast or AI stopping threshold overrides the gate. An AI must not request or confirm an under-Scope Alpha exit or submit an under-Scope launch. The authoritative AI release path must reject such a request without creating a release, consuming RNG or changing finance/Market/Fans. A choice-policy check alone is insufficient as the eventual implementation guard.
3. Prevent known impossible projects at Studio through an eligible, phase-legal finite supply and funding screen. If a live project becomes short, pursue only ordinary legal remaining-supply search or another supported recovery; otherwise retain a genuine blocked/unreleased project. A legal cancellation/restart path, draw-search allowance and distressed-project handling remain **OPEN**. Do not grant Scope, inject a Feature into a frozen project, mint cash, silently drop the project or publish it to maintain market density.
4. Retain the separate **LOCKED** rule to play every currently legal, affordable visible Feature in Design and Alpha, even after Scope is met. Scope permits phase progression only when the applicable Feature-exhaustion conditions are also satisfied. Beta Search→Debug and funded Marketing remain separate quality/reach decisions; full Scope alone does not guarantee a 5.6–6.2 Review or an 8.0+ game.
5. A pre-start rival game produced by simulated ordinary hands must satisfy the same gate. Frozen authored historical archive entries are not live release commands; their recorded Scope/provenance should be internally full-Scope if they are presented as completed games. Archived entries remain outside post-start sales, Fans, saturation, news and 1980-only rankings.

An older checkpoint may already contain an AI project in Beta below required Scope. It gains no grandfathered permission to publish under-Scope. Its legal recovery, migration or explicit blocked status must be designed and tested before adopting the guard; do not rewrite the saved Scope or release history to bypass validation.
