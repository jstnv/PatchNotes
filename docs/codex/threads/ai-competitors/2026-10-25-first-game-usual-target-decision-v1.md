# Player first-game target under strong incumbents — decision, 2026-10-25

**Status: LOCKED interpretation; numerical market and cohort design OPEN.** After the [opening capability review](2026-10-25-opening-market-capability-review-v1.md), the user chose **“Usually 7.0 or lower”** for the player's first game's Review. This resolves the review's absolute-versus-usual question. It does not impose a hard 7.0 Review cap, a hidden first-game penalty, or a proof obligation for every legal build. An exceptional legal first release may exceed 7.0. The old [universal 9.9 gate withdrawal](2026-10-09-first-game-gate-withdrawal-decision-v1.md) remains in force; the October 9 constructed 10.0 witness remains historical evidence, subject to current-roster revalidation.

## Intended opening experience

- Strong incumbents should be visible immediately through authentic frozen pre-1980 history, substantial opening Fans/capital and prerequisite-coherent owned Features. The user's requested bounds are **at least one 8.x or 9.x historical game per starting rival**, Fans in the thousands, and **at least 35 unique eligible printed Scope** per rival. Exact Fan count, cash, title Core vectors, Feature IDs and resulting post-start Reviews remain OPEN.
- Historical 8.x–9.x titles can inform an opening Review benchmark only through explicit, dated four-Core evidence and a versioned benchmark rule. They do not automatically become recent live releases or cause ongoing Genre/Theme sales saturation. Opening Review pressure should be visible in the frozen project target breakdown.
- The player target is about the distribution of normal first games, while rival quality is measured from games the rivals actually produce after January 1980. A strong archive does not count as a strong new rival release. Keep legal Design, Alpha and Beta hands and shared scoring for those games.

## Recommended bounded pilot — DESIGN, not adoption

1. Make a source-matched control from current `main`, including the current v3 player roster. Declare ordinary and high-effort legal player policies before seeing results; stratify fresh starts across all eight player Genres and fixed seeds. Record first-game Review, targets, Core, Scope, Bugs, variance, release date, sales and cash. A **provisional 80% at or below 7.0** ordinary-policy screen is a proposed operational meaning of “usually,” not an approved balance threshold. Preserve high-effort exceptions and the old 10.0 route as separate tail evidence.
2. Compare an opening **benchmark-only** candidate, an incumbent **resources/Feature-only** candidate, and their combination from separately regenerated compatible legal starts. Use the same seeds and declared policies, but validate starting states, RNG and rule differences; equal seeds alone do not imply a matched world. The benchmark pilot should explicitly expose any of the eight Genres with no qualifying historical title rather than silently applying a global penalty. Eight-Genre archival coverage is a candidate if the unserved Genres undermine the usual ≤7.0 goal.
3. For each rival's first two new games, measure actual Review, played Scope, residual Bugs, productive cycles, bills/arrears, title density and sales/Fan pressure against the player at the same calendar. Retain failures, incomplete cases, exact replays, Studio-only Continue and CPU/save cost. Do not improve headline Reviews by assigning a synthetic final score or delaying all publications out of the comparison window.

Only a later source-pinned finding can recommend cohort, benchmark and AI production values for adoption. The current +8 maximum market lift and active sales coefficients remain ACTIVE/TRIAL. No canonical gameplay, shared TODO or old-save migration is changed by this decision.

The [pre-run pilot protocol](2026-10-25-opening-incumbent-pilot-protocol-v1.md) records exact **OPEN** candidate inputs, comparison arms, policies to declare, seeds and stop conditions. Its values are not part of this LOCKED interpretation.
