# 2026-10-09 decision: count proportional specialty Core gaps equally

**Status:** **LOCKED overall Feature-shopping fit principle; OPEN feasible-vector selection and arithmetic details.** Authority: the user's choice **A** in this design thread on 2026-10-09, resolving the [proportional versus raw-gap brief](2026-10-09-next-decision-v7.md).

For a jointly attainable legal one-project Core mix, measure each remaining positive Graphics, Sound, Technology and Design gap as a **proportion of that Core's own permanent specialty target**. Count the four proportional gaps **equally** in the overall Feature-shopping fit comparison. Smaller combined remaining gap means better overall fit; equal combined gap is not a strict overall improvement. Raw missing Core points do not receive equal weight merely because they are points. The [prior cap](2026-10-09-excess-core-shopping-cap-decision-v1.md) makes a Core's gap zero at or above its target, with no extra credit or direct penalty for excess.

The targeted positive normalized shortfall must still strictly improve for a candidate to qualify. If the selected after-mix worsens another positive shortfall, the [tradeoff rule](2026-10-09-net-fit-tradeoff-decision-v1.md) additionally requires a strict reduction in the combined proportional gap. This comparison uses the studio's permanent specialty target for Feature shopping; each project's independent Genre still controls its **ACTIVE/TRIAL** Review targets and separate 1.25 per-Core Review ratio cap.

A [later A ruling](2026-10-09-best-overall-legal-mix-decision-v1.md) selects a jointly legal mix by best overall fit first, then the targeted gap when that target is known. Pre-target baseline ties, how exact rational values are represented or rounded, further legal-mix tie handling, cash/cycle assumptions, effective quotes, prerequisite lookahead and CPU method remain **OPEN**. The current target table's exact balance remains **ACTIVE/TRIAL**. This does not prescribe actual AI hands or change gameplay, the PR, `TODO.md`, or implementation handoff status.
