## What this does not yet establish

The prelude still uses its own 1977-based `RunState` at cycle72. Its four releases are live session ledger entries earning/settling during 1977–79; the private candidate **does not** close them into a frozen archive, reset the player clock to cycle0, hydrate a shared 1980 world or prove old-save/active-Market exclusion. Rewriting cycle fields is known to fail validation. A versioned typed projection with retained source/RNG/finance and historical-target provenance is the next Gate A task. Nor did this test generate first-release four-Core standards or model a shared rival market; later historical Reviews under the current 1980 base table are not evidence that the proposed new standards work.

Next one-rival work should (1) expose a legal finite-supply/hand quote instead of relying on private hand introspection, (2) build and reject-test the typed cycle72→1980 cycle0 projection with frozen archive accounting, and (3) in a separate numerical version, compare specialty-directed paid Feature buying and provisional target profiles. Reuse the two declared seeds and retain the 5.4 first game, zero 8+ histories and unplayed boundary project as failures. Do not queue the eight-rival implementation or treat $25,000, seven Beta hands or four releases as approved balance.
