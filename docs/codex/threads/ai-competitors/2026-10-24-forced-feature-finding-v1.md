## Design disposition

The user's forced-Feature rule **does** repair much of the premature Scope exit and materially improves Reviews without changing scoring. It is not ready as a complete challenge policy: full Scope does not reliably produce 6s/7s, and longer projects publish less often, easing the player's measured market. Keep the LOCKED qualitative Feature behavior; keep exact hand ranking, QA/redraw, owned Feature supply, full-project affordability, entrant/density response, CPU/save limits and any Core-efficiency bonus **OPEN**. A next study should preserve this legal Feature behavior while testing a separately specified QA policy and additional legal rival title supply/population at a matched fixed horizon. Do not turn the observed 9–13 cycles into a cap or a target. No canonical gameplay, TODO handoff, commit or push follows from this study alone.
