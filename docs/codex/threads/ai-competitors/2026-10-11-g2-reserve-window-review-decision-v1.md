## Recommended direction to review

For a first AI slice, keep **the locked Research-completion liquidity screen as the hard Store admission gate**, and require the fixed `B` to be present under that same Research-completion cash calculation if it is used in the F1/F5 comparison. Count current unpaid bills and known Research-window dues exactly once. Recheck actual cash and legal actions after Research and at later Studio/phase boundaries; use the selected finite project plan's legal exit when its remaining actions are unaffordable. This does **not** promise funding through launch. It avoids ranking a Feature against a project plan that is already unaffordable on the predicted ownership boundary, while allowing the player-like rival to use later actual sales to meet later rent. `B`, finite hands, later replan/exit thresholds and complete liabilities remain **OPEN**; do not implement from this record.

The reason is narrow. In two real first-release Studio fixtures, adding the entire eight-cycle known rent window to the hard admission test rejected the cheapest qualifying node at both 60,000-cent trial plans. A one-cycle Research window admitted the qualifying nodes and replayed the real payments. At a 100,000-cent Save Files trial, that narrower rule admitted a purchase that later lacked 100,000 cents after seven rent cycles in the prior control. Thus the recommendation is a **tradeoff for review**, not a proof of solvency or balance. Actual future sales, if earned, enter only when settled; they are not pre-credited by the candidate gate.
