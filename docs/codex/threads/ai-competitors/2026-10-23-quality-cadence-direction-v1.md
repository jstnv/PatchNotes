# Rival quality and cadence direction — 2026-10-23

**USER-DIRECTED target; numerical implementation policy OPEN.** The user observed rivals releasing about every seven cycles with average Review 3.7 and directed that rivals spend **at least ten committed productive cycles on each released game**, aiming for Reviews generally in the **6s or 7s**. This is a target for results from actual legal Design, Alpha and Beta hands, not a forced rating, universal guarantee, 7.x cap, artificial calendar wait or Review formula change. Preserve the separate resources, ordinary legal exits/launch, frozen market standards and active trial market transaction.

The [bounded finding](2026-10-23-rival-quality-cadence-finding-v1.md) shows why a fixed hand-count response is insufficient. A private 5/7/3 Action and 5/11/3 Adventure mix lifted individual Reviews through legal hands and made second titles without arrears. In two matched real worlds, it reduced two-rival publication count from sixteen to five by cycle 56 and increased the player's earned units and settled cash. **Reject that exact fixed mix for adoption as a challenge rule.** The current 2/2/2 LIVE/ACTIVE-TRIAL policy remains in canonical gameplay; its low quality is not accepted final balance.

**Next design gate:** jointly solve credible 6–7 Review production after at least ten cycles **and** enough actual recent rival titles to maintain or increase player market challenge. First test shorter legal hand/Feature choice and cash-aware quality exits; then test a bounded population/entrant route or a separately reasoned market-presence rule with the same fixed-horizon player outcomes. Do not make Reviews higher by injecting outputs or relaxing player Review standards. The exact hand counts, per-phase thresholds, cash quote, Feature acquisitions, incumbent/entrant count, timing, saved-state migration, CPU limit and any market-window/coefficient change stay **OPEN**. Market coefficients remain a separate design decision. A private variant or passing simulation cannot approve these values or alter canonical gameplay.
