# Rival two-hand QA choice — pre-run plan, 2026-10-25

**DESIGN/OPEN, private simulator study.** The prior [QA opportunity result](2026-10-24-beta-qa-opportunity-finding-v1.md) showed that five extra legal Search+Debug hands improved one opening title but lost a rival publication and eased player sales at cycle 56. This study asks whether better choices within the **existing two Beta hands** improve Bugs and Review without spending more cycles. It does not propose a new Beta hand count, Review formula, cash grant or player rule.

## Read-only current-policy audit first

Audit the frozen fixed-two source family, starting with the existing seed-4417 [control world](sims/rival-proportional-beta-control-4417-20261024-v1/world-run.json). For each distinct accepted first/second Beta hand, reconstruct the seven public candidate cards and chosen four from `rival_choices`. Enumerate structurally eligible four-card combinations using the current `rival_beta_synergy_choice.gd` assessment (finite uniqueness, categories, QA Search/Debug, specialization and public known-Bug/required-Scope bounds). Do **not** inspect hidden Bugs or future draws to choose. Count: visible Search+Debug pairs, selected Search+Debug, alternatives with lower average predicted remaining Bugs than the selected hand over hidden counts `0..required_scope`, current selector's dominance vetoes, Marketing and payout tradeoffs. The audit is observational; a visible combination is not proof that the authoritative command will accept it under all finance states. If there are fewer than two distinct missed Bug-reduction opportunities in seed4417, stop with a finding and no prototype.

## Conditional candidate and comparison

If the audit finds at least two opportunities, create a named AI Competitors variant **from the frozen fixed-two control**, preserving its recorded canonical baseline and ancestry. Change only private `project/scripts/market/rival_beta_synergy_choice.gd`: among structurally eligible visible hands, choose the Search+Debug hand with the smallest average public-proxy remaining Bugs over `0..required_scope` when it improves on the current chosen hand; tie on higher Marketing, then payout, then sorted tokens. Otherwise retain the current chosen hand. This deliberately relaxes the existing all-hidden-count dominance veto and permits a Marketing/income tradeoff; it is a candidate, not an approved optimal policy. Keep two productive Beta hands, current Design/Alpha, cohort, actual Beta settlement/RNG, market coefficients and player route unchanged. A second, more selective tradeoff rule requires a separate revision and plan.

Compare A fixed-two current selector versus B private same-duration selector from legal fresh starts with seed4417 Action/Fantasy first. Verify same initial full state and typed RNG and one private selector-source delta; no checkpoint crosses revisions. Limit each world to 210 player commands, eight player releases, cycle64, twelve zero-step transitions and 480 seconds. Retain rejected, failed and timed-out routes. If B rejects a hand or yields no changed accepted hand, stop and diagnose; do not silently adjust a seed, budget or policy. If B is legal and changes choices, also run matched seed4418 A/B under identical bounds to check whether the direction repeats. Frozen seed4417 A may be reused; seed4418 A requires its own independent snapshot and fresh legal start.

Measure accepted hand choices and Search/Debug operations, actual discovered/fixed/remaining Bugs, cash/Beta income/Marketing, project Review and release cycles, title count and right-censored projects at common cycle56, player earned units/settled sales/cash/Fans, world/checkpoint sizes and wall timing. Preserve project-by-project effects and distinguish source-driven later divergence from a direct hand effect. Run affected smoke/maintained checks, representative fresh-process exact replay and Studio-only Continue for every successful candidate seed. Candidate passes a **design screen** only if no rival title is lost at the fixed horizon, the 6.0+ count or mean Review improves, and player earned units do not rise in both seeds. This screen does not approve balance or implementation. If unmet, write a dated rejection finding; no handoff/TODO implementation entry. If met, retain the exact candidate and prepare a separate design review of the Marketing/income tradeoff before any adoption handoff.
