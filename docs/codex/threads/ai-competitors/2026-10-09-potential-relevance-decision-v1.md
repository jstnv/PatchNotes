Apply the already locked next-shortfall fallback, then earliest **available** era and cheapest effective Store quote within that era, only among relevant candidates that the rival can legally acquire. Existing gates, prerequisites, cash/payment/research and FIFO rules remain in force. This decision does not grant ownership on queue admission or prescribe a shopping route.
