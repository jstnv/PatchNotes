# G2 pre-admission reserve study v1 — 2026-10-11

**Status: predeclared private design experiment; not an approved AI rule or gameplay value.**

## Question and hypothesis

The prior held-plan check found a candidate that could complete Research but could not retain a larger development allowance afterward. Test a pre-admission guard at a legal Studio boundary: `current cash >= current conditional completed Research quote + known dues through Research and the declared development window + held development cash B`. This is a proposed AI spending policy only. The existing Store's legal admission and payment rules are the control.

The hypothesis is that this guard rejects the prior 100,000-cent Save Files negative control **before** charging its down payment, leaves state/RNG unchanged on rejection, and admits at least one affordable comparison candidate. A stricter no-future-sales rule may also reject Save Files at the 60,000-cent allowance that passed the earlier post-Research check; retain that difference as a finding, not a failed study to hide.

## Cases and boundaries

- Fork the private `g2-held-plan-v1` simulator into `g2-pre-admission-v1`; edit only its `project/` verifier. Record baseline, ancestry, candidate source, and canonical drift. Regenerate legal Studio prefixes in this version because source identities may differ; do not forge checkpoint identities.
- Two predeclared Action/no-trait seeds, `4417` and `90210`, with the existing finite fixture policy, 60-command cap, one release, 2/2/2 finite development hands. Require the resulting Studio checkpoint, source/RNG compatibility, and a fresh-process replay for both prefixes.
- At each checkpoint compare: no purchase; the prior first-ranked qualifying node (`animated_sprites` for 4417, `multi_directional_scrolling` for 90210); `save_files` with held B=60,000 and B=100,000 cents. These are **experimental** one Design/one Alpha plan allowances, not approved rival budgets or a population policy.
- Known-liability scope for the executable guard is the debt-free/no-employee Studio fixture. Count current unpaid obligations plus each scheduled $500 rent due during the next eight productive cycles: one Research action and seven declared development cycles. Do not credit prospective sales, unknown discounts, or future financing. Report unsupported liability states separately. The source audit will list Bank, education, and payroll behavior without claiming this small executable covers them.
- For each attempted Feature, use the live conditional completed quote, test legal `can_admit` and liquidity, then either reject without commands or perform real `admit_research` and `research`. Measure quote/down/remaining, known dues, B, acceptance reason, state/RNG before and after, cash and ownership, and exact command tape. Do not compare different post-acquisition states as identical; the common starting Studio checkpoint and RNG are the comparison boundary.

## Limits and stopping

At most two prefix cases, two fresh prefix replays, eight guard observations (two seeds × first-ranked and Save Files at the two B values, with one repeated first-ranked allowed), and representative fresh-process acquisition replay for each distinct admitted node. Each runner process has a 240-second timeout. Retain rejected, incomplete, failed, and timed-out records; do not change seeds, B, hand counts, horizon, or caps to improve outcomes. Stop after those declared cases or on source identity failure, and report the limitation. No canonical project edit, commit, push, or TODO implementation queue follows from a passing result.
