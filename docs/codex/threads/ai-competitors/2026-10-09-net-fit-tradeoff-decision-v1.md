# 2026-10-09 decision: allow a Core tradeoff only for better overall fit

**Status:** **LOCKED tradeoff principle; OPEN whole-vector math.** Authority: the user's choice **B** in this design thread on 2026-10-09, resolving the [no-worsening versus net-gain brief](2026-10-09-next-decision-v5.md).

For the currently targeted positive shortfall against a rival studio's permanent specialty four-Core target, completed legal ownership of a candidate Feature **may** qualify even if the resulting feasible project mix worsens another Core shortfall. It qualifies only when that candidate enables a newly attainable, jointly legal one-project mix that **strictly improves the targeted shortfall and strictly improves overall four-Core specialty-target fit** under the eventual whole-vector comparison. Equal overall fit, printed-stat improvement without new legal potential, or improvement only in another Core does not qualify for this target. If no candidate qualifies, use the already locked next-shortfall fallback; then apply earliest available era and cheapest effective legal Store quote among the relevant candidates.

This rules out an automatic veto for every cross-Core tradeoff. A [later B ruling](2026-10-09-excess-core-shopping-cap-decision-v1.md) stops shopping-fit credit at each specialty Core target without penalizing excess; later A rulings count [four proportional gaps equally](2026-10-09-equal-proportional-gap-decision-v1.md) and select the [best-overall legal mix first](2026-10-09-best-overall-legal-mix-decision-v1.md). Pre-target baseline ties, exact legal-mix search/arithmetic, cash/cycle assumptions, prerequisite lookahead, quote details and CPU method remain **OPEN**. The specialty target guides rival Feature shopping; each project's independent Genre still governs that project's Review calculation. Actual AI hands and Store acquisition remain legal under their existing rules.

This is design authority only. No gameplay, PR, `TODO.md` or implementation handoff change follows.
