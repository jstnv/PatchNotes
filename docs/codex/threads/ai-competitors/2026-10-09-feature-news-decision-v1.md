## Feature choice direction

For long-run acquisition, find the largest positive normalized shortfall across Graphics, Sound, Technology and Design against the studio's **permanent specialty** target. Consider a candidate Feature's complete Core vector, including secondary stats and any effect of excess Core on the attainable mix; the later shopping cap gives excess no direct credit or penalty. If no ratio-relevant legal candidate serves that shortfall, try the next shortfall. Among relevant legal candidates, prefer the earliest **available** era, then the cheapest effective Store quote within that era. Respect the rival's calendar/progress gates, ownership and prerequisites, spendable cash, payments/research and FIFO queue. Recalculate from the studio's state; do not prescribe a scripted shopping route.

The current Genre target table is **ACTIVE/TRIAL** for project Review scoring, and a project's Genre can differ from the permanent specialty. This decision does not change Review scoring.
