**Do not queue 3/5/2, this Beta redraw rule or a flat rival Core award for canonical gameplay.** A cheap arithmetic boost cannot rescue weak first releases under the current owned card pool and residual Bug pattern, and a longer two-rival plan leaves too few market titles. The next bounded design candidate should make incumbent starting capability and low-cost QA opportunities explicit while retaining legal hands and ≥10 cycles; project progression needs an earned, saved resource or authored pool improvement rather than a constant hidden Review bump.
