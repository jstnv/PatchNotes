## Recommendation and limits

Adopt the [candidate common-plan protocol](2026-10-11-g2-held-plan-candidate-decision-v1.md) for further design review: freeze `B` and hands once per Studio shopping decision, keep the baseline fixed across candidate nodes, and defer an unfundable held plan instead of letting the candidate price quietly redefine the target. This 60,000-cent study only exercised candidates that **could** leave that allowance; the [later negative control](2026-10-11-g2-held-plan-unfunded-control-finding-v1.md) separately exercises the private no-partial-rank return. Its pre-admission guard, all bill types, first-game/trait/employee states, 2/2–3/3 capacity, actual AI policy, F5 runtime adapter and F6 prerequisite progression remain **OPEN**. The numerical `B`, hand counts, due horizon and CPU limits require separate choice/evidence. Passing these narrow screens is not balance or canonical adoption.
