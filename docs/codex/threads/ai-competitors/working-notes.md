## First reconciliation: Store, Genre, Fanbase and calendar

- **Store:** Starting specialty grants a fixed Primitive roster once. Optional player starter Primitive purchases are instant and zero-cycle before first departure; later acquisition uses unlocked-only FIFO research, zero-cycle half-base admission, paid productive Research actions and ownership only at completion. A queued card is not available for a hand or as a parent. Familiarity changes installments when paid. Future era years are necessary but their extra gates and node data remain OPEN. Rival shopping must use its own Store state and legal payment path; quote-comparison details remain OPEN. See [Store design](../../design/feature-store-progression.md).
- **Genre:** The current **ACTIVE/TRIAL** 132-total target table supplies distinct Graphics/Sound/Technology/Design standards. The studio's permanent specialty grants its roster; each project's Genre is independent and controls that project's Review targets. The October 9 shopping direction uses the permanent specialty target for long-run Feature gaps, without changing the project's Review calculation. Later rulings choose [owned input](2026-10-09-owned-feature-capability-decision-v1.md), [one-project potential](2026-10-09-legal-project-capability-decision-v1.md), a [deterministic ceiling](2026-10-09-deterministic-ceiling-decision-v1.md), [excess-Core cap](2026-10-09-excess-core-shopping-cap-decision-v1.md) and [equal proportional-gap fit](2026-10-09-equal-proportional-gap-decision-v1.md); exact vector selection and arithmetic remain OPEN. See [Genre design](../../design/genre-specialties.md).
- **Fanbase:** Each rival owns a separate audience ledger. Review exactly 5.0 is Fan-neutral; above/below may gain/lose through actual earned-unit monthly accounting, not a launch projection. Current gain/loss coefficients and `min(150, floor(Fans/4))` launch Awareness are **ACTIVE/TRIAL** player-system values, not new competitor balance approvals. See [Fanbase design](../../design/fanbase.md).
- **Calendar:** January 1980 starts the run; two productive cycles form a month. Browsing, rejected actions, redraws, phase transitions and launch have no calendar cost. A rival gets one legal productive opportunity on a committed player productive cycle. A rival release records its frozen result at launch; its news becomes eligible at the *next* month start and appears at the first safe player-facing point. Launch cash is not instantly spendable; sales and settlement follow each studio's own ordinary monthly ledger. Exact transaction order and numeric CPU bound remain OPEN. See [world decision](2026-10-09-rival-world-delegated-decisions-v1.md), [core rules](../../design/core-and-studio.md) and [lifespan](../../design/game-lifespan.md).
