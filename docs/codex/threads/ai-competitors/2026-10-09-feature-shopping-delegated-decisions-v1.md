## Calculation and legality boundary

The [overall-first legal-mix priority](2026-10-09-best-overall-legal-mix-decision-v1.md), [equal proportional-gap comparison](2026-10-09-equal-proportional-gap-decision-v1.md), [net-fit tradeoff rule](2026-10-09-net-fit-tradeoff-decision-v1.md) and target/era/price fallback remain in force. A candidate is modeled as owned only **after** legal Store research completes. Compare the same frozen project plan before and after; check acquisition affordability separately through the rival's own spendable cash and existing transaction preflight. Do not count queued supply, unearned sales, future familiarity discounts or an impossible combination of independent Core maxima. Actual AI hands and Store transactions must still be legal when played; planning does not guarantee a draw, Review or purchase.

No ordinary project-cycle cap exists in current source, so the finite planning envelope is essential to keep this ceiling distinct from a raw catalog total. If the legal-mix search cannot complete within the eventually profiled budget, the safe design outcome is **defer the shopping decision**, not substitute a catalog sum or a guessed Review. This is a search-failure behavior, not approval of a CPU time limit.
