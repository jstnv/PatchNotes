## Design disposition and next evidence

**Do not select this exact `gap_rank` or $2,500 phase cash floor for canonical AI.** Marginal printed Core/Scope alone did not consistently improve same-count Reviews and sometimes selected materially costlier hands. The floor observes cash only *after* prior commitments, so it cannot guarantee an affordable next hand, known dues or retained project allowance. Retain the LOCKED deterministic phase-local/legal-exit direction; keep objective, weights, affordability calculation, hand counts, redraw/exit thresholds, Theme/later Genre, entrant rules, G1a numbers, G2/G3 integration and CPU limits **OPEN**.

The next G4 design candidate should expose an exact read-only current-hand cost/known-dues quote through the shared rules, then compare legal hand selection and phase exits on **Review gain per committed cash/calendar exposure**. It should record reasons for deferral and second-project opportunity cost. This is a recommendation for a new versioned private test, not a selected formula or implementation handoff. No market effect on Reviews is inferred. Item 22 already says DESIGN review needed; no TODO or canonical gameplay edit follows from this trial.
