## Exact intent at the legal phase boundaries

The [opening pilot's](2026-10-25-opening-incumbent-pilot-protocol-v1.md) proposed 35 printed owned Scope provides headroom but does not itself satisfy the project. Only legally played Design/Alpha Features and any ordinary project effects count. The AI must observe `current.scope >= current.required_scope` before finishing Alpha into Beta and before launch. Design may end below the target if its current legal Feature opportunities are exhausted and Alpha can still complete it.
2. **Retain Feature exhaustion and search remaining supply.** In each Design/Alpha deal choose the maximum feasible visible Feature count in an ordinary affordable four-card hand, then re-evaluate the refill. Reaching 30 early does not authorize abandoning a still-playable Feature. If Scope is short and no Feature is visible, distinguish **owned, unplayed phase supply** from an empty supply; the former is knowable inventory, not a peek at future RNG. Test legal redraws and then a financed, finite ordinary Pass-hand search only when that inventory can still close the shortfall. The exact draw/search allowance and cash screen are **OPEN**. An AI Alpha exit with Scope shortfall must not request or confirm the existing under-Scope warning. A blocked project stays a real blocked project; the AI cannot fabricate Scope, a release, a new Feature or an unbounded Pass loop.
3. **Prevent known impossible starts.** Before a rival begins a project, verify that its frozen eligible finite Feature supply can in principle produce at least the required Scope, with phase and prerequisite legality respected. Screen funding and known dues with the separate [project runway ledger](2026-10-24-project-runway-ledger-v1.md). A static supply deficit must be addressed at Studio through a legal owned-Feature/Store plan or a deferred start, not by beginning a project that can only release under-Scope. The exact draw-aware guarantee, cash policy, later-Store path and behavior if a live project becomes irrecoverably short remain **OPEN**. The current Wayfinder v3 owned pool has only 27 printed Scope; simply enforcing a full-Scope launch gate on today's cohort would make its first release impossible without a supply change.
4. **Beta: Bugs first, then reach.** Interpret “Debug” as reducing remaining Bugs through **legal Search and Debug cards**: Search reveals Hidden Bugs and resolves before Debug in the same actual hand; Debug fixes Known Bugs. The AI may use only public observations, never its hidden Bug count or future draws. Rank a legal, useful QA opportunity ahead of Marketing, using observed Known Bugs and a declared, bounded estimate for undiscovered Bugs. Once another QA hand has no defensible expected benefit, consider Marketing Output if the next productive hand and known month-end dues are affordable without a new loan and a stated post-launch reserve remains. A mixed legal hand may deliver both; card arithmetic and specialization remain authoritative. Marketing Output increases launch reach; it is **not cash paid into the bank**. Beta need not reach zero hidden Bugs or a mandatory Marketing amount before launch.
5. **Finite publication decision.** Cash is a ceiling on optional Beta work, not a reason to farm renewable Marketing indefinitely. Compare marginal QA/Marketing benefit with the lost publication cycle, then use the ordinary Beta exit and launch when no justified funded hand remains. Exact hand ranking, Bug estimate, Marketing value, reserve, opportunity horizon and finite stopping threshold are **OPEN**; no arbitrary game-cycle cap, free cash, Review lift, new loan or player-rule change is selected here.
