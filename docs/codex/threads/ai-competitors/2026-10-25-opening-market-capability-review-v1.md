## Recommended design split

1. **Authentic incumbent head start.** Author at least one 8.x–9.x pre-1980 release per incumbent, with a dated, internally coherent four-Core result if it will serve as a benchmark. Keep the archive frozen: no continuing sales or backfilled monthly Fans. Give each January 1980 Studio a separate, once-journaled historical retained-capital and opening-Fans snapshot with explicit provenance; do not relabel an invented pre-run month as current income. Build an authored, prerequisite-coherent, era-legal owned Feature set meeting the requested **35 unique project-eligible printed Scope** for its specialty. Audit draw/finite-use legality, affordability and actually played Scope separately. Ordinary post-start Design/Alpha/Beta hands and Review scoring still decide current games. Exact dollar, Fan, card and Core values remain OPEN.
2. **Opening benchmark layer.** Seed a versioned January 1980 **Review-standard-only** record from eligible historical titles, preserving title/owner/date/Genre/four-Core provenance and a clear expiry or handoff to actual market facts. Freeze the benchmark at legal project begin, show its contribution in the existing target breakdown, and keep it out of active Genre/Theme sales saturation. This is a candidate architecture, not approval to insert old titles into the live release index or change the current +8 coefficient. Changing the month-zero neutral identity, market validation and old-world compatibility would need explicit migration tests.
3. **Player first-game target.** The user's [later choice](2026-10-25-first-game-usual-target-decision-v1.md) is **usually 7.0 or lower**, with exceptional legal scores above 7.0 allowed. Measure first-game distributions and high-end routes separately across all eight Genres, legal starter/Store and funding routes, project length, Scope, Bugs and supported variance. Recheck the old 10.0 witness against the current roster as tail evidence if still legal. The prior universal gate was [withdrawn](2026-10-09-first-game-gate-withdrawal-decision-v1.md), and this choice does not restore it.

## Experiment order and acceptance

Before any code candidate, pin the authored archive/Core vectors, Genre coverage, starting capital/Fans, exact owned IDs and benchmark lifetime in a versioned trial table. First run a source-matched read-only opening formula screen, including a current-roster recheck of the old 10.0 witness and all eight Genre bases. Then use a new thread-owned simulator variant for: (A) historical benchmark only, (B) legal incumbent resources/Feature pool only, and (C) both together. Regenerate each arm's legal fresh start; compare matched player/rival RNG and the actual first two rival releases, review distribution, playable Scope, Bugs, cash/arrears and first-game player Reviews, followed by same-calendar title density, sales/Fans, checkpoint/replay and CPU. Include a disjoint player Genre and prior-world Studio-only Continue. Do not silently raise the market +8 cap or synthesize a final rival Review.

**Acceptance for further design review**, not gameplay adoption: each rival has a genuine archived 8.x–9.x record, authenticated opening Fans/cash and ≥35 unique eligible owned Scope; legal current projects show meaningful early Review improvement without losing the player's market challenge to publication delay; January benchmark provenance and old-world Continue survive validation. Test the usual ≤7.0 first-game target through a declared distribution; no universal ceiling is claimed. Current two-rival trial values, news and leaderboard remain unchanged; no canonical implementation or TODO handoff follows from this review.

The 8.x–9.x historical-title target is distinct from the Review distribution of games rivals create after January 1980. The proposed opening records can establish reputation and a benchmark, but the current AI must still earn any new 8.x–9.x result through legal production, QA and the same Review calculator. That post-start quality target remains OPEN and needs its own observed acceptance threshold.
