# G4 hand-choice proxy — design review, 2026-10-18

**Decision: reject the tested printed-benefit proxy and cheapest-affordable as universal rival Design/Alpha hand rules. Detailed G4 choice remains OPEN.** This is a disposition of two private test policies, not a change to the LOCKED finite legal phase-plan direction or the current ACTIVE/TRIAL rivals. It follows the [source-pinned finding](2026-10-18-g4-benefit-choice-finding-v1.md).

The benefit proxy produced 4 higher, 10 equal and 2 lower first Reviews against token-first across 16 matched Genre/seed openings; its mean Review difference was zero and Puzzle/4417 lost 0.4. All arms remained legal, but the declared screen required a consistent material gain without regression. A second Action/4417 release tied token-first at 2.6 with only $50 more cash. Cheapest-affordable lowered first Review in all 16 and its bounded second Review was 1.0 versus 2.6. These results do not justify a universal switch. The two-handed plan itself was held fixed, so this review cannot select final hand allowances or exit thresholds.

**Recommended next design slice:** retain the current features-first trial as a reproducible control, then test an exact visible production/Scope/Bug benefit projection with next-hand opportunity and legal phase exits in a real incumbent world, including Store admissions. The current 35-choice quote may be a useful cash input, but its unavailable reasons, low-cash behavior and population CPU are unresolved. Do not use an arbitrary fixed cash floor or a price-only selection. Avoid hard-coding Genre targets into the policy when market pressure is activated: the project-frozen Review standards must be visible to an AI planner. Keep the market's effect on Reviews as its own design review and its numerical values OPEN.
