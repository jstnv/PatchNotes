## Disposition

**Do not hand off this one-node Store policy as a challenge improvement.** It proves a legal later Feature can be funded, owned, drawn and played, and can lift a near-term Review modestly. In both declared campaigns the Research month costs a rival publication within the fixed horizon, and later Reviews do not show a consistent quality gain. The current two-hand, low-Core plans remain the larger challenge bottleneck.

Next design review should pair an affordable **Design-shortfall** candidate with a richer-pool hand selector that keeps synergy and cost reasoning after purchase. Predeclare a longer matched horizon and overlapping player route to test both Review quality and market pressure; include known next-cycle rent/payroll/debt service in the AI admission screen. The exact node, jointly attainable Core potential math, held next-project allowance, later Genre/Theme choices, entrants, high-Review news, leaderboard, population CPU and final trial coefficients remain **OPEN**. No implementation handoff, canonical edit, commit or push follows from this result.
