## Shadow affordability from committed actions

The separate [read-only runway audit](sims/rival-runway-shadow-audit-20261024-v15.json) covers v15 B/C across both seeds. It uses the frozen v15 `card_ledger.json`, confirms every Studio has $500 rent and **no payroll, Bank or education account**, and matches every selected Design/Alpha Feature hand's exact-cent printed fee to the saved `feature_play` finance action at that Studio/project/cycle. The harness logs post-boundary *suggested* actions as well as actual ones; the audit joins to committed finance entries so previews do not count as spend. It reports separate Design/Alpha played and spent meters after each hand, the unplayed known-price roster, phase-aware hand lower bound, known future rent and cash margin. A rejected initial audit join is retained in the session log and corrected before output; it never changed a world.

Across **70 legally started projects**, 60 have released by the fixed horizon and ten are right-censored active projects. The four provisional rosters contain Design/Alpha counts of 9/12, 11/11, 11/10 and 11/9, with full known play quotes **$1,550, $1,410, $1,380 and $1,460** respectively. Each has a six-Feature-hand *phase-separated lower bound*. All twelve observed first projects start with $5,850 and have positive no-future-income lower-bound margins of **$2,300–$2,470** after the full pool quote and known rent for one setup, six Feature hands and two Beta hands.

The lower bound is a poor completion-time promise: **46/60** completed projects use more than six Design/Alpha Feature hands, with actual Feature hands spanning **5–10** and total setup-plus-development durations **8–13** cycles. A legal visible-pool exit can use fewer than six hands while leaving unseen roster Features; only **28/60** completed projects play their entire frozen roster. Actual direct Feature spend ranges **$1,250–$1,550**. This confirms why `played/total`, actual spend, current all-pool quote and draw-dependent time must be separate fields.

At Studio start, **16/60 completed projects** have *negative* cash margin even under the six-hand, no-future-income lower bound; all are later projects. Using each release's observed duration instead of the lower-bound duration gives **19/60** negative start margins. Nevertheless these titles legally completed while existing portfolio sales settled during development. Across all seventy starts, eighteen are negative under the lower-bound screen. Only three committed post-hand snapshots have negative phase-aware, no-future-income remaining margin; those are warnings, not illegal hands or proven failures. A strict `cash ≥ all unplayed Features + all future rent` admission gate would have deferred at least sixteen observed completions **if applied at those starts**. That is a counterfactual inference; its downstream titles, sales and rivals have not been simulated.

## Design disposition and next gate

Keep the requested **Design/Alpha runway ledger** as an internal planning direction, with exact committed spend and counts, start quote provenance, rolling reprices, known typed bills and two time views: a hard hand lower bound and an explicitly uncertain draw-aware planning estimate. For AI decisions, distinguish **cash-backed** (known cost/due window covered without future income), **contingent** (next action legal but full window needs future receipts or a shorter realized draw), and **blocked** (the actual next command/bill cannot preflight). A contingent warning must not silently override the LOCKED rule requiring currently legal affordable visible Features; nor may a forecast spend unearned sales. These are design categories, **not approved thresholds or implemented gameplay**.

Do **not** queue the one-redraw rule, the four-Studio opening cohort or a hard full-project admission screen. The next bounded candidate should test legal QA selection with sufficient Search+Debug opportunity under forced Feature exhaustion, then measure first/later Reviews jointly with a legally authored publisher/entrant schedule and player-market density. Separately test whether a shadow forecast using *already earned unsettled amounts* and a disclosed sales scenario can guide Studio admission without stalling subsequent releases; preserve next-hand cash legality and no cycle cap. Exact QA allowance, Genre/Theme variation, entrant identities/resources/timing, sales-risk policy, unknown later Feature fees, market numbers and CPU/save limits remain **OPEN**. No approval of 6s/7s balance or implementation handoff follows from this study.
