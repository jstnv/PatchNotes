## Selected architecture for the legal opening

1. **Versioned offline prelude.** Build each accepted opening cohort from actual 1977–79 legal CampaignSession commands under a prelude calendar, with owned-parent/era/Store, cash, bills, Bank, phase hands, RNG and release Review receipts retained. Generate and certify cohort tapes offline, then select a sealed version deterministically for each new save. New-save creation hydrates a validated January projection; it does not synchronously replay three years for eight rivals. An unsupported or invalid tape fails construction rather than inventing a release, cash, Feature or Scope. The number of certified cohort tapes and whether variety is sufficient remain OPEN.
2. **First Genre coverage through certification, not force.** Each of the eight Genres needs a first real 1977 release with recorded four-Core output before that Genre can have a generated persistent benchmark. The assigned specialist may make that founding release only if its owned pool, funding and legal hands support a competent project in its specialty. A candidate tape that cannot supply such a legal first release is rejected and retained as evidence; the AI does not override the funded Core-fit rule. Later projects may use any suitable Genre. This reconciles one-specialist-per-Genre history with dynamic project-Genre choice.
3. **Standards are an explicit historical source.** The first release records its full Core vector, Review, Scope/Bugs and date. A versioned transformation derives the four initial persistent standards from that release and its provisional scoring profile. Make the result effective at the **start of the following month** for new project Begins only. Later historical releases can only maintain or raise the corresponding historical benchmark under a versioned rule; frozen projects and published Reviews never change. At January 1980, retain the eight benchmark vectors separately from the active Market release index. The exact provisional 32-category table, transformation, rise limit and January interaction with the ACTIVE/TRIAL 1980 base table are **OPEN**. Uniform 75% targets are rejected by the earlier screen; `standards = cores` has not been approved.
4. **January projection is a typed boundary.** The certified tape yields eight independent Studio resource snapshots, eight typed frozen archives, their benchmark provenance and a distinct legal project already in progress for each rival. The user's requested January opening capability is **at least 35 printed, owned, phase-eligible Scope per rival project**, acquired through the legal prelude rather than granted at projection; actual played Scope must still meet the frozen requirement before release. The current new-Studio roster begins at 12 printed Scope, so a 1977 first project already needs at least 18 more eligible printed Scope to reach the current required 30, and a January 35-Scope supply needs at least 23 more if no separate lawful incumbent grant is approved. Printed supply remains a necessary, not sufficient, hand/affordability condition. Project phase, committed Core/Scope/Bugs, remaining finite Feature supply, visible deal/RNG and finance obligations must project into the cycle0 shared world through a versioned validator. The pre-1980 live sales ledgers are closed into history; no old title is inserted into `released_games` or replayed for January income. No arbitrary cycle-field rewrite is a valid projection. This explicitly crosses the source's 1977 prelude clock to the player's unchanged January 1980 cycle0 clock.
5. **Prospective only.** Activate the new cohort for new saves with an explicit opening version. Existing two-rival saves retain their exact rival IDs, archives, ownership, projects, cash, history and RNG; do not backfill six Studios or re-score old titles on Continue. A later opt-in migration needs separate authority and evidence.
6. **Opening challenge measured by the whole market.** A handful of late-1970s 8.0+ historical titles across multiple Studios should be visible, with earlier weaker titles and ordinary early 1980 live releases from January work in progress. The provisional screen is 3–5 historical 8.0+ titles across at least two non-Racing Studios, not a scripted rating quota. Historical leaders and 1980-only leaders have separate labels. The user's wish that a rival ordinarily leads 1980 Reviews remains a **separate OPEN outcome**; historical records cannot satisfy it. Measure live Review, release density, Genre/Theme sales crowding and player cash together before claiming challenge.
7. **Performance is part of admission.** Offline tapes avoid per-save multi-year simulation, but all eight active rivals still advance on each player productive cycle. The planner must use bounded, deterministic search/caching with exact legal fallback and source/RNG parity; it cannot skip legal Feature work to meet a timing target. Eight-rival CPU, memory/save size and actual input responsiveness must be measured after that hotspot is addressed; no eight-rival runtime budget is assumed to pass.
