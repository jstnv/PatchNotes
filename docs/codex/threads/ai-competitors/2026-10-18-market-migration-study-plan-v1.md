# Pre-market save migration — predeclared private study, 2026-10-18

## Design question and status

The [selected market transaction boundary](2026-10-17-market-transaction-boundary-decision-v1.md) preserves already earned sales and already begun project Review standards. The [v10 finding](2026-10-18-market-transaction-finding-v1.md) passes new-source checkpoint routes but leaves named-source migration **NOT RUN**. This study asks whether a trusted, whole-checkpoint adapter can import one exact pre-market source without changing past units, cents, Fans, release Reviews, project state or RNG. The migration contract is **CANDIDATE** until verified. The market coefficients are **OPEN/TRIAL** and remain unchanged in this study.

## Hypothesis and rules under test

An old committed Studio/world pair can be validated against an exact named old gameplay source and original field shape, copied in memory, and extended with: (1) neutral 10000-bp factor records for each release-age month that already earned at least one half; (2) fixed pre-market Genre standards and a tagged legacy source for active rival projects; and (3) a committed current-month snapshot containing only validated owner releases older than that month's first cycle. A half-earned old age month keeps its neutral factor on the next half. The original checkpoint is never rewritten in place; unknown source IDs or malformed old fields reject. The new pair must pass the *current candidate* player/world codec, Fanbase/finance validation and exact Studio Continue before saving a new generation. A player-only old save keeps no rivals.

The exact allowed source for the primary fixture is the old audit-only control's non-debug gameplay identity `f8f130c6a3e6b7d27710e11a56b445ea6c73d9dbc26ca26a8501ca2cf7ca32de`, verified by its retained run report. The current canonical presentation-compatible identities are a separate later acceptance set; do not add them without a matching legal fixture. Old and new source identities remain distinct. Never edit a checkpoint source marker to satisfy a guard.

## Comparison cases and legal prefixes

1. Generate actual old-source player/world Studio pairs in an audit-only child of the unchanged control, seed `4417`, no Traits, current two trial incumbents, no grants, legal zero-work player idle cycles and legal rival policies. Save committed pairs at cycles 7, 8 and 10. Cycle 7 has same-month rival releases excluded from snapshot6; cycle 8 has the first earned half and those releases in snapshot8; cycle 10 has a partly earned second age month and an active second rival project. Retain the old pair bytes plus old codec validation/digests/RNG and the old control's sales/cents/Fans.
2. Import those copied fixtures into a new private migration child of v10. Validate original source/shape, migrate a copy, validate new pair, cold restore, and resume one or two productive cycles to check a partial age month's neutral factor followed by first new market factor where legal. Compare all pre-migration owner fields and RNG exactly; new schema fields and subsequent earnings are expected differences.
3. A separate legal old player-only Studio checkpoint with and without earned sales must remain worldless after migration. An exact unknown-source world, a missing old sales field, and changed earned cents should reject without mutating the original fixture. A pre-1980 Founder/former-Studio save remains a later named-source case if the primary control source cannot legally produce it under the bound; mark it NOT RUN.

## Measurements, limits and stopping

Record old and new source identities/ancestry, schema field counts, old source guard, original and migrated checkpoint hashes, full per-owner release/RNG/units/cents/Fans/cash/project fields, snapshots and age-month factor histories, rejection reasons, fresh-process Continue/replay, exact commands and process exits. The old audit fixture and new candidate must use independent frozen variant projects, profiles and new output folders. Use `tools/simulator_variant.py create` and `tools/simulate.py run --variant`, then the documented isolated native wrapper and fresh replay. Maximum 10 old productive cycles, 2 post-migration cycles per fixture, 50 player commands, 2 legal rival releases, and 240 seconds per process. Keep seed `4417`, rival policy, trial coefficients and output limits fixed. Do not extend a failed route or silently switch seeds.

Stop adoption on source/provenance drift, old codec rejection, schema/finance/Fanbase mismatch, mutated original bytes, changed pre-migration accounting/Review/RNG, forged-source acceptance, invalid current-month release inclusion, or failed cold Continue. Retain every failure. A successful private adapter remains **CANDIDATE** until current canonical source, old player-only and Founder variants, actual Coordinator generation recovery, numbers, and integration are reconciled. Only then consider a source-pinned adoption handoff and minimal TODO edit. Canonical `patch-notes/`, player saves, `.codex-godot-temp`, commit/push and computer control are outside this study.
