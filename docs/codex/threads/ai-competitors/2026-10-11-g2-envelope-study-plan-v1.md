## Question and hypothesis

G2 needs a finite fresh-next-project Design/Alpha planning envelope, but the number of planned hands is OPEN. Test whether an additional planned hand in each phase changes legally achieved Core and Scope on a next project, and whether longer plans encounter cash or exit blockers. Hypothesis: 1/1, 2/2 and 3/3 Design/Alpha hands produce materially different legal vectors, so a count cannot be chosen from a catalog sum or the example fixture's two-hand setting. This is an observed-policy sample, **not** the exact jointly attainable ceiling or a recommendation to set a G2 value.

## Cases and legal common start

- Seeds: decimal strings `4417` and `90210`, no seed substitution. Each creates a legal Action-specialty Studio with no traits through recipe schema 1. Fixed project Genre `action`, Theme `fantasy`, `features_first` public-candidate policy; no Store purchase, Bank, Contract, employees, courses or rival world. These are policy exclusions, not rules changes.
- Prefix: one legal release per seed using existing `finite_fixture_policy.gd`, two Design, two Alpha, two Beta hands, 60-command cap. Retain each resulting *Studio* checkpoint. A failed or non-Studio prefix is retained and excludes only that seed's dependent arms; never manufacture a starting checkpoint.
- Comparison: from the **same checkpoint bytes for a given seed**, one additional release in three arms: Design/Alpha `1/1`, `2/2`, `3/3`; Beta fixed at two hands. Same policy source, Genre, Theme and candidate order. Each arm has its own fresh policy instance. Within-seed initial state hash and RNG must agree; if they do not, comparison is invalid. A release target is two total.
- The arms differ in committed commands and therefore later RNG and calendar paths. Compare achieved vectors and costs as conditional routes, not as a causal effect isolated from all RNG differences. Across seeds, recipes differ and are not paired.

## Measurements, limits and stopping

Record case status/rejection, accepted commands, final Studio boundary, release cycle, Design/Alpha/Beta development cycles, achieved four-Core vector, Scope and required Scope, Bugs, Review as an observation only, cash, arrears/finance, and any Store/ownership drift. Retain raw result, tape, telemetry and reports. Each case stops at the next release, 60 commands, or 240 seconds per import/run. Stop the study after these two seeds and three arms, even if an arm fails or looks unfavorable. Replay one completed arm per seed in a fresh process; replay any rejected representative if present. Do not increase budgets or add seeds post hoc.

## Evidence and authority boundary

Run with `tools/simulate.py run/replay` into thread-owned unique output directories. Use the authoritative current command resolver and the maintained finite fixture policy without modifying it. G2's true planner must enumerate jointly feasible legal hands, finite Feature use, cash after known dues and Store candidate ownership. This study tests a small observed route only. A chosen hand count, search math, CPU bound or price decision stays **OPEN** pending a design ruling. Market effects on Reviews remain separate.
