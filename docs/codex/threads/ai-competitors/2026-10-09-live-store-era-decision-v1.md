# 2026-10-09 decision: one era tier for the live later Store catalog

**Status: LOCKED first-slice ranking metadata; future era specifications OPEN.** The user selected one **current-live era tier** for the 11 later Store nodes already present in the playable catalog. They lack individual era fields; the catalog's broad 1981–1983 description is not authority to assign each node a particular year. This is a rival-shopping comparison tier, not a new calendar unlock or a rollout of proposed cards.

After the existing legal/unlocked/owned/relevance screen, rival shopping preserves the [locked earliest-available-era priority](2026-10-09-feature-shopping-delegated-decisions-v1.md). The live later nodes compare as one tier; within it, use the current conditional completed-ownership quote, then the previously selected Research-action and stable-ID ties. Current Primitive reserve Features retain their existing earlier progression position and Store legality. Actual ownership still requires the ordinary FIFO research and payment path. Requote installments when paid.

Future or merely proposed nodes remain unavailable until their own content, availability and per-node era metadata are approved. Do not guess a date from a label, use a node's price as its era, or treat this classification as approval of future content.
