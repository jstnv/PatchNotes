# 2026-10-09 decision brief: finite rival project policy

**Status: Choice A selected by the later [finite project-policy decision](2026-10-09-finite-project-policy-decision-v1.md); exact allowances remain OPEN.** [A2](2026-10-09-ai-scope-delegated-decisions-v1.md) already requires bounded deterministic legal choices, but it does not say when a rival stops developing and launches. Current projects have no ordinary hard cycle cap. Passes are renewable, Beta actions can remain useful, and a legal under-Scope launch can occur after the ordinary confirmation when positive Feature work exists. A rival that simply keeps playing every positive-looking action may remain in a project indefinitely.

| Choice | Behavior | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Finite project plan with legal exit (recommended)** | Give the AI an explicit finite Design/Alpha/Beta decision allowance and a state-based launch policy. At the policy stop, take an ordinary legal phase exit or launch, including a legal under-Scope launch when selected; otherwise idle/recover through an explicit future rule. Every played hand still uses actual candidates, costs and shared resolution. | Guarantees a project-level stopping rule without imposing a new legal cycle cap on the player or granting scripted output. Exact allowances, targets and fallback remain **OPEN**. |
| **B — Continue while any modeled improvement exists** | Keep taking legal productive actions and seek Scope 30 before launch. | May never terminate with renewable Passes/Beta choices and would turn the player's Scope warning into an unapproved AI legality rule. |
| **C — Fixed scripted release cycle and result** | End at a predetermined calendar point and assign a target Review. | **REJECTED by existing locks:** bypasses actual legal hands and ordinary Review scoring. Included only as the prohibited shortcut, not an option to approve here. |

This decision does not choose hand scores. A later policy must rank displayed legal candidates, redraws, cash costs, Bugs/QA and project Genre Review targets; stable ties and saved per-studio RNG remain required. A project may choose a Genre different from the studio's permanent specialty under existing rules. The user later [selected specialty as each rival's first playable project Genre](2026-10-09-first-project-genre-decision-v1.md); a legal best-fit or varied first Genre remains the logged alternative. Later Genre selection, Theme, trait loadouts, exact policy weights, exit thresholds and AI population remain **OPEN**.

The finite project allowance is an AI choice budget, not the Store planner's G2 hypothetical hand envelope, a universal player project cap or a change to the current Review formula. It must be chosen with source-matched route and resource evidence before implementation, then checked against representative long-running worlds for G5 performance.
