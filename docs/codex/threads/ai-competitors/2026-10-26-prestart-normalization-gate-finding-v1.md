## Failed candidate and interpretation

The first diagnostic version [v1](simulators/opening-prestart-normalization-gate-20261026-v1/variant.json) had a probe parse error (`Cannot infer the type of "unchanged"` at line 58); its [failed run receipt](sims/opening-prestart-normalization-gate-20261026-v1-seed4417-01/prestart-normalization-gate-run.json) and log are retained. v2 changed only the diagnostic's local type annotation; seeds and budgets were not altered to obtain a passing result.

The current [world codec](simulators/opening-prestart-normalization-gate-20261026-v2/project/scripts/market/rival_world_checkpoint.gd) requires player/world/rival cycles to match, and the [Studio codec](simulators/opening-prestart-normalization-gate-20261026-v2/project/scripts/persistence/studio_checkpoint.gd) requires finance last-cycle and journal provenance to match the run. The game calendar and month bills derive from completed cycles. A one-field or two-field rewind therefore cannot represent legally paid pre-start work. This does **not** prove that a versioned January bootstrap is impossible.

**Recommended design contract, still OPEN:** retain a source-pinned legal pre-start action tape and final prelude state; introduce an explicitly validated January opening-state conversion that carries actual cash/Fans/owned Features/project/hand/RNG while starting the live world and player at cycle zero. The conversion must account for pre-start dues and frozen project market targets without putting historical titles in active sales, Fans or saturation. Reject corrupted/mismatched tapes, preserve old saves and verify the first post-start legal hand plus cold Continue. A snapshot template may bound new-save CPU only if its source, seed/recipe, schema and validation are explicit. No adoption handoff or TODO queue is justified yet.
