# 2026-10-09 decision: remove the first-game balance gate

**Status: LOCKED scope change; G1b SUPERSEDED, not passed.** After selecting the [revised incumbent opening rule](2026-10-09-opening-rule-revision-decision-v1.md), the user said a formal first-game balance test is unnecessary. Human playtests have not produced a 10.0 first game, and the user directed this thread to work on incumbent/Feature design and AI/world rules instead. This later instruction supersedes the earlier requirement to prove that **every legal** player first release freezes at most 9.9 before AI Competitors can proceed.

The [current-source 10.0 witness](../../findings/ai-competitors-gates-v1/README.md) remains valid evidence of one constructed legal route. It does not describe ordinary human play, and the human playtest experience does not invalidate it. We therefore do not claim that 10.0 is impossible under current rules, that the old proof gate passed, or that a gameplay rebalance occurred. The intended player experience is that a perfect first release is extraordinarily unlikely in normal play; no universal Review ceiling or separate first-game balance task is authorized by this decision.

**Current opening gate:** G1a alone covers typed pre-1980 history, coherent stronger incumbent starting resources and actual legal first post-start releases under ordinary hands/scoring. It requires no guaranteed Review ordering against the player. The former G1b balance mechanism and universal proof are **REMOVED from the AI Competitors handoff**. G2–G5 remain OPEN. A future explicit player-balance request can revisit the 10.0 route separately without reopening the incumbent rule by default.

The user asked this design thread to advance the remaining work in two groups:

1. G1a incumbent setup and G2–G3 Feature planning/Store rules.
2. G4–G5 AI choices, entrants, news, save migration and measured performance.

This is a documentation-only ruling. It does not authorize gameplay implementation in this thread, turn historical playtest observations into a universal source claim, or decide any OPEN number, AI policy or CPU limit.
