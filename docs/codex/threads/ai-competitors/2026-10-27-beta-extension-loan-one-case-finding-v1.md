# Beta extension and legal loan — one-case headless finding, 2026-10-27

**COMPLETE bounded DESIGN evidence; no balance or gameplay adoption.** In one synthetic all-Features Adventure Studio, seven ordinary Beta hands raised Review from **6.8 to 9.0** by fixing the seven Bugs exposed in hands four and five. Five hands alone left Review at 6.8. A genuine one-time $1,500 recovery loan was accepted, but it **did not change the selected hands, Review or release cycle**; the matched no-loan Studio funded all seven hands without arrears. The loan trigger tested here is **rejected as an AI spending rule**. QA opportunity and prudent lending thresholds remain OPEN.

## Recommendation and remaining uncertainties

**Keep QA before Marketing and prefer funded Beta work when it can convert known Bugs into real Debug progress.** A useful policy must track **known Bugs created by Search** and assess the available Debug opportunity across later hands; merely extending to five hands can delay release without a Review gain. Do not hard-code seven hands from this synthetic route. The post-fifth observed value came from two specific legal deals, not a guaranteed schedule. A finite public-state QA opportunity rule and a broader rival-world comparison are needed, including publication count and player sales at the same calendar boundary.

**Do not auto-borrow at `cash < 3 rents`.** This test proves a real recovery loan can be accepted and serviced once, but its no-loan counterpart finished the same seven-hand project. Before adopting loans, require a quote and a cash/debt runway comparison showing that borrowing enables a valuable legal hand or protects required Scope/operating obligations. Ordinary first-project eligibility, active debt, recovery use, later repayment, cross-Genre quality, richer cohort CPU/save and market challenge remain **OPEN / NOT RUN**. No implementation handoff or TODO entry follows this one-case finding.
