# First-release provisional standards — bounded headless finding, 2026-10-26

**Finding: a uniform 25% reduction is not a viable 6.0–7.0 opening rule.** The [predeclared fresh-Studio screen](2026-10-26-first-release-bootstrap-screen-plan-v1.md) ran 16 legal first releases per arm across all eight Genres and two seeds. The lower targets increased mean Review from **1.49 to 2.06**, but **all 16** first games remained below played Scope and 6.0. The [capable-rival follow-up](2026-10-26-first-release-bootstrap-screen-plan-v2.md) used an older private 35-Scope, whole-supply/full-Scope two-rival policy: under current targets, first Reviews were Arcade **6.2/6.6** and Wayfinder **4.1/4.6**; at 75% targets they became Arcade **8.0/8.3** and Wayfinder **5.2/5.9**. No first game in that B arm landed in 6.0–7.0. The desired founding range cannot be obtained by this blanket target change alone.

## Screen 1: eight legal fresh Studios, limited founding supply

Each arm used the same 16 declared cases: one Studio per Genre for seed4417 and seed4423; no traits/extras, `features_first`, two Design, two Alpha and two Beta hands, Fantasy, 50-command cap and one-release target. All **32/32** cases completed at command18/cycle7; A/B initial encoded state and RNG matched in **16/16** pairs. Played Core, Scope, Bugs, cash, commands and release cycle matched in **16/16**; only the provisional scoring targets and consequent Review changed. The lower profile yielded Reviews **1.2–2.6**, versus **0.8–1.9** with the current table. **16/16** in both arms released below required Scope30, so this finite policy is unsuitable for the LOCKED rival full-Scope rule. The complete [row-level output](sims/first-release-bootstrap-analysis-20261026-v1.json) preserves every Genre, seed, Core, standard, Scope and Bug count.

The 75% treatment was computed by round-half-up in all 32 target cells, for example Action `[40,26,40,26]→[30,20,30,20]` and Adventure `[33,26,20,53]→[25,20,15,40]`. This case demonstrates that easier standards do not compensate for inadequate played Scope/Core. It does not demonstrate 1977 AI performance: these are independent player Studio routes under a fixed test policy, not eight coexisting rivals or a generated historical career.
