## Recommendation and remaining gate

The two cheap purchases are legal and sometimes help, but **price-first acquisition cannot substitute for a four-Core project capability plan**. Reversing price priority to printed Core gain also failed. S shows Genre fit can materially help Wayfinder (+1.1 in seed4417 and an 8.1 in seed4431), yet its forecast is rough, it can choose a non-specialty Genre, and it did not produce an 8.0 by the c36 18-month checkpoint. Keep P's one-strong-Feature/Search-receipt mechanism as the stronger bounded two-year reference, not an approved final policy.
