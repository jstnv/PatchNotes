# AI Competitors design-review rulings — 2026-10-11

**Authority:** The user directed this design thread to take its recommended choices for every design-review prompt and log the alternatives. These are design rulings, not a request to copy private simulator code into canonical gameplay. The October 9 competitor structure and the later opening-rule revision remain in force. Findings supply evidence; they do not approve numerical balance.

## G1a: frozen incumbent archive representation — LOCKED qualitative

Use a typed pre-1980 `historical_titles` collection in each incumbent's persistent studio state, separate from ordinary live `released_games` and release metadata. Each row has stable identity, title, Genre, Theme, historical year and frozen Review. Keep an explicit January 1980 `opening_snapshot` for that studio's authored opening cash, Fans, completed Feature ownership and applicable familiarity/provenance. Archive titles are visible history, but cannot generate post-start sales, Fans, finance transactions, high-Review news or a live release ordinal. The first on/after-January-1980 title remains an actual legal Design/Alpha/Beta/launch result. This selects the representation recommended by the [G1a candidate record](2026-10-11-g1a-archive-candidate-decision-v1.md) under the already [LOCKED frozen-history choice](2026-10-09-incumbent-history-decision-v1.md). The [corrected private packet](../../findings/overnight-20261011-v1/G1A-RESULT.md) supports ID validation and Studio persistence, but is not adopted source.

**Alternatives:** putting historical rows in the live release ledger invents cycle-zero production and post-start accounting; keeping only an external sidecar would not make Studio Continue prove persistence; continuing old-game sales conflicts with the frozen-history lock. Those remain rejected for the first slice. The cohort count, identities, titles/Reviews, traits, cash/Fans, owned Features, familiarity, old-save migration, visible UI and shared-world persistence remain **OPEN**. No tested $5,850/20-Fan fixture value is approved.

## G2/G3: fixed-plan Store liquidity boundary — LOCKED qualitative

At one Studio shopping decision, freeze one legal hypothetical next-project hand envelope, owned-pool baseline and exact-cent development allowance `B` across all candidates. If `B` is used to justify a Feature's Core potential, elect a new Store admission only when the rival's **currently spendable exact-cent cash** covers the node's current conditional cost through completed ownership, known obligations through its planned Research completion, **and that same `B`**. The down payment is part of the completed quote and counts once. A queued node still supplies no Feature; actual installments are requoted and paid under ordinary legality. This extends the previously [LOCKED Research-window admission rule](2026-10-09-admission-liquidity-decision-v1.md) with the fixed-plan consistency selected in [F1/F3](2026-10-11-g2-held-plan-candidate-decision-v1.md).

This is a decision-time AI spending screen, **not escrow or a promise that the next project can finish**. Do not add every expected rent bill through the whole project to this hard admission gate. Count no forecast sales or optional future financing. After Research and at each later legal action/phase decision, use actual settled cash and the [LOCKED finite-project legal-exit rule](2026-10-09-finite-project-policy-decision-v1.md); the rival may defer, recover, exit or launch only when ordinary legality permits. Exact `B`, Design/Alpha hands, phase actions/exit thresholds and later cash policy remain **OPEN**. The [two-window private finding](2026-10-11-g2-reserve-window-finding-v1.md) demonstrates both the full-window stall and the Research-window later-cash risk; its 60,000/100,000-cent and 1/1 controls are not gameplay values.

**Alternatives:** the full eight-cycle no-sales hard reserve rejected all tested qualifying purchases and is not selected for the first slice; down-payment-only or quote-only admission can justify a Feature with an already unaffordable held plan; shrinking `B` per candidate breaks one Store-screen comparison; pre-crediting unearned sales invents spendable cash. The [review brief](2026-10-11-g2-reserve-window-review-decision-v1.md) retains the measured tradeoffs.

## G3: known-liability accounting contract — LOCKED scope / OPEN computation

The Research-window screen must use one typed due snapshot for every liability that exists in a supported rival Studio: already-unpaid obligations, scheduled rent, payroll, Bank and education installments whose amount and due cycle are known at the decision. Count an already-issued unpaid bill once, rather than adding its old schedule again. Exclude hypothetical new borrowing, hires, sales, familiarity or discounts that have not occurred. If the rival's current state has a liability the planner cannot account for, defer that new admission instead of treating its due as zero. Actual action preflights and settlement remain authoritative. The precise projection algorithm, transaction ordering, changing bill state, multi-action Research windows and supported optional-system coverage remain **OPEN** and need current-source tests. The [source audit and rent-only prototype](2026-10-11-g2-reserve-window-finding-v1.md) do not prove this broader calculation.

**Alternative:** multiplying today's monthly expense display across the window and ignoring scheduled loan/education dates is not selected; it can miss due installments or double-count overdue bills. No new player reserve or finance rule is created.
