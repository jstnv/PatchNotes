For the owned, project-eligible Feature pool before a candidate and the hypothetical pool after that candidate's completed legal ownership, represent potential with a **jointly legal one-project Core mix having the smallest combined equal proportional specialty gap**. Do not choose a mix primarily because it maximizes the currently targeted Core while a better-overall legal mix exists. When the targeted Core is already established and several mixes have equal best overall fit, favor the one with the smaller remaining gap in that targeted Core. Use the same overall-first priority on both sides of the comparison.

This selected-mix comparison remains subject to the existing candidate rules: the targeted positive gap must strictly improve, and if another positive gap worsens, overall four-Core fit must strictly improve. A candidate need not qualify merely because some nonselected legal mix improves the target; the representative before/after mixes govern the screen.
