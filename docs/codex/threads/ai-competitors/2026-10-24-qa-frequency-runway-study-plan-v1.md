They get no extra cash, Review, cycles, hidden-card knowledge or later Store purchase. Their identities, grants, archives and Genre/Theme are provisional study inputs, not entrant approval.

All arms use one private variant descended from forced-Feature v13; only its `project/scripts/market/rival_trial.gd` changes.
