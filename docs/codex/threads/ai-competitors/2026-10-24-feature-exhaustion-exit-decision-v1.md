# 2026-10-24 decision: play available rival Features before phase exit

**2026-10-25 supersession:** The later [full-Scope release decision](2026-10-25-full-scope-qa-marketing-decision-v1.md) retains the requirement to play every legal, affordable visible Feature but withdraws the rival's ordinary **under-Scope Alpha exit/launch** fallback. This record's under-Scope permission below is historical, not current design authority; canonical runtime has not yet adopted the new rule.

**Status: LOCKED qualitative rival behavior; NOT IMPLEMENTED.** Authority: the user's direct 2026-10-24 instruction: “Rivals are required to play every possible feature available in the hand. If there’s no feature in the draw pool, the AI can proceed.” This narrows the [finite legal project-plan decision](2026-10-09-finite-project-policy-decision-v1.md). It supersedes the current trial policy's fixed two-hand exit as a sufficient stopping condition, and supersedes the proposed Scope-aware exit wherever that proposal would leave a playable Feature in the current deal.

In **Design and Alpha**, before requesting an ordinary phase exit, the rival must consume every Feature instance that it can legally and affordably play from its **current visible candidate pool**. A productive hand still contains exactly four current candidate instances and resolves through the ordinary player command. Select as many playable Features as can jointly fit a legal, affordable hand; after a successful hand and refill, re-evaluate the new visible pool. Continue until no current visible Feature can be included in any legal, affordable hand. A fixed hand count, a reached Scope target or a favorable Review projection does not authorize leaving one behind.

When the current pool contains no Feature, the AI **may proceed** through the ordinary legal phase-exit path. A visible Feature that cannot be included in any legal, affordable hand is not “possible” at that state; the AI may take a legal exit or explicit blocked-state fallback and must record why it could not play the card. Undrawn Feature supply is not privileged information for the AI, and this ruling does not require Pass-only hands or repeated free redraws to search for hidden Features. An exit that is illegal still needs an ordinary legal fallback. Confirmed under-Scope exit and launch remain legal when otherwise allowed; this ruling does not award Scope, Core, QA, cash, cycles or a Review.

The rule applies to the rival's finite **Feature** supply in Design/Alpha. Beta's QA/Marketing/Insider cards and its stopping policy remain separate. A player productive cycle still offers at most one rival productive action; zero-cycle transitions remain bounded. The [scope-exit audit](2026-10-24-scope-aware-exit-finding-v1.md) establishes the current mismatch, not the performance or balance of this new rule.

**OPEN:** tie-breaking and synergy/Review ranking among equally Feature-maximal legal hands; the cash reserve used by rival hand choice; whether and how optional redraws are used when no Feature is visible; interaction with the user's at-least-ten-cycle quality target when supply ends early; blocked-state recovery, Beta allowance/exit, later Store supply, Genre/Theme, entrants, publication density and CPU/save budgets. The new stopping gate requires legal-route and market-challenge testing before an implementation handoff. No market coefficient or player hand rule changes here.
