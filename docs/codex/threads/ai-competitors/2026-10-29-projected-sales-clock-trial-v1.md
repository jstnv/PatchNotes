**Status: TRIAL private design, not approved gameplay.** The [v25 failure](sims/one-action-5607-post-release-prepare-20261029-v25/post-release-run.json) reproduces a rejected first post-release rival month. The [v26 diagnostic](sims/one-action-5607-post-release-diagnostic-20261029-v26/post-release.json) isolates it: at owner c78 targeting c79, cash is $42,408.54; shared Market snapshot c6, its factor and player preview are valid; the new sales record is valid, but `ReleasedGameSales.next_cycle` returns empty, so aggregate sales and the full productive preflight also fail. Source inspection pins the failed invariant to `ReleasedGameSales._validate_record`: it expects the factor `cycle_start` from physical `last_processed_run_cycle` (c78), while the shared world's factor is c6. The owner/world offset is +72. This is a calendar-provenance issue, not an affordability or Studio policy issue.

**Chosen trial approach:** give only post-start sales records of a projected rival an explicit immutable `market_clock_offset=72`; normal and frozen historical sales retain offset zero/absent. Validate each Market factor against `(physical first earning cycle − offset)` at the ordinary two-cycle month boundary. The owner Studio checkpoint must prove that the field is 72 exactly for live releases after its typed historical close and absent for old/ordinary releases. The world checkpoint continues to validate the mapped live release and its owner. This keeps one shared world Market snapshot and leaves the owner Studio's physical career clock intact.

**Alternatives logged:** copying a separate physical-c78 Market snapshot into the rival ledger would give player and rival different Market identities for the same month; exempting the factor-cycle check would lose provenance. Neither is selected for this trial. More general offsets, migration of old saves and multiple 1977 starters remain OPEN.
