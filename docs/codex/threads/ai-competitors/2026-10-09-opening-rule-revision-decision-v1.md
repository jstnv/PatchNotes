## Revised opening rule

- January 1980 has a small incumbent cohort with authored pre-run titles, separate resources and stronger Feature access. An incumbent's designated opening game remains its **first actual release on or after the run start**.
- Every post-start incumbent game must use ordinary legal Design, Alpha and Beta hands, project rules and Review scoring. Prior history and stronger resources are the opening advantage. There is **no universal promise** that each incumbent opening Review exceeds every legal player first Review. An exceptional player first game may tie or outscore an incumbent.
- Do not grant a hidden incumbent Review bonus, roll a final rating independently of legal work, or change the shared ordinary Review cap to manufacture the advantage. Exact cohort assets, histories, hands, Review results and AI choices remain **OPEN**.

## Separate first-game target and current gap

- **Historical design target, later SUPERSEDED:** A legal player's **first released game** must not freeze at 10.0 Review. Under one-decimal Reviews, acceptance would have required a source-matched bound of at most **9.9** across all legal first-release routes, including Studio builds, traits, starter/Store access, project Genre, cards/hands, optional income, costs, timing and variance. The 14-cycle pacing reference is not a legal project cap.
- **Current source fails that target:** the [G1 investigation](../../findings/ai-competitors-gates-v1/README.md) reproduced a legal, solvent first Action release at frozen 10.0 through ordinary cards and scoring, in two fresh processes. This is a balance gap, not proof that the route is disallowed or a reason to falsify the source status.
- **OPEN mechanism:** Decide and verify the smallest coherent player-start, access, economy or scoring adjustment that removes every legal first-release 10.0 route without silently changing rival legality or already frozen releases. The current witness is a mandatory regression case, but eliminating it alone does not prove the universal target. A numerical incumbent Review floor is no longer required.
- This target does not authorize a hard-coded first-release Review clamp or any gameplay edit in this design thread. The separate implementation task must obtain the specific balance ruling before changing player rules and cannot claim first-game balance acceptance until the bound is proved.
