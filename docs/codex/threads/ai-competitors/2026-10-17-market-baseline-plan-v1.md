# Market challenge baseline audit plan — 2026-10-17

**Status:** read-only current-rule audit before any market formula. The [market direction](2026-10-17-market-pressure-direction-v1.md) selects qualitative structure; numerical influence remains OPEN.

**Hypothesis:** The current two trial rivals can produce legal releases, but those releases cannot change the existing fixed Genre Review target table or Genre/Theme sales calculations. This audit measures their real release Reviews, genres/themes and earned sales after a bounded 20-cycle world route. It does not claim that their current strength is adequate or compare causal market effects, because those effects do not exist yet.

**Cases and legal starts:** The retained `verify_rival_trial_activation.gd` route creates a fresh Action player Studio with no traits and seed `4417`, then two trial rivals from `RivalTrial.create_world`. The player takes exactly 20 legal productive idle cycles; each rival takes at most one legal productive opportunity per cycle under its current finite visible-card policy. The verifier includes Studio checkpoint, active Design/Beta Continue and frozen archive controls. As a source/import control, the existing `foundation-legal-action-4417` simulator case creates a separate legal player Studio, max 50 commands, one release, fixed 2/2/2 test policy. These are different legal starting states; their Review/sales values will not be compared as a matched policy pair.

**Measurements:** Exact branch/HEAD, dirty-source identity, Godot hash, world cycle count, rival live release IDs, Genre, Theme, frozen Review tenths, cumulative earned units, cash/Fans, full-state Continue checks, process exit and warnings. A private audit-only copy of the verifier prints these records without changing any rule or player save. Source inspection separately confirms whether Review and sales calculators consume world history/Theme; simulator output alone cannot prove a negative across all source paths.

**Bounds and stopping:** One private variant from current canonical source; edit only its `project/scripts/debug/verify_rival_trial_activation.gd` telemetry. One `simulate.py run --variant` into a unique folder with a 120-second process bound; one focused verifier into another unique folder, port `47752`, 120-second bound. Stop on source drift, import failure, incomplete 20-cycle route, missing release, invalid Continue, timeout or verifier failure; retain the failed output. No seed, hands, command bound or output is replaced silently. If successful, do one representative fresh-process replay of the player source/import control (not the rival world) and report its narrower meaning. Do not adopt market balance values from this baseline.
