## Question

Can existing post-start Feature acquisition become a paid, persistent FIFO research queue while preserving exact-cent finance, normal productive-cycle settlement, Store prerequisites, starter behavior and Studio checkpoint safety? The queue should separate admission from completion and charge familiarity-discounted installments only when Research succeeds. Do not claim this change prevents rapid consecutive research without behavior evidence; live nodes still require one research cycle.

## Approved first-version rules

1. **Admission:** The player may queue a currently unlocked, unowned, not-already-queued Feature. Admission charges half its **base** price as an irreversible down payment and costs zero run/calendar cycles. Reject insufficient cash, duplicate, stale or locked admissions atomically. A queued parent does not satisfy child ownership; distinct Gameplay-owned count gates remain distinct. Multiple distinct eligible entries may wait in FIFO order; only the head receives research. No cancel/reorder control in this version. Show the payment and lack of cancellation before confirmation.
2. **Installments:** Divide the remaining half of the base price into the Feature's required successful Research actions. Recompute the existing parent-familiarity discount for each installment at action time; previously paid down payment/installments never change. Use integer cents: down payment receives an indivisible half-cent, each of the first `N−1` nominal installments receives `floor(remaining/N)`, and the final nominal installment receives the remainder; round a discounted installment down to a cent. With no discount, all payments sum to the base price exactly. An unaffordable current installment or failed normal cycle preflight rejects the Research action before cash, queue progress, time, sales, bills, redraws or RNG change. Same-cycle earned/unsettled revenue cannot fund the installment.
3. **Time and completion:** One successful Research button press charges its current installment and advances one normal productive run/calendar cycle through the central transaction, including portfolio sales/settlement, rent, payroll/Bank due bills, credit and redraw effects. Current live later Store and post-start Primitive reserve Features each need **one** such action for now; the user's $1,500/two-action example is arithmetic for a possible future Feature, not a live duration change. Only final successful research completion grants permanent ownership, child unlock and future-project Feature supply. Queue membership alone grants none of these or a draw/play guarantee. A paused queue item creates no automatic due bill.
4. **Starter and employee boundaries:** Required Genre-specialty grants and first-project optional zero-cycle Primitive purchases stay instant and unchanged. No employee-driven research in this slice. Future default research assignment and outside-Store progress remain a separately deferred Employees design, including how an unaffordable automatic installment behaves. No new cards, prices, later-card play fees, era gates, reserve threshold or lifespan changes.
