## Interpretation and next design question

**Recommendation, not approval:** do not buy either candidate automatically at the first legal Store visit based on these routes. Keep Sub-Areas as a bounded first-node design candidate and Background Music as a separate Audio candidate; neither center price nor fee is approved. The next useful comparison is the already handed-off Task 2 study: immediate purchase versus waiting for the whole missing-parent-plus-child chain plus rent/production buffer versus buying after a predeclared stronger release and actual settlement. Preserve true $5,500 and $5,700 starts as separate cohorts, with existing-node controls and matched calendar checkpoints.

Limits: one seed, three specialties, one early first-game budget, current trait start only, zero candidate play fee, immediate buying only. A purchase changes cycles, rent boundaries, sales settlement, hand composition, and later RNG consumption; Game 2 Review differences are whole-route associations, not isolated card effects. Several purchase routes are censored at two releases, so their terminal cash and Review cannot be compared as Game 4 outcomes. The process-local shadow registration and temporary UI-step repair were used only in this analysis driver. `run-report.json` records one incidental ignored `analysis/__pycache__/task22_prepare_v1.cpython-312.pyc` appearance during concurrent repository activity; **no tracked scripts, scenes, data, or analysis source changed** in the before/after manifest.
