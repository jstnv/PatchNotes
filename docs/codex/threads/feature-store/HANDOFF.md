The [current-source 18-route screen](FINDINGS-v4-immediate-purchase-screen.md) found 11/12 immediate candidate-purchase arms financially blocked after Game 2 from genuine $5,700 starts, while all six no-purchase arms reached Game 4. It supports testing timing and a full-chain cash buffer, **not** a price/fee ruling. The shared Task 2 read-only timing brief was handed off by the Feature Pools thread and promoted to the internal TODO at the user's separate request; this thread's gameplay design rows remain **proposed**.

The [21-route timing pilot](FINDINGS-v5-timing-pilot.md) adds a narrower result: four Action routes bought after a stronger release's **actual settlement**, then supplied/drew/played their candidate in Game 4 without arrears. The full-next-project reserve arm made no candidate purchase, and two Adventure settled-stronger arms did not meet the Review gate. Treat the observed threshold and survival as **candidate evidence**, not approved behavior or completion of the shared Task 2 handoff. Before a design ruling, extend to more seeds, genuine $5,500 starts, parent-missing cases, live-node controls, Game 4 settlement and a subsequent project; compare price/fee sensitivities separately.

The focused [five-release payoff follow-through](FINDINGS-v6-sub-areas-payoff.md) adds two Action seeds with Sub-Areas bought after Game 3 and played in Games 4/5. All four trial routes stayed solvent, but one had worse Reviews and $979.68 less cash by Game 5 launch; only one had more cash at the shared cycle-73 checkpoint. Use its **proposed** solvency, realized-sales and cash-payback scorecard in the remaining Task 2 evidence. Do not infer a price or play-fee approval from three positive milestone paths.

The [$0/$50/$90 fee overlay](FINDINGS-v7-sub-areas-fee-sensitivity.md) uses those same four fixed candidate routes. A $90 charge per actual play leaves the paired cash signs unchanged and keeps a conservative observed cash floor above $4,562, but it is **not a native paid-play replay** and does not approve $90. For Task 2, distinguish simple accounting sensitivity from a route that checks the fee atomically and may change play decisions. The [bounded-pilot evaluation standard](DECISION-v1-optional-upgrade-standard.md) is now **approved for design assessment only**.

The [24-route parent comparison](FINDINGS-v8-sub-areas-parent-rule.md) led to a delegated [bounded-pilot access ruling](DECISION-v2-sub-areas-access.md): Sub-Areas is an independent Store-only node, with no Levels prerequisite or parent discount and no Contract supply. Only one of four Strategy shopping points could afford the $2,150 parent chain at the trial values, while three could afford the independent $1,700 child. One independent buyer still blocked, so this does **not** approve the $1,700 quote or fixed purchase timing. Keep the broader Task 2 full-chain reserve/deferred comparison distinct.

The focused [post-Game-4 deferral probe](FINDINGS-v9-sub-areas-one-release-deferral.md) kept the independent $1,700 card and replayed four Strategy routes. Two bought and played in Game 5; one ended with $145.87 unpaid rent, while two other routes could not afford the card. This argues for Task 2's explicit next-project/rent reserve comparison rather than approving a fixed purchase window. Game 5 sales had not settled, and the candidate later-card fee remained $0.

At the user's request, this design thread ran a focused [48-route Strategy reserve screen](FINDINGS-v10-sub-areas-reserve-screen.md) across three seeds, two policies and genuine $5,500/$5,700 starts. Quote-only post-Game-3 buying produced six buyers but three added financial failures; a $1,000 post-quote floor produced only one distinct seed/policy buyer across the two ledgers, while $5,000 produced no buyers. This narrows the next question to native lower-price/access sensitivity without approving a reserve. It does **not** complete all cross-specialty/live-node/Background Music or post-Game-5 sales elements of queued Task 2; coordinate before duplicating those remaining elements.

The subsequent [48-route native price sensitivity](FINDINGS-v11-sub-areas-price-sensitivity.md) compared $1,200/$1,450 with $500/$1,000 post-quote floors on identical gameplay-source hashes. $1,200/$1,000 gained one clean current-flow buyer over the $1,700/$1,000 control; $500-floor arms bought more often but caused earlier blocks/extra rent arrears. The user asked to **hold** this research arm on 2026-10-06. It remains **OPEN**, not approved or handed off. Shared [Task 2](../../TODO.md) is now **IN PROGRESS** and already covers behavior-faithful paid-play and post-Game-5 settlement; avoid duplicating that study here.

**Next design question:** work through [era-unlock semantics](NEXT-DECISION-v2-era-unlocks.md) while Task 2 gathers economics evidence. Return to the [price/availability brief](NEXT-DECISION-v1-price-availability.md) after Task 2 results and explicit user review. No numerical rule follows from this thread's bounded pilots.
