## Why this is next

The [parent comparison](FINDINGS-v8-sub-areas-parent-rule.md) removed an unnecessary prerequisite but did not make the historical $1,700 study quote safe: a Strategy route purchased the independent card after Game 3 with $104.71 left, then stopped after Game 4 with arrears. Earlier [immediate-purchase evidence](FINDINGS-v4-immediate-purchase-screen.md) also found frequent early financial blocks. The [$0/$50/$90 accounting overlay](FINDINGS-v7-sub-areas-fee-sensitivity.md) covered cash-rich Action routes and cannot verify native paid-play behavior near a cash boundary. Availability, **when it can be bought**, and **whether a purchase is prudent** are distinct questions.

Follow-up evidence: a [four-route native Strategy deferral probe](FINDINGS-v9-sub-areas-one-release-deferral.md) bought after Game 4 in two routes at $1,700, but one of those finished with $145.87 unpaid rent; the other two could not afford the card. A fixed one-release delay alone does not pass the approved solvency/access evaluation. The next broader comparison should test an explicit project/rent reserve before selecting price or unlock.

The [48-route Strategy reserve screen](FINDINGS-v10-sub-areas-reserve-screen.md) now supplies a bounded answer: at the $1,700 trial quote, a $1,000 post-quote floor avoided added failures but reached only one distinct seed/policy buyer; $5,000 reached none. It tested genuine $5,500/$5,700 starts but remains narrower than the cross-specialty/live-node Task 2 scope. A narrow native lower-price sensitivity is the next design-thread experiment if no broader Task 2 result supersedes it; all price and reserve values remain OPEN.

The [lower-price sensitivity](FINDINGS-v11-sub-areas-price-sensitivity.md) is now complete at its narrow scope. The leading research arm is $1,200 with a $1,000 post-quote floor: one additional clean current-flow buyer over $1,700/$1,000, no added block among the 12 paired routes, but cash remains below control by Game 5 launch and the proposed paid-play fee is absent. The $500-floor arms produced earlier blocks. Next verify Game 5 actual settlement and behavior-faithful fee before any numerical ruling; this supersedes the previous paragraph's prospective price-run step.
