# Sub-Areas bounded-pilot evaluation standard

Date: 2026-10-06 (America/Los_Angeles). **ACTIVE/TRIAL design evaluation rule**, approved by the user's “Alright sure thing” in reply to the recommendation following [findings v7](FINDINGS-v7-sub-areas-fee-sensitivity.md). Recorded in the [Feature Store design authority](../../design/feature-store-progression.md). This agreement approves the *way to judge the bounded pilot*, not a content rollout or numerical value.

1. **Required access and solvency:** a candidate purchase must be legal, enter the next-project supply, and be drawn and committed at least once within two subsequent projects in the evaluated route. Compare with a paired no-purchase route and reject a pilot claim if it creates extra arrears or an earlier financial block. Report misses due to draw variance instead of implying guaranteed card access.
2. **Separate player benefits:** report Review/Scope and actual per-title settled net sales. A higher Review alone is insufficient to claim economic return. Compare sales at equal title age and a matched calendar checkpoint.
3. **Cash and risk:** compare net cash at matched calendar checkpoints and the same release-count milestone, including purchase/parent chain, any play fee, extra rent and timing. Positive payback by Game 5 is a target over a broader paired sample, **not a per-seed requirement**. Report adverse outcomes and their magnitude; do not erase a matched-calendar deficit with a later milestone surplus.

Still **OPEN**: a numerical success-rate/downside threshold, base price ($1,700 analysis center), later-card fee ($90 analysis analogue), purchase timing rule, final Levels parent/prerequisite semantics, first-era availability, UI, Contract-pool eligibility and implementation. [Findings v6](FINDINGS-v6-sub-areas-payoff.md) used two seeds; v7 used a fixed-route accounting overlay, not native paid-play behavior. Neither is enough to approve those open values.
