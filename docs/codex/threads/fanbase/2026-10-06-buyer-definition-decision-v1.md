# Fanbase audience definition decision v1

2026-10-06, America/Los_Angeles. Source: the user's direct response, “I think thats a good idea,” to the proposal that Fans represent an estimated audience count and the Fanbase branch's conservative buyer proxy remain a trial.

## Ruling

- **LOCKED definition:** The studio's Fans count is an estimated audience measure derived from sales and the Fanbase rules. It does not claim to count distinct identified people. Copies sold do not reveal whether purchasers are existing fans, repeat buyers, or buyers of another studio title.
- **ACTIVE/TRIAL method:** For the demo comparison, retain the `bd450a9` branch's conservative per-title eligible-unit proxy: cumulative earned units reserve up to the fan count snapshotted at that title's launch before units can recruit new fans. This is a working model to test, not a final conversion or returning-buyer policy.
- **OPEN:** Exact conversion and loss rates, whether the full launch-fan reserve is the best estimate, buyer overlap across concurrent releases, rounding/feedback balance, and player-facing wording. The current branch's 8%/15% and fan-to-Awareness constants remain provisional. A legal route capture can validate arithmetic and visible behavior; it cannot establish actual unique-buyer shares.

This decision does not authorize new gameplay work, a distinct-buyer ledger, a TODO entry, or a numerical lock.
