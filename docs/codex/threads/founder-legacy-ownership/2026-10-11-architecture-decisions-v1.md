# Founder Legacy & Corporate Ownership — selected architecture

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Authority:** the user delegated the architecture choices to this design thread. **Status:** SELECTED DESIGN, unimplemented. This resolves the architecture questions in [SPEC-v1 §11](SPEC-v1.md#11-approvals-required); [SPEC-v2](SPEC-v2.md) is the consolidated current contract. It does not approve untested balance coefficients, canonical AI policy, a TODO dispatch or production changes.

| ID | Selected decision and reason | Rejected or deferred alternative |
|---|---|---|
| A1 — identity | `WorldState` owns the calendar and an immutable `FounderProfile`; each legal Studio owns a separate gameplay/finance/AI state. One founder-selected operator Studio may use manual seven-card play. Ownership and management are different fields. This preserves Studio resource boundaries and the existing command resolver. | Moving Studio cash/IP into the founder, or giving every subsidiary a playable team. |
| A2 — founder consideration | A founder's share-sale or dividend consideration is paid **outside the simulated Studio economy**. Record exact price, shares, payer and an `external_owner_settlement` event, but store no personal balance, transferable token, formation credit or founder loan to a Studio. Career financing history/reputation may reflect the completed exit. A later venture must receive fresh loan/equity funding. This keeps equal economic terms without a hidden wallet. | The v1 formation-entitlement proposal, automatic transfer of old Studio money, or free startup cash. |
| A3 — opening ownership | Formation is a separate incorporation event, not a positive-pre-money primary round. Issue 1,000,000 equal units. A bank-only funded new Studio begins 100% founder-owned; a normal outside-equity package begins with 600,000 founder / 400,000 pooled passive-investor units. A distressed backstop package may be majority investor-owned, with a disclosed founder CEO mandate. These allocations are **first-slice trial economics**, not final balance acceptance. All cash is an actual subscribed equity receipt or a named loan. | Treating founder reputation as a spendable or booked asset; dividing by zero to price formation shares. |
| A4 — cap table and control | Every Studio's issued units equal all registered holder units. Each unit has one vote and one equal economic right. Only a **single direct holder** with `2 × units > issued_units` controls that entity; 50% and informal coalitions do not. A controlling parent directs its corporate votes in a child, so control can cascade operationally through acyclic parents, but the founder's indirect economic share is not mislabeled as direct ownership. | Voting agreements, coalitions, preferred stock, retail traders, or multiplying indirect percentages into a second set of child votes. |
| A5 — transfers | Primary issuance deposits cash in the target and dilutes existing holders using a positive pre-money quote and checked integer-unit rounding. Secondary sales change holder units and settle price from buyer to seller; the target's cash and property do not move. Full acquisition is 100% of issued units through one or more valid deals. Founder sells only by explicit player acceptance. | A scripted takeover that changes control without a paid share transaction, or a hostile target-controlled primary issue. |
| A6 — zero value | If an established Studio has zero equity value, ordinary formula-priced primary issuance is unavailable. A separately negotiated **positive-cent** restructuring would need its own creditor/shareholder contract later; the first slice may decline it. Existing debts never vanish on acquisition. | Free controlling shares, negative prices, automatic debt forgiveness or a zero-denominator quote. |
| A7 — valuation | Value **external non-IP operating earnings** separately from owned IP. Normalize only completed-month external service/publishing operating margin; exclude release sales and IP licensing from that earnings base. Value existing release/IP rights once from projected future net cash flows using the current lifespan model and explicit discount, plus separately evidenced unlaunched rights. Add actual eligible cash and child share investments, then subtract outstanding debt principal, accrued unpaid interest and other unpaid liabilities once. No internal group commission is capitalized as external earnings. This clean split prevents release-profit double counting. | Capitalizing all game sales in earnings and then adding the same games' full IP forecast. |
| A8 — banking | Preserve the ordinary Bank's two-positive-settled-month eligibility and one-active-loan rule. A distinct `startup_loan` quote may underwrite a newly formed Studio using founder reputation, verified previous credit events, era package and industry conditions; the Studio is borrower and owns its schedule. A startup loan occupies the one-active-loan slot until closed. A registered financing broker can combine it with a passive investor's formation subscription. | Waiving the ordinary loan gate for all Studios, putting old debts on the founder, or creating an unrecorded grant. |
| A9 — career continuity | Voluntary departure at a safe Studio boundary can enter a paused Founder Interlude or atomically start an already funded replacement Studio. Forced loss can enter the same Interlude. A minimum investor-backed formation offer is available once per founder-quarter; it grants cash to the new Studio for a controlling investor stake and time-limited CEO mandate, never cash to the founder. `seek_backing` advances a real world cycle without a manual Studio team. Repeat departures affect reputation/terms and Studios remain liable. | A free menu-time wait, abandoning a hand to escape bills, or a deadlocked mandatory takeover with no playable route. |
| A10 — CEO rights | A founder without majority may manually operate **one** Studio only under an explicit board mandate. The mandate reserves equity, financing, M&A and parent policy to the owner; the founder may play that Studio's ordinary legal development actions. Dismissal or resignation takes effect at the next safe Studio boundary, preserving any current hand/Contract. | Silent permanent manual control after takeover or mid-hand forced scene replacement. |
| A11 — world order | A committed player productive action creates one world tick. Each active AI Studio gets at most one legal productive opportunity in stable ID order, with bounded zero-cycle setup. Per-Studio earning/month closure/bills/credit then settle exactly once. A new release cannot earn on its launch boundary. Corporate quarter evaluation follows that settlement on cycles divisible by six; deals see settled cash then and cannot retroactively fund earlier actions. | Background-frame AI turns, menu-time progression, or projected sales funding a current action. |
| A12 — takeover policy | Corporate AI offers and transacts only at a six-cycle quarter. Filter acyclic eligible pairs and priced, funded sellers; inspect a deterministic bounded candidate set. Friendly issuance/transfer needs target/seller authorization. Hostile purchases can only take **willing existing holders' shares**, including pooled passive investors; they cannot compel founder shares. Crossing 25% and 40% hostile ownership and a credible control-crossing offer create staged warning events (trial thresholds). No warning changes votes. | Rolling a takeover from valuation score, automatic founder confiscation or hostile dilution by target issuance. |
| A13 — saves | The authoritative Continue envelope is a world snapshot at a quiescent founder Studio boundary **or committed Founder Interlude boundary**. It contains all Studio ledgers, AI sessions/RNG, cap tables, active operator/mandate, pending offers, quarterly cursor and delivered news. A pure converter may wrap only an exactly validated supported legacy Studio checkpoint, preserve the original bytes/backup, and record historical opening equity without adding cash. Other saves remain incompatible and preserved. | Rebuilding rivals from a seed on load, saving only the active Studio, or silently upgrading unverifiable historical saves. |
| A14 — migration and era | The old Studio keeps every asset, debt, employee, title, contract and subsidiary. Its existing run state becomes AI-operated. The founder retains only history/reputation/milestones/financing summaries. A new Studio receives the **currently legal** era catalog and specialty roster, with no gifted later Features, old Fans or old IP; its known starting costs are funded in the formation contract. One-shot biographical education selections cannot be reissued on every migration. | Copying company traits/assets or re-awarding current first-Studio financing at every founding. |
| A15 — subsidiaries | Each acquired Studio remains an autonomous legal entity with its own AI development session. Parent policy sets high-level priorities; a commission is a signed intercompany contract with mirrored expense/receipt and no card micromanagement. Game Development is first-slice; Publishing/Marketing, Research and Manufacturing/Distribution need later dedicated economic designs. | Consolidating subsidiaries into one player hand or allowing free cross-company cash sweeps. |
| A16 — performance and milestones | Corporate evaluation uses revision-keyed valuation caches, indexed candidate filters and fixed work-unit quotas, never wall-clock-dependent decisions. Benchmark real population sizes before setting numeric CPU/save caps. Milestone IDs persist on the founder and present Continue; no victory freezes the world. | Per-frame full pairwise scans, nondeterministic timeout choices, or victory ending the campaign. |

## Calibration still required

The 60/40 formation split, 25%/40% warning thresholds, distressed investor stake, quote premium, normalized-earnings window/multiple, IP discount, startup credit coefficients, minimum era budget, founder reputation effects, buyer candidate quota, population/CPU limits and specific milestone thresholds are **CANDIDATE or OPEN calibration**, not proven balance. The architecture fixes where each value belongs, who owns it and how to verify it. Do not take figures from AI Competitors' private 60,000/100,000-cent controls or G1a fixtures as values for this system.

No other thread's detailed G1–G5 AI policy, market Review effects, employee future wages or Store card economics is superseded here. Their existing OPEN values remain with those threads.
