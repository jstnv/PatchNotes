# Founder Legacy & Corporate Ownership: user direction

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Status:** LOCKED direction for this design thread; implementation absent. Source: the user's founding request in this chat. This record does not approve the detailed candidates in [SPEC-v1](SPEC-v1.md).

## Decisions supplied by the user

1. Follow one persistent founder identity across Studios. The founder can leave voluntarily or lose control through acquisition. Former player Studios persist under AI management. Only one development team is manually controlled with the seven-card system.
2. Money, credit, debt, employees, Features, IP, subsidiaries and contracts belong to their Studio. There is no personal wealth or personal bank account system. New ventures obtain financing through banking and, if equity is used, ownership transactions.
3. Start with equal voting and economic shares. The ledger supports founder, corporate and passive outside holders; primary issuance, secondary transfers, partial/majority/full acquisitions. Control requires **strictly more than 50%** of voting shares. No retail exchange or preferred classes.
4. Value Studios using normalized operating earnings, IP, cash and debt without counting the same earnings twice. Primary issuance must show pre-money, post-money and dilution correctly.
5. AI takeovers need actual ownership transactions, with warning events before hostile control. After loss of control the founder may remain CEO if permitted, negotiate an exit, or resign and establish a new Studio. No automatic transfer of company property or bank balance.
6. Departure occurs at safe boundaries. The old Studio keeps all its obligations and resources; the founder keeps career history, reputation, milestones and a lightweight financing history. New-venture terms depend on approved credit, reputation and industry conditions; financing is a loan or investor equity, not free cash. New Studios start with era-appropriate conditions.
7. Subsidiaries operate autonomously under optional parent policies/commissions, with later Game Development, Publishing/Marketing, Research and Manufacturing/Distribution specializations. No second manually playable development team.
8. Corporate AI evaluates every six game cycles using cached valuations and filtered candidates, separately from seven-card development decisions. Require seeded determinism, validated transactions, benchmarks and no circular corporate ownership in the first slice. Milestone victories do not end the campaign.
