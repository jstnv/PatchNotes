# Banking B3 input and payment implementation

2026-10-07. Prepared before gameplay edits under the user's overnight direction to use recommended answers and defer nonblocking questions. Implements the accepted selectable amount/term, two-settlement capacity and 1% scheduled opening-principal rules. This supplies explicit implementation mechanics; no historical $2,500 ceiling, $100 step or preset term menu is adopted.

- Amount is an exact cent integer of at least 50,000 cents. The UI accepts decimal dollars with at most two digits after the decimal; no floating-point conversion is used.
- Term is a positive whole number of months. Require at least one cent of scheduled principal in every installment (`term <= principal_cents`), and reject calendar or arithmetic overflow. No fixed term menu or arbitrary economic maximum is added.
- Use equal principal with any indivisible cents assigned to the earliest installments: `q = floor(P/N)`, `r = P mod N`; installment i repays `q+1` cents for i≤r, otherwise q. Scheduled opening principal is `P - q*(i-1) - min(i-1,r)`. Interest is 1% of that opening balance, rounded half-up to cents; no floating point or compounding on arrears. The first installment is the maximum. The full quote shows the declining schedule, principal/interest totals and dated dues before explicit acceptance.
- Derive the largest eligible principal from the actual conservative capacity and selected term. Use exact integer arithmetic, overflow checks and bounded-time formulas; keep large schedules compact and paginate the preview rather than allocating one object per future month.
- First due is even acceptance cycle+2 or odd cycle+3, recurring every two cycles. Loan proceeds are financing, interest is expensed in its original due month and principal only affects cash. Partial bill service pays due interest before principal; rent precedes bank service and future live payroll belongs between them.
- Early payoff is outstanding principal plus already-due unpaid interest. Future interest is waived. Quote identity includes the run and current finance revision; stale/duplicate acceptance or payoff rejects atomically. A paid-off loan preserves its history and permits a fresh quote only when current eligibility passes again.

Validation will compare independent cent schedules at short, long, minimum, maximum-capacity and overflow inputs, then native monthly finance/credit, partial recovery, phase-safe controls and versioned state mapping.
