When a voluntary education payoff is confirmed, first verify the approved cash test: cash covers the education payoff quote plus all already-due unpaid rent, payroll and Bank installments. In one atomic **zero-cycle** action, service those due rent, payroll and Bank balances first, then pay the education quote once. Include already-due education interest and principal in that payoff, marking their original education bills paid with truthful interest/principal allocation and payment history; apply any remaining payoff principal to the unscheduled future principal. Close the education account and end future scheduled bills. Do not leave reserved due bills unpaid or double-pay education arrears. Insufficient cash, stale or repeated confirmation leaves the pre-action state unchanged.

This elective action order is distinct from ordinary monthly settlement, which remains rent → payroll → Bank and education bills merged by original due date, Bank first on a same-date tie. Thus an older education arrear can precede a newer Bank bill at monthly settlement while a chosen full education payoff services the already-due Bank bill first. Paying now stops future lateness on cleared bills but does not erase prior late-credit history or award an immediate credit bonus.

For the illustrative $1,700 cash, $500 rent due, $200 Bank due and $1,000 education payoff quote, the confirmed action pays all $1,700 and leaves zero cash and no unpaid included bill. The numbers are an example of action ordering, not a gameplay price or affordability guarantee.
