## Matched effect through the second launch

Each pair began from byte-identical c16 Studio checkpoint/RNG. All eight arms finished game two at cycle 29; second Reviews were 5.2 (seed4417) and 4.6 (seed90210) in both arms. No action was financially blocked; arrears were $0 and credit matched within every pair (612/615 by seed). Graduate's first two education dues at c26/c28 were paid on time, totaling $565.46 ($299.79 principal + $265.67 interest) by c29. This is a matched finite-policy result, not a claim that a human facing an unaffordable choice would gain nothing from liquidity.
