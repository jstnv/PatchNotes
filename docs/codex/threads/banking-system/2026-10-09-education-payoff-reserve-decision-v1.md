# Education payoff cash reserve — decision v1

2026-10-09, America/Los_Angeles. **LOCKED future design; education debt remains inactive.** The user approved the proposed voluntary education-payoff affordability rule, including the case where an education arrear is older than a Bank installment.

At the instant of an explicit payoff, require exact-cent spendable cash to cover the education payoff quote **plus every already-due unpaid rent, payroll and Bank installment balance**. Already-due includes overdue bills. The education quote includes all remaining unpaid education principal and already-due unpaid education interest, so it is not reserved a second time. Do not reserve future bills that have not yet come due. An insufficient-cash payoff is rejected without changing finance state or advancing a cycle.

This elective-payoff reserve is deliberately stricter than automatic monthly service in one case: an older education arrear precedes a newer Bank installment in the monthly original-due order, but the voluntary full payoff still must leave cash for that already-due Bank installment. The approved automatic settlement order remains oldest rent, payroll, then Bank and education bills by original due date with Bank first on a same-date tie.

The existing approved payoff amount remains unpaid principal plus already-due unpaid interest, with no fee or future unaccrued interest; past bill and credit history remains.
