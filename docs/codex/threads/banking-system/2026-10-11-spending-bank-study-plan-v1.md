# Paid-Feature education/Bank study — predeclared 2026-10-11

## Question and hypothesis

Does a legal $1,700 Recorded Sounds acquisition after a roughly 13-cycle first release make College Dropout or Graduate's first due at cycle 26 constraining? At the post-second-release Studio boundary, can an actually eligible $500/12-month Bank loan improve the next release's liquidity or legal actions compared with no loan from the *same* saved state/RNG? These are current-rule balance questions, not a request to change LOCKED terms.

Hypothesis: paid Store spending reduces the cash cushion around the first education due, especially for Graduate. A Bank loan may improve immediate spendable cash but cost interest; it may be ineligible after weak monthly sales. Preserve whichever result occurs.

## Predeclared policies, cases and bounds

1. **Existing behavior first:** six independent new Action Studios (none, College Dropout, Graduate × seeds `4417`, `90210`) using `finite-development-fixture` delegation with four Design/four Alpha/four Beta hands per release, visible-order legal selection, Action/Fantasy, two-release target, 100-command bound, telemetry, 240-second timeout. At the first post-release Studio boundary, attempt the current `recorded_sounds` Store quote (Action owns the parent); if admitted, complete its one paid Research action. If quote is absent, unaffordable or rejected, retain that failure and stop the case. No Contract, hire, extra Store item, artificial wait, loan or manual cash adjustment. Seed4417 previously produced first Review4.0; seed90210 previously produced second Review4.3, but these are *prior-source expectations*, not fixture overrides.
2. Run those six cases once on the canonical source via the isolated `tools/simulate.py run`. Then create private Banking simulator `education-bank-quote-v1` with `tools/simulator_variant.py create`. Its only intended project delta is a read-only `bank_quote_500_12` field in the **Studio** observation after two releases, calling the existing public quote method. No finance, RNG, Store, save, card or gameplay rule change. Rerun all six legal prefixes from fresh recipes on this variant. Do not feed a canonical checkpoint into changed simulator rules. Check identical actions/results of canonical versus variant prefixes except the new observation field, and record source identities/diff.
3. For each completed variant **Graduate** two-release Studio checkpoint, create two continuation arms from the exact same checkpoint envelope/RNG: no loan versus request the observed current $500/12 quote immediately, then use the same legal development policy to target three total releases. If the quote is empty/ineligible, the loan arm makes one explicit rejected request and stops; retain that outcome. Do not replace it with another amount or seed. If both Graduate seeds are ineligible, the test reports that gate instead of a loan-benefit result. Compare first due/overlap reserve, exact loan receipt and installments, cash low/arrears/credit, first blocked action, Review, launch timing and debt-adjusted cash at common calendar/release boundaries. Each arm has 100-command bound, telemetry and 240-second timeout. No payoff is scripted.
4. Replay at least one completed paid-Feature prefix of each trait and any eligible loan/control pair in fresh processes. Retain rejected, incomplete or timed-out evidence. The paired reporter can be used only within the same variant/source and identical Studio starting envelope; cross-trait and canonical-versus-variant comparisons are separately labeled, never passed to it as a matched pair.

## Measurement and stopping rules

Report genuine trait-specific start cash, Store down/installment and ownership, first/second Review and settled-sales months, Bank quote basis/capacity/reserved education amount, first education due at c26 if reached, original bill interest/principal/payment/overdue age, cash low, credit, release cycle and final principal owed. Stop each prefix at two Studio releases; stop continuations at three releases, first rejected command, 100 commands or timeout. If the first due is not reached, state that the case cannot answer the due question. Do not silently expand seeds, budget, Feature list or term to obtain a quote.

No balance coefficient or strategy becomes approved from these routes. No computer control, canonical gameplay edit, player save access, commit or push. A design-review handoff may recommend more evidence or a ruling; a private quote-observation variant is not itself an adoption request.
