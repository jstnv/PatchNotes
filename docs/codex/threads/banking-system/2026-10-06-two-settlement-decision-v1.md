# Banking two-settlement design ruling

2026-10-06 (America/Los_Angeles). The user agreed to the [B2 recommendation](2026-10-06-b2-two-settlement-findings-v1.md) with “Alright Im with it” after the comparison and asked what comes next. **LOCKED design, not implemented:**

1. A first bank-loan quote requires two consecutive completed calendar months with positive actual settled portfolio sales. A zero-sales month breaks the streak. Earned but unsettled sales, partial-month values, publisher/Contract receipts and financing do not count.
2. Use the lower of the latest settled-sales month and the floor of the two-month average as the sales basis. Subtract $500 monthly rent and other live required monthly obligations, floor at zero, then reserve 75%; the remaining 25% is the maximum scheduled bank installment. Recompute for a fresh quote. No overdue bill or active bank loan may coexist with a new quote. Later sales decline does not rewrite an accepted schedule.
3. The cash HUD's Finances → Bank path may accept a loan and an early payoff during any active gameplay phase where Finances is accessible, including Design and Pre-Development. Navigation costs no cycle; acceptance and payoff must be explicit, atomic and keep the underlying phase input blocked while the modal is open.

One active loan at a time and future unaccrued interest waived on early payoff remain accepted. The earlier first-offer ceiling of $500 was the baseline during this ruling; the user subsequently [reopened the ceiling for larger-offer review](2026-10-06-larger-offer-findings-v1.md) because it is small against $500 monthly rent and progressing early-game revenue. **OPEN:** amount ceiling/menu, rate, term, installment split/rounding, first due convention, score pricing/eligibility, and exact payoff/delinquency presentation. The B1/B2 overlays establish only read-only affordability on their scripted routes. Loan issuance, typed bank installments, Bank actions and credit effects are not implemented. No task was added to the shared TODO.
