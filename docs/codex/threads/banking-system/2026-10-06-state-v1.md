## Accepted foundation and current behavior

- Two productive cycles make one month. Starting capital is $5,500; the separate four-unused-point trait receipt makes applicable new runs $5,700. Rent is $500 at each completed month. About $1,150 of first-game Feature play is an observed reference, not an automatic charge. A first release around cycle 14 or sooner is the pacing target; the reported 6.2-rated longer-route bankruptcy pressure is qualitative human evidence without a complete local action ledger.
- HUD Cash opens Studio Finances and then Bank. Finance reports distinguish earned net sales, settled cash, operating profit, financing, original rent due, and payment-month recovery. Typed bills retain source/type/original due/balance/payments/age; partial service retains age and late history. `OutstandingExpenses` starts credit at 600 and closes once per month. Current +3 clean-profit and typed age penalties are **ACTIVE/TRIAL** values. A paid loss or break-even month gets 0 absent late penalties.
- Rent is the only live bill producer. Bank installment, payroll, and Student Loan types are hooks. Loans are unavailable in the Bank UI and RunState. Accepted lending **direction** is post-first-actual-settlement, at most $500, one active loan, capacity-qualified, with future unaccrued interest waived on early payoff. Rate, term, first due, capacity formula, and score pricing remain **OPEN**.
