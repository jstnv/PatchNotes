## Recommendation

Keep the approved R5 and payout terms. Shorter production addresses part of the cash problem but does not yet establish Crown eligibility. Neither new policy wins across genres; do not adopt adaptive as a universally stronger strategy.

Next, refresh the private candidate onto the current catalog and test a staged, sales-funded career: affordable releases first, then targeted weak-Core/Scope upgrades before attempting Review7. This is a proposed research direction, not proof that sales will fund it. Preregister an explicit release/cycle/spending cap, preserve unsuccessful routes and measure time to discovery and then earned R5. Avoid further extending this old 48-search grind or treating its 1987 Game2 as normal early pacing. Actual cash/staffed settlement gates remain dependent on a genuine earned-R5 route.
