## Core relationship model

Each **Studio–publisher pair** owns two separate, monotone integers in `[0,10000]`, displayed as 0.00–100.00. A founder changing Studios does not carry either relationship to the new Studio. All events carry a publisher ID, Studio ID, source deal ID, terms version and immutable commit ID; an event applies once. Existing five publisher unlocks and current work-Contract cash/Promotion/challenge rewards remain unchanged.

| Track | Meaning | Gained by | First effect | Candidate status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reputation `R_p`** | This publisher's trust in this Studio as a contractor. | Explicit reputation-bearing work Briefs and delivery of commissioned games. | Opens game commissions at gates 20/50/80. | Shape recommended; scale/gates PROPOSED. |
| **Investment capacity `I_p`** | This publisher's willingness to commit resources to the Studio's future games. It is a rating, not cash or a spendable pool. | Delivery of commissioned games and a supported Studio game meeting both frozen Review and Month3 sales criteria. | Allows support at 15 and determines its committed budget. | Shape recommended; amounts PROPOSED. |

The older Bible term **Publisher Investment** meant one work Contract's reward budget. Retain that historical offer-budget meaning in old records. Name the new stored field `publisher_investment_capacity_bp` and each support deal's fixed amount `support_budget_cents`; do not reuse the old payout field or retroactively turn it into a relationship ledger. Reputation and investment capacity do not decay or decrease in the first pilot. Missed promises award zero new capacity; cash already committed is not clawed back.

## Three offer families and progression

1. **Work Contracts and Relationship Briefs.** Existing cash-first offers are preserved and grant no automatic or retroactive `R_p`/`I_p`. A new, explicitly reputation-bearing Brief can use a publisher's signature challenge and the existing two-hand Contract framework. The first candidate is the [SideStreet two-hand Brief](SIDESTREET-RELATIONSHIP-BRIEF-PROPOSAL-2026-10-18-v3.md), whose exact targets remain PROPOSED. The prior [release-paced cadence](PUBLISHER-REPUTATION-CADENCE-PROPOSAL-2026-10-18-v1.md) becomes this design's candidate schedule: at most one pending/active Brief per eligible publisher; a later successful Studio-developed game release is the next issuance chance; no instant refill or missed-release backlog. Ironclad's initial Contract stays challenge-free; a later Ironclad Brief may teach Reputation separately. Other publishers need legal promise definitions before activation.
2. **Commissioned game Contracts.** Once `R_p` reaches a tier gate, the Studio can quote a publisher-owned game at project planning. The publisher pays a fixed quoted full fee for full delivery, owns that title's IP and distribution revenue, and the Studio receives no sales royalties on it. The Studio retains its reusable Feature technology and receives developer credit/Fanbase from people playing the game. An accepted deal is bound to one project and its later release ID. It cannot be assigned retrospectively after seeing Review. **Candidate issuance:** one pending/active game commission per publisher; after a commission closes, its own launch does not immediately issue a replacement. The next later successful Studio-developed release is the next quote chance; no backlog. A missed commission follows the same pacing and never grants relationship points.
3. **Support for a Studio-owned game.** Once `I_p≥1500`, the Studio can quote a different deal at project planning. The Studio owns the title and its ongoing sales; the publisher commits upfront cash and targeted launch marketing, then recoups from a limited share of **that title's** post-platform sales. Meeting both frozen Review and cumulative unit targets by release-age Month3 increases `I_p` for *future* deals. It never changes the accepted game's budget or reopens its result.

Only one game deal can be bound to a project. A work Contract still follows the existing one-active-work-Contract guard; game-deal confirmation and `BeginProject` should be one atomic transaction in Studio. No mid-project deal acceptance, stacking multiple publishers on one title, ordinary game-deal expiration or retroactive support. The game-deal browser can recompute a fresh quote from current ledgers, while confirmation freezes all terms **against the pre-`BeginProject` Studio snapshot**. Starting a project spends a productive cycle and may mature an older title's Investment milestone; that same cycle must not improve or retroactively qualify the newly bound quote.

## Reputation formula and commission tiers — PROPOSED

For a new reputation-bearing Brief, freeze completion `C_bp` and promise grade `G_bp` in `[0,10000]`. At its one committed result:

`ΔR_p = floor(1000 × min(C_bp, G_bp) / 10000)`; `ΔI_p = 0`.

A full Brief gives 10 Reputation points, half gives 5 and a missed promise gives 0. This adopts the *shape* of the earlier [formula finding](PUBLISHER-REPUTATION-FORMULA-FINDINGS-2026-10-18-v1.md) with a new trial maximum; the finding did not approve 10 points. No Brief payout, existing challenge bonus or legacy completed result is changed by this recommendation.

| Commission tier | Minimum `R_p` | Quoted full fee | Review target | Full `R_p` and `I_p` gain |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 20.00 | $6,000 | 6.0 | 15.00 each |
| 2 | 50.00 | $8,000 | 7.0 | 20.00 each |
| 3 | 80.00 | $10,000 | 8.0 | 25.00 each |

The displayed fee is frozen cents, not a percentage of future sales. The accepted brief may also freeze an authored Genre, Theme, owned-Feature or era prerequisite, but an offer must be checked as legally producible before it appears. The **first SideStreet pilot** would use any currently legal Genre/Theme and no extra Store gate; later authored publisher-specific content is OPEN. The offered tier never increases mid-project when Reputation changes.

The accepted quote freezes a **positive `required_scope`** taken from the legally prepared project's `ProjectState.get_required_scope()` and binds it to the project/release ID. Reject zero/invalid denominators before acceptance. Later Scope changes never alter the denominator; a future authored minimum Scope per tier remains OPEN pending legal route evidence. At launch, compute exact Scope completion `S_bp=clamp(floor(10000×delivered_scope/required_scope),0,10000)` and the frozen one-decimal Review in tenths `r`. For tier Review target `t`, define `V_bp=clamp(500×(r−(t−20)),0,10000)` and `D_bp=min(S_bp,V_bp)`. On a publisher-owned commissioned release:

- `fee_paid_cents = floor(full_fee_cents × D_bp / 10000)` once at launch.
- `ΔR_p = ΔI_p = full_tier_award_units` once **only when `D_bp=10000`**, capped at 100.00 per ledger; otherwise both gains are zero. Salvage pays for partial work, but only full promised delivery earns new trust or investment capacity.
- Full Scope and Review at/above target pay the full flat quote. A miss receives the **prequoted underdelivery salvage** on this two-point Review band; it never changes sales ownership or creates royalties. At tier1, full Scope and Review5.0 pay $3,000 and **zero** relationship points; Review5.9 pays $5,700 and **zero** relationship points; Review≤4.0 pays zero. This exception to the full flat fee must be prominent before acceptance.
- An unlaunched/abandoned project has no committed release or payment event. Mid-project cancellation, if later added, requires an explicit policy; it is outside this first pilot.

The title counts as a Studio-developed release for Review/Fanbase, release-based publisher unlocks and ordinary release history. It **does not** count as a completed legacy work Contract for Starwave's three-Contract gate; neither do Relationship Briefs. Its observed units and raw post-platform net remain in the market/fan ledger, while **Studio sales entitlement, Studio earned/settled sales and Bank qualifying sales are zero**. The sales HUD and finance report must identify publisher-owned units separately from Studio earnings. Studio-paid post-launch campaigns are unavailable on publisher-owned titles in this pilot; the publisher has the marketing rights. Existing banked next-successful-launch Promotion still applies and is consumed at a commissioned launch, with a warning in the quote because the Studio receives no title sales. No current source implements these rights; implementation requires a typed release ownership path.

## Support quote, recoupment and criteria — PROPOSED

At `I_p≥1500`, display `i=floor(I_p/100)` whole Investment points. Freeze the support budget `B_cents=min(300000, 50000+2500×i)`: $875 at15, $1,750 at50, $3,000 at100. `I_p` is **not spent** on acceptance. This is an amount the publisher will offer per eligible project, not Studio cash until a deal commits. **Candidate issuance guard:** the first support quote requires `I_p≥1500`. After accepting one, that publisher's next support quote requires `I_p≥last_support_acceptance_I_bp+1000` and a later successful Studio-developed release; only one pending/active supported project per publisher and no backlog. Thus a missed target cannot be used to repeat the same nonrecourse package at unchanged capacity. At the 100.00 cap, further offers stop in this pilot; longer-term renewal is OPEN. Publisher personality splits that fixed budget into funding and marketing:

| Publisher | Cash share basis points | Review ≥ | Cumulative units after Month3 ≥ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SideStreet | 8,000 | 7.0 | 1,000 |
| Crown & Quill | 6,000 | 7.5 | 1,100 |
| Neon Circuit | 4,000 | 6.5 | 1,200 |
| Starwave | 5,000 | 8.0 | 1,350 |

**Ironclad's former 100% cash/6.5/900 row is REJECTED as a support offer**, while its introductory work Contract remains unchanged. With no Awareness difference, cash-only support would give `B−min(B,20%×N)≥0` extra Studio receipts at every checked sales level, earlier cash, and a possible +10 Investment with no countervailing cost. At zero sales it is an unrecovered grant. The [v1 economic screen](PUBLISHER-SYSTEM-ARITHMETIC-FINDINGS-2026-10-18-v1.md) retains that row as diagnostic evidence, not a recommended publisher profile. A future Ironclad support deal needs a distinct obligation or cost and its own screen.

Let `raw_cash=floor(B_cents×cash_share_bp/10000)`, `marketing_points=floor((B_cents−raw_cash)/3500)` and `funding_cents=B_cents−3500×marketing_points`. The final cash includes any sub-$35 marketing remainder, so `funding_cents+3500×marketing_points=B_cents` exactly. The $35 conversion is a trial budget equivalence, **not** a player exchange rate or final economic valuation. Funding is transferred once at deal/BeginProject commit as a new, labeled **nonrecourse publisher support advance**. It is spendable then and may service ordinary due bills under the existing finance ordering. It uses a new finance transaction and cash-reconciliation field, **not** legacy `publisher_receipt`: it is excluded from operating revenue/profit, sales earned/settled, Bank underwriting and direct profit-derived Credit improvements. It can still help the Studio pay due bills on time and thereby affect Credit through the ordinary arrears path. It creates no Bank loan or debt bill. Marketing points are bound to this project and added once to its launch Awareness alongside existing banked Promotion, **before trait conversion**, without consuming another publisher's Promotion or affecting an older release.

The publisher's title-only recoupment uses the authoritative cumulative **raw** post-platform net `N_cents` after the existing 30% platform retention. Through the title's first 12 release-age earning months, `publisher_recoup_cumulative_cents = min(B_cents, floor(N_cents×2000/10000))`. **`B` includes both cash and the $35-per-point imputed marketing value**; the publisher may therefore withhold more title sales than the Studio received in cash, a term that must be explicit at acceptance. Each earning cycle commits only the positive delta from the prior keyed cumulative share. Studio-rights earned for that cycle equal raw post-platform net earned less the new recoup delta, and only those Studio-rights earnings enter Studio sales/Bank credit. Calendar-month settlement pays the accumulated Studio-rights amount once. After the title's 24th productive earning cycle (release-age Month12), the publisher share freezes permanently and later sales are fully the Studio's, including when the 24th cycle is not a global settlement boundary. If sales cannot repay the budget, the shortfall is the publisher's risk: no Studio debt, arrears, interest or off-title claim. Finance shows raw title sales, platform retention, publisher recoup and Studio net separately; no double 30% deduction.

The accepted deal freezes Review threshold, Month3 unit threshold, share/cap and six-earning-cycle observation point. Review is the existing frozen launch Review. Units are the title's **actual cumulative earned units** after its sixth productive earning cycle (release-age Month3), not projected Month1 units, settled cents, calendar-month number, or all-Studio sales. At that once-only observation, if both thresholds pass: `I_p=min(10000,I_p+1000)`; otherwise `ΔI_p=0`. Reputation does not change. A late sales recovery does not retroactively pass the Month3 promise; a miss does not claw back funding or marketing. Existing/old releases are never enrolled after launch.

At Investment15 and neutral Review7.0/Awareness100/market1.0, the [arithmetic screen](PUBLISHER-SYSTEM-ARITHMETIC-FINDINGS-2026-10-18-v1.md) puts SideStreet at $700 upfront, +5 Awareness, 1,098 Month3 units, and only +$13.81 cumulative Studio receipts versus no support by Month12. This is a **reference sensitivity check**, not a legal campaign or proof of value. Lower demand/quality can make support worse after recoup; higher Investment can make it materially better. The screen omits rent, development liquidity, Fanbase, issuance scarcity and the future value of Investment. The first playable trial must test those branches at legal cash and calendar boundaries.

## Event, save and player-facing contract

**Identity and ordering:** one new relationship event per committed **qualifying Reputation Brief** result, fully qualified commissioned release, or successful supported-game Month3 milestone. Existing five cash Contracts emit none. Use source offer/deal/release IDs and an event-kind key; reject previews, retries, duplicate callbacks and unsupported old states without mutation. At launch, preflight the frozen commission terms and finance/release plan, then commit its fee, title rights, raw sales seed, Studio sales entitlement, Fanbase, Promotion consumption, publisher unlock and checkpoint as **one atomic release operation** before scene handoff. A failed step leaves the prior state intact; retry with the same commit ID is a no-op after success. The current graphical path calls `LaunchSettlement.freeze_results()` before `StudioPhase.setup()`/`RunState.register_release()`, so a fee appended after registration would be too late. Preserve immutable raw sales records for validation and Fanbase, with a separate keyed rights/recoup ledger for Studio income. Keep Studio-only Continue; no inferred mid-phase save. The current checkpoint uses an exact revision/shape: bump content revision, migrate every supported prior version explicitly, initialize zero relationships/deals only for pre-feature saves and never backfill awards from old results. Version every accepted term set so later balance changes do not rewrite active or completed deals. A frozen support quote cannot be upgraded by another game's mid-development Investment gain.

**Player explanation:** each publisher page shows Reputation, Investment capacity, next commission gate, quote availability and the reason a cooldown or Investment step blocks another support deal. The commission screen shows the fixed full fee, underdelivery salvage band, required Review/frozen positive Scope/content, publisher ownership/no Studio sales, unavailable Studio-paid campaigns and banked Promotion consumption. The support screen shows upfront funding, targeted marketing, 20% title-only recoup **capped by total cash-plus-marketing budget**, Review and Month3 unit targets, observation timing and future Investment reward. Results show actual grade, fee/recoup, title owner and exactly why a milestone passed or missed. The Bank/Finances screens distinguish non-operating publisher support from earned sales, ordinary publisher work-Contract receipts and debt.

**Current integration boundary:** the existing [code-owned Contract registry](../../../../patch-notes/scripts/contracts/contract_definitions.gd) covers five Primitive work offers only; [RunState](../../../../patch-notes/scripts/run_state.gd) records publisher offers, Promotion, releases and finance but has no relationship/deal ledger. [Launch settlement](../../../../patch-notes/scripts/simulation/launch_settlement.gd) freezes Review/units at release, [ReleasedGameSales](../../../../patch-notes/scripts/sales/released_game_sales.gd) earns raw units/net by title age, and [StudioFinanceLedger](../../../../patch-notes/scripts/finance/studio_finance_ledger.gd) currently routes `publisher_receipt` into operating income/profit. Commission sales rights, campaign exclusion and support advance/recoup therefore require new typed release/finance/checkpoint paths; adding IDs to the existing work scorer is insufficient.

## Adoption order and remaining uncertainty

1. **Publisher relationship foundation:** typed per-Studio/per-publisher ledgers, idempotent events, old-save zero baseline and read-only UI, with no automatic awards on old cash offers.
2. **One reputation-bearing Brief:** SideStreet two-hand terms after broader legal-route tests; keep Ironclad's first offer introductory. Test release-paced issuance, grades, exact ledger event and rollback.
3. **One commissioned-game pilot:** SideStreet tier1, project-bound quote, full/salvage fee, publisher-owned sales, Fanbase and release-gate behavior, finance and old-save migration.
4. **One supported Studio game pilot:** SideStreet funding/marketing, title-only capped recoup, capacity-step issuance and six-earning-cycle criteria. Test low/neutral/high market, partial/missed criteria, support accepted before project start, actual cashflow, replay, both-size presentation and exported Studio Continue.
5. **Broader publisher profiles and tiers:** Crown/Neon/Starwave and tiers2–3 only after the pilots show legal access and understandable tradeoffs. Ironclad support needs a new non-dominant proposition; its cash-only row is rejected. Revenue share beyond the 12-month capped pilot, exclusivity, control, multi-Studio publisher competition, premium content prerequisites and final balance remain separate future decisions.
