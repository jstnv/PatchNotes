## Delivery scope

Implement an extensible, Studio-owned search source and the one-cycle search action, accessible through current Contracts/Publisher UI and headless commands. Recommended first live catalog is SideStreet cash work, Reputation Brief and R20 commissioned game using their **existing** rewards, challenge rules, hand counts, commission fee/Review/Scope/ownership and standing awards. SideStreet-first is the bounded implementation recommendation; no new higher-publisher terms or Reputation thresholds are approved. Registration must allow later explicitly approved templates without adding arbitrary disk-authored evaluators.

Operating defaults in the direction document: select publisher/type before paying; one durable offer per successful search; no extra cash fee, no empty-result charge, no randomized term reroll; at most one new pending/active offer per publisher/type, with legacy pending work preserved first. No per-release cooldown after completing search-sourced work. A repeatable contract can support another search without making a game; commissions retain their existing released-game/preparation prerequisites. No shared Investment/Reputation currency or auto-scaling reward multiplier.

## Minimum behavioral matrix

1. SideStreet unlocked at low standing: short cash/Brief searches legal when financially eligible; commission locked below R20. At R20 with no released game, explain the remaining release prerequisite; do not charge a search that cannot supply eligible work.
2. Valid search: cycle +1, one receipt/offer, no direct payout or standing increase. Exact cold Continue finds the same offer. Repeating the committed request cannot charge or generate again.
3. Accept and finish a searched Brief: normal two productive hands and exact once-only standing; another search available without an intervening release. Zero/partial/full results all preserve source validity. Two perfect Briefs have an arithmetic lower bound of six cycles including searches, not a promised legal outcome.
4. Cash searches preserve base and challenge rewards, and cash results/counts remain distinct from search actions and Briefs. Normal rent/Bank/payroll still apply; search does not give future payout credit for affordability.
5. Prepared commission invitation at R20: cancel/reprepare, invalid/stale Begin, successful bind, full/partial/zero delivery, ownership and once-only rewards. Existing support eligibility and title-only recoup unchanged.
6. Legacy pending cash/Brief, backlog, active/completed contracts and accepted commissions survive migration with identical historical terms and receipts. Their eventual completion does not charge a missing historical search.
7. First unlock still issues only its original qualifying introduction once; later releases do not refill search-enabled repeatable work. No auto-offer on migration. Cross-publisher/owner IDs and forged search snapshots reject.
8. Failed search across a calendar/bill boundary is atomic; successful search crosses that boundary once. Search quote remains the pre-search choice even if another system changes standing during its productive cycle.
