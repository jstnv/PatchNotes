## Recommendation

**PROPOSED** first reputation Brief prototype: use a distinct optional one-hand SideStreet Relationship Brief *after* the already selected independent cash-brief direction; freeze its accepted pool/terms, forbid successful redraws, grade the Scope promise full at <=6, half at7 and zero at>=8, and normalize one-hand completion against Scope5 and each Core2. Compute candidate outcome grade `min(C,G)` in fixed-point basis points for the Publishers owner to consume once. Compared with `(5,3)`, `(5,2)` allows five rather than one full combined choices in the actual observed deal while still requiring a blend of all four Cores; the 25/7/3 promise split remains unchanged. No maximum reputation award, cash/Promotion rule, unlock cadence or migration rule is approved by this study. A private playable variant and additional legal draws are needed before any implementation handoff.
