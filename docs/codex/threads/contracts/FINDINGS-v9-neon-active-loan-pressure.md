## Recommendation and limits

**Retain the existing Neon ACTIVE/TRIAL terms; do not retune or lock final balance.** This one early-Neon route verifies that a low-cash Studio can carry an active loan and queue commitments without corrupting the Contract or finance behavior, but actual settlement made it solvent before the Neon payout mattered. It therefore does **not** establish a survival or arrears-rescue value for Neon. It does show that the $0 acceptance advance cannot supply pre-hand cash; the completion payout follows two productive hands. The deliberate queue admission policy is a stress case, not an estimate of normal player purchases or a recommendation to buy those nodes. No Crown-first loan route, real arrears case, Fanbase interaction or exported Continue was tested. The shared To Do List and gameplay were untouched. A player-facing pilot review is a better next balance signal than another broad advance grid; any actual-arrears study needs a separately justified legal low-sales foundation.
