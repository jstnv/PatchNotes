## Sports/1104: observed first-hand choice did not leave a solvable second hand

The second distinct Brief, sourced from Game 2, began with seven visible token IDs `0..6`. The tape's play request used `[1,3,4,6]`: `multiple_endings`, `power_ups`, `sound_effects`, `keyboard_and_mouse`. The three surviving original tokens in the final serialized state were `0:design_pass`, `2:sound_pass`, `5:menu_system`; the play event added replacements `7:sound_pass`, `8:split_screen`, `9:sound_pass`, `10:sound_pass`. Thus the full **observed** first deal comprised `design_pass`, `multiple_endings`, `sound_pass`, `power_ups`, `sound_effects`, `menu_system`, `keyboard_and_mouse`; the **observed** second deal comprised `design_pass`, four `sound_pass`, `menu_system`, `split_screen`. Neither deal contains a Graphics contributor in the frozen card ledger.

The chosen first hand produced Scope 5 and Core half-units Graphics 0 / Sound 6 / Technology 14 / Design 6. The remaining `menu_system` (Scope 2), `split_screen` (Scope 3) and passes could have made exact aggregate Scope 10, but every possible second-hand quartet leaves Graphics at 0, below the full Brief's required 6 half-units (3 points). This is a genuine no-redraw impossibility **at that observed second deal**, not an arithmetic ranking error when the policy rejected it. Both older and current-market runs reached the same stop digest `909dd64ab68d6f782722c3102de7a034a948e43fb3a5c2f987065f5414f6a543` and the same visible card names. They are still separate source revisions.

This does **not** prove that Sports/1104 can never earn two full Briefs. A different first-hand selection could change exhaustion and the replacement draw, producing a different second visible deal. The audit neither simulates those counterfactual draws nor assumes they match the retained draw.

## Recommendation and remaining question

For the proposed R20 commission access pilot, use legal 1108/1112 prefixes and regenerate them under any new descendant private commission variant; do not relabel a prior-revision checkpoint. No seed replacement or Brief value revision is needed to establish *existence* of legal access. If player-facing Brief feasibility becomes the question, predeclare a bounded same-source comparison that branches the first-hand policy across visible legal quartets from an identical pre-Brief prefix, retains all changed second draws and rejected results, and uses fresh-process exact replays for selected outcomes. That separate experiment would test first-hand strategy effects; this audit cannot. The proposed `Scope 10 / each Core 3 / no redraw` terms, R10 award, R20 gate, commission and support economics remain **OPEN**. No adoption or balance approval follows.
