## Declared cases and measurements

- Enumerate all possible four-card multisets per specialty with zero to four unique owned Primitive Features plus renewable Passes, retaining only four-card choices with unique Features. All eight specialty starter rosters are included. No actual seven-candidate draw, redraw policy, purchased Feature, Studio checkpoint, RNG or policy choice is simulated. Equal opportunity counts must not be interpreted as draw probabilities or player frequencies.
- Compare no-redraw Scope grades using `(maximum,fade_excess)` candidates **A=(4,4), B=(5,3), C=(6,2)** with the current `(12,4)` as control. The grade is `max(0,10000 - floor(10000*max(0,Scope-maximum)/fade_excess))`; any successful redraw sets the combined grade to zero. The latter redraw case is a structural control, not an observed sampled redraw.
- As a separate completion-shape probe, calculate `C=floor(10000 × (4×min(Scope,6)+Σmin(CoreHalf_i,6))/48)`. This is the existing SideStreet weighting scaled to one hand, not a proposed approved score rule. Report its maximum and distribution by specialty and whether high promise grade can coexist with meaningful completion. Do not use current two-hand `C` as a one-hand payout.
- Measure, per specialty and candidate: roster size, maximum attainable one-hand Scope, counts of combinatorially possible full/partial/miss Scope grades, minimum/maximum one-hand completion score, highest completion while the promise is full, maximum of the weaker completion/promise grade, and counts with both grades at least 5000 or both full.
