# Contracts v5: integrated Promotion through two settlements

2026-10-07 (America/Los_Angeles). **Read-only finding; no new rule approval.** This answers whether the implemented Crown/Neon bounded pilot's earned Promotion produces material *settled* sales after the promoted launch, and what the two Contract cycles cost in a legal no-offer continuation.

## Observations

All three native trial/cash-only pairs had identical pre-branch state, offer ID, Contract hands/result, accepted state, later card/draw/selection trace, Review, and launch cycle. The award alone changed launch Awareness and sales. Each arm reached `L+4` with a valid finance ledger, nonnegative cash and zero overdue bills. Cash-only did not consume Promotion; trial consumed precisely the earned target award.

Neon reached 80/86 completion in both cohorts: $0 advance, $1,451.16 completing cash and floor(20×80/86)=18 Promotion. Crown reached full completion: $150 once at acceptance plus $1,770 at hand 2, $1,920 total and 12 Promotion. These are the existing trial terms, not a new cap recommendation. Target-title settled gains exactly matched the trial-minus-cash-only end-cash gains in all three pairs; other observed finance paths did not change. At `L+4`, trial and cash-only credit scores matched (618, 621, 633 respectively); no arrears were present. The no-offer credits were 621, 621, 636, reflecting its different calendar and earnings path.

The no-offer arm had **more target-title sales settled by the same calendar date** because it launched two cycles earlier. Yet trial end cash exceeded no-offer by $1,080.67 (current Neon), $775.88 (Lease Neon), and $1,188.78 (Lease Crown), including Contract payout and all intervening sales/expenses. These combined differences are **not** Promotion-only estimates. Crown's 12-point Promotion moved its Game 4 frozen Awareness from 114 to 126. Both Crown Contract arms unlocked Starwave through the existing **three completed Contracts** condition; the no-offer arm had only two completed Contracts and did not unlock it. Starwave's offer remains unimplemented. The Starwave difference is **not** a Promotion effect.

## Limits and recommendation

Three deterministic legal continuations are not a frequency estimate or final cap optimization. They have no active Bank loan, arrears, cash block, post-launch campaign, Fanbase integration or exported Continue interaction. The Crown cohort had already completed Neon, so it cannot answer whether Crown Promotion makes Neon newly available. The no-offer comparison changes launch date and subsequent development, and the cash-only award suppression cannot be performed by a player. Source was a dirty worktree pinned by hashes, not a committed build.

**Recommendation:** retain the already approved **ACTIVE/TRIAL** Crown 12 and Neon 20 maximum Promotion package for the bounded pilot; do not retune or lock final balance from these runs. The measured settled value is modest next to direct Contract cash in these routes, while the two-cycle opportunity cost is visible. A focused Crown-before-Neon route would answer whether next-launch Promotion makes Neon available earlier; this cohort cannot. A genuinely liquidity-constrained or active-loan route may also be warranted before final economic balance. Keep final Crown/Neon tuning, Starwave offer details and player-selected Neon focus **OPEN**.
