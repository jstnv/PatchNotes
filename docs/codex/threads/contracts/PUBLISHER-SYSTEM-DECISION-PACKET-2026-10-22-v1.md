2026-10-22 (America/Los_Angeles). **Designer recommendation; all new rules and values PROPOSED.** This packet gathers the Contracts thread's current publisher-system proposal and private evidence for review. It is not a gameplay implementation, a numeric lock, or a shared To Do List entry. The independent-client cash Brief remains the selected first new Contract type.

## Proposed pilot rule set

| Stage | Proposed player rule | Intended dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 — relationships | Each Studio has separate, per-publisher Reputation and Investment capacity in hundredths from 0 to 100. Existing cash Contracts grant neither automatically or retroactively. Typed committed events apply once. | Versioned Studio checkpoint, publisher identity, old-save zero baseline and rollback. |
| P2 — SideStreet Reputation Brief | A separate two-hand Brief, issued after a later Studio release, awards `floor(1000 × min(completion_bp,promise_bp)/10000)` Reputation basis points once: full 10.00, half 5.00, missed promise 0. Initial Ironclad stays introductory. | P1, frozen promise, legal offer cadence and player-visible result. |
| P3 — SideStreet tier-1 commissioned game | At Reputation 20.00, bind a quote before a project starts. Full Scope against a positive frozen requirement and Review at least 6.0 pay a $6,000 flat fee and grant 15.00 Reputation plus 15.00 Investment. The publisher owns title sales; the Studio retains Feature technology and Fanbase from play. Below full delivery, a prequoted Review/Scope grade scales only the fee and grants no relationship gain. | P1–P2, atomic project/release/finance/rights transaction, owner-aware views and saves. |
| P4 — SideStreet supported Studio game | At Investment 15.00, quote an $875 package: $700 upfront as nonoperating cash and five launch Awareness, with 20% of this title's raw post-platform sales recouped up to the full $875 through earning cycle 24. Review at least 7.0 **and actual cumulative units at least 1,000** at earning cycle 6 award 10.00 future Investment once. The Studio owns remaining sales; a miss creates no clawback or debt. | P1/P3 title-rights and finance seams, support issuance guard, age/criteria ledger and owner-aware views. |
| P5 — broader publishers | Crown, Neon and Starwave profiles and commission tiers 2–3 remain deferred. The prior cash-only Ironclad support profile is rejected as a playable proposal. | Legal content and economic evidence after P3–P4 acceptance. |

The full formula, quote, issuance, save and explanation definitions are in [publisher design v3](PUBLISHER-SYSTEM-DESIGN-2026-10-18-v3.md). [HANDOFF](HANDOFF.md) owns the implementation tasks and acceptance criteria; this packet does not change their **PROPOSED** status.
