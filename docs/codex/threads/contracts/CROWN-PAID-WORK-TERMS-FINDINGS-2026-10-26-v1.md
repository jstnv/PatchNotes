## Calendar and payroll limit

Monthly rent is$500. A fresh Search+two-hand job starting on an even completed cycle crosses one bill date; starting on an odd cycle crosses two. Both cases cross one date before the completing-hand receipt. The constructed screen assumes payroll is already fully active and ignores sales, debt, prior arrears, hire/training expenses and startup timing. Net quote-minus-bills is not spendable-cash solvency.

At the$1,500 cap, all six quotes exceed one$500 rent bill; five exceed two rent bills. The low quote is$136.37 below two rent bills. With$1,000 monthly payroll, even one combined$1,500 bill exceeds all six quotes. Two-staff$2,000 and raised$2,200 payroll scenarios also exceed every quote. Existing sales/reserves may cover these costs; the study did not test that. Do not inflate cash awards until every staffing choice passes or describe this job as an arrears rescue.
