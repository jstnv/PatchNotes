# SideStreet Reputation Brief — proposed pilot recommendation

2026-10-23. **PROPOSED pilot terms; bounded research complete.** The [72-case screen and three exact replays](SIDESTREET-ACCESSIBILITY-AND-PACING-FINDINGS-2026-10-23-v1.md) support useful partial progress with the existing candidate targets. User authorized accessibility/pacing research while P1 implements. P1 foundation approval does not approve these P2 terms. This note supplements the [v3 promise](SIDESTREET-RELATIONSHIP-BRIEF-PROPOSAL-2026-10-18-v3.md) and [release cadence proposal](PUBLISHER-REPUTATION-CADENCE-PROPOSAL-2026-10-18-v1.md).

## Recommended player loop

Keep SideStreet's existing cash Contracts intact. Offer a separate optional two-hand Relationship Brief after introductory Ironclad, with later opportunities sourced from subsequent releases. Each hand costs one productive cycle. Taking three briefs therefore uses six productive cycles; the brief itself grants no cash, Promotion or Investment under this candidate.

Keep the proposed full-delivery targets at10 Scope and3 points in each Core, with a no-redraw/small-Scope promise. Explain partial delivery as useful trust gained. Two perfect results can reach proposed R20; three ordinary graded results are the intended accessibility question. Never make perfect completion a hidden requirement for receiving Reputation or accepting a later brief.

The study deliberately takes three briefs even when R20 is reached after the second. Its fourth-release cash difference measures that fixed three-brief policy, not the minimum cost of reaching R20. A player may stop taking optional briefs once the desired standing is reached. A separate stop-at-gate policy would be needed to measure that exact economic route.

One pending/active brief per publisher/type, no backlog, no immediate replacement upon completion. A pending offer persists when the player concentrates on games or other Contracts. Preserve one globally active Contract. Old saves receive prospective opportunities rather than historical awards or a stack of past offers.

## Candidate formula, unchanged

Let S be aggregate Scope and H_i the four resolved Core half-unit scores after exactly two committed hands.

`C = floor(10000 * (4*min(S,10) + sum(min(H_i,6))) / 64)`.

`G = 10000` at Scope<=10 with no successful redraw, `5000` at Scope11, otherwise0. A successful redraw makes G0. Failed/rejected redraw requests must not count. `award_bp = floor(1000 * min(C,G) / 10000)`, where100bp is one displayed Reputation point. Award once through P1's trusted result resolver; the source must be the immutable accepted brief/result, never an externally supplied grade.

This screen's Scope8 alternative changes only its shadow completion denominator to56 and capped Scope term to8. It does not implement that alternative, change player decisions or establish its economics. Prefer retaining a clear upper target if partial grades already provide dependable progress; make any target change an explicit later decision.

## Presentation needed before a playable pilot

- Before acceptance: two hands, two productive cycles, Reputation-only reward, exact targets/promise, and the benefit that standing will unlock. Keep it distinct from the cash Contract.
- After hand1: actual cumulative Scope and each Core, remaining hand, redraw status and live projected grade. Do not promise a full result from unseen cards.
- On completion: show the exact earned amount such as `+8.43 SideStreet Reputation`, the total standing and why the grade was partial. Positive partial delivery should not be labeled a failed Contract.
- Do not advertise commissioned games as playable until that dependent type actually exists. The proposed R20 gate is not proof that a Studio can deliver a commissioned game's Review/Scope requirements.
