# Crown Compact Feature Package — design candidate v1

2026-10-26. **PROPOSED; existing rewards unchanged.** User delegated development of this assignment after the recommendation to investigate completion, Core balance and Scope limits. This authorizes design/research, not approval of new numerical terms or canonical activation. [Findings](COMPACT-PACKAGE-FINDINGS-2026-10-26-v1.md) · [review handoff](COMPACT-PACKAGE-HANDOFF-2026-10-26-v1.md).

## Purpose and recommendation

A short cash job: “Deliver a compact, well-rounded feature package. Cover the requested work and keep the final Cores close together.” Its purpose is paid delivery; the separate Balanced Craft Brief builds Reputation. Preserve the one-shot Crown introduction and all existing rewards.

Continue with a **completion payment plus separate balance/size bonus**. Compare the two component grades openly. Do not penalize base completion when the challenge is missed. This matches the existing separate-bonus direction and generic minimum-of-challenges evaluator.

Three possible structures were considered:

| Structure | Tradeoff / disposition |
|---|---|
| Balance-only paid assignment | Simplest and a useful control, but adds little mechanical distinction from Crown's existing challenge. |
| Balance plus Scope ceiling, affecting only the bonus | Preferred research candidate. Can create a paid-work tradeoff while preserving earned base cash. The current evidence does not yet establish that the ceiling creates a useful decision. |
| Hard Scope cap, failed completion or reduced base pay on challenge miss | Reject for this proposal: contradicts the requested separate-bonus approach and needs new failure/legality behavior. |

The ceiling equals the completion Scope target in the research case. Full size and full completion therefore require final Scope exactly9; the fade permits partial bonus above9. This is an intentional precision tradeoff to investigate, **not proof it is beginner-friendly**. A zero challenge remains possible from either imbalance or oversize. Selecting fewer than four cards, a special pass, abandon/retry and card-pool filtering are not added.

## Formula proposal and settlement contract

Let S be final Scope and h1..h4 final Cores in integer half-units. For the Core4/Scope9 candidate:

`N = 2*min(S,9) + sum(min(hi,8)) + 2*min(min(hi),8)`; `D = 66`.

Base completion is N/D. Base cash, once its cap A in cents is approved, is `floor(A*N/D)`. Do not first round a displayed percentage and then multiply cash.

`balance_bp = max(0,10000 - 1250*max(0,max(hi)-min(hi)-4))`.

`size_bp = max(0,10000 - 2500*max(0,S-ceiling))` with the proposed fade4.

`bonus_bp = min(balance_bp,size_bp)`; cash bonus `floor(B*bonus_bp/10000)`, and Promotion `floor(P*bonus_bp/10000)` only if those components/caps are separately approved. Do not multiply the challenge grades or fold base completion into the bonus formula. Challenge perfection and full base completion are different facts; the existing framework permits full challenge with partial completion. Do not silently add a minimum-work bonus gate to hide that distinction.

No settlement occurs during selection. Freeze terms at the appropriate offer/acceptance boundary, preserve typed Search identity and actual legacy offers, and settle once at completion through existing finance/calendar authority. Exact new source IDs/migration are implementation design work after terms approval, not part of this offline screen.

## Progression and player information

The proposed sequence remains qualitative: discover Crown → earn trust through Briefs → gain an optional paid package → later qualify for a commissioned full game. Lower work remains available; neither a perfect Brief nor completing this package is a hidden commission requirement. Commission qualification and Support Investment gates remain separate decisions.

Before Search, expose the assignment type, eligibility and Search cycle cost. Before accepting, show two hands, completion targets, size tolerance, base/bonus limits and any actual advance. During play show **Base payment** separately from **Challenge bonus**, with provisional first-hand language, raw scores and explicit balance/size reasons. Do not reuse the Crown Brief's zero-Reputation reminder for this cash job. Example: full work plus zero challenge still displays earned base payment. A full challenge plus partial work must not say “full payout.”

## What the evidence changes

The existing [catalog](CROWN-SEARCH-CATALOG-DESIGN-2026-10-25-v1.md) proposed this composite but had no concrete compatibility result. The new screen supplies three recorded joint-full paths, from three of six progressed cohorts. It also finds8/18 reached final hands with no positive balance option, unaffected by any tested ceiling. Ceiling9 reduces only4/630 alternative bonuses and removes just2 positive choices. The size rule may be largely redundant under the old Brief-focused policy. Do not sell this as a solution to Brief affordability or zero streaks.

**Next research:** keep Core4; compare ceiling9 versus10 and a balance-only control using candidate-specific two-hand planning. Preserve every failing route and target a bounded expanded legal cohort with varied Scope supply. Use Pareto outcomes for base completion versus bonus until actual new payout terms exist; no cash-optimal policy or profitability conclusion can be defined without them. New full campaigns need a named private simulator version, registered terms/source, regenerated legal prefixes and the isolated runner. Existing feedback implementation proceeds independently.
