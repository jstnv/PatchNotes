## Interpretation and recommendation

**Retain the current ACTIVE/TRIAL Contract terms.** One costly legal first-game strategy established Crown/Starwave reachability in one additional sampled specialty and seed. It did not clear Crown for the other four specialties, and it delayed early releases by six cycles. The sample has two fixed seeds, one trait bundle, one deterministic choice policy and matched specialty/Genre, so it cannot isolate roster from Genre target or claim broad fairness. Later Starwave opportunity cost for Sports was not compared to a matched skip branch. No advance, cap, Promotion or gate retune is approved or handed off by this finding.

The next design question is whether Crown access should be judged through a small set of **player-feasible first-game strategies** (including Store purchases and alternative Core/QA allocations), or whether the current bounded trial is sufficient until a broader balance pass. A new study needs a separate predeclaration and should avoid simply extending the seed search.
