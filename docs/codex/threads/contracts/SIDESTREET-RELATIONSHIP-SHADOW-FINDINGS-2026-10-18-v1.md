## Recommendation and limits

Retain the Scope10/Core3 and Scope10/11/12 no-redraw shape as a **private design candidate**. The completion branch, the three promise grades and a successful redraw branch now have actual committed two-hand witnesses with exact replay. The shadow event has no publisher identity, no frozen distinct offer and no ledger, so it is **not** implementation-ready. Before approving a type or filing an adoption packet, choose release-paced eligibility/coexistence and the publisher reputation award/ledger ownership, decide cash/Promotion and Starwave-count treatment, then test broader legal seeds/specialties and an actual typed Brief with rollback, duplicate/rejection, old-save and presentation gates. Do not promote this candidate because these runs passed.
