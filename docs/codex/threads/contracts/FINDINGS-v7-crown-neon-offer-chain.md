## Recommendation and limits

The chain is **conditionally attractive in cash by cycle 76** on this solvent, deterministic route. That does not show it is too strong: its two-cycle delay causes an early cash deficit, lower matched-calendar sales and less Game-5 progress, and the study stops before the next release's sales. Four arms from one seed cannot estimate how often Crown unlocks Neon, how players value timing, or what happens with arrears, an active Bank loan, Publisher Connections, Store research or Fanbase. The cash-only arm is an analytical intervention, not a legal choice.

**Recommendation:** retain the already approved Crown/Neon **ACTIVE/TRIAL** terms. Do not retune the 12/20 Promotion caps or send a balance implementation task on this evidence. Before a final lock, the more discriminating follow-up is to carry the same legal arms through Game-5 launch and two actual sales settlements at a common calendar, and separately test an actual liquidity-constrained/loan case. Keep Starwave offer terms, final publisher balance and player-selected Neon focus **OPEN**. The shared To Do List was not edited by this study.
