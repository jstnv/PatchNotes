## Player-facing shape

SideStreet may offer a separate **two-hand Relationship Brief** alongside its existing two-hand cash Contract. The player sees the promised objective, two productive hand cycles and reward terms before accepting. Accepted type/version, offer identity, publisher, eligible pool, hand count, promise, and reward terms are frozen. Normal four-of-seven hand play, finite Features and renewable Passes apply. The UI shows cumulative Scope/Core progress after hand1; only committed hand2 produces the final Contract result and any publisher-grade event. Existing SideStreet cash, challenge bonus and Promotion remain unchanged.

**Scoring is OPEN.** A useful first private comparison is the existing SideStreet no-redraw and total-Scope<=12/four-Scope fade challenge as a control versus a distinct reputation-oriented promise. Normalize completion against reachable **two-hand aggregate** Scope/Core targets; do not reuse v1's Scope5/each Core2 one-hand formula. Grade a completed aggregate once, not twice by averaging separate one-hand payouts or by letting the first hand irrevocably fail a recoverable total-Scope objective. A successful redraw may affect the whole promise only if that is explicitly chosen and tested for two hands. The proposed Contract-to-Publishers output remains a committed, immutable offer/publisher/type/version/grade record consumed at most once; the Publishers owner decides actual standing award and effects.

## Length and reward ladder

Use two hands as the ordinary new-Contract length. A later premium Contract can have three hands **if** its expected net benefit at comparable access/difficulty is meaningfully greater after the extra productive cycle, rent/credit and delayed game release. This can be cash, Promotion, standing or a combination clearly shown in the offer; it is not an automatic cash threshold. Four hands remain deferred. Current Ironclad/SideStreet/Crown/Neon two-hand and Starwave three-hand terms are not retroactively reclassified or changed.
