## What the evidence supports

Crown $150 is the smallest tested advance that clears the reproduced $147.65 historical rent block and permits both actual native Contract hands. It preserves a meaningful reason to use Ironclad earlier: three legacy routes that skipped Ironclad need $300 to rescue their later Crown checkpoint, while their still-available $400 Ironclad offer works. A $150 Crown offer would not be a universal bailout. The matched $5,700 historical start needs no Crown advance.

Every legal Neon arm completes both hands with A=$0. Advances add cash at partial completion without improving access in this cohort, so no advance is supported here. These are policy-conditioned recommendations from a small deterministic cohort, not estimated player success probabilities or final economy balance.

## Explicit payout and later implementation acceptance

For candidate maximum C cents and fixed-share completion n/d, pay **A once at accepted zero-cycle offer**, then **floor((C−A)×n/d)** on the committed second hand. Total is A at zero completion and C at full completion. No recoupment, repeated advance or extra completion bonus.
