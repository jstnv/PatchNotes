|---|---:|---:|---:|
|Action|29|31|30|
|Puzzle|27|29|30|
|Simulation|24|26|30|

Finite Features can contribute Scope once in their assigned phase; renewable Passes add zero Scope and the project freezes its supply at Begin. These are inventory ceilings, not guaranteed draws/affordability. More hands alone cannot fill this Scope shortfall. Other traits, employees or acquisition routes were not tested by this inventory bound.

Thirty Scope is a full-completion target, **not a new hard launch/Crown requirement**. The29-Scope Action witness proves that below-target releases can qualify. Its seed4521 Game4 Review7.1 includes favorable review variance; its highest measured production/Scope/bug combination without variance rounds to6.6. Seed4522 reaches7.0 on Game3 and has a best variance-neutral diagnostic around7.2. These post-run arithmetic diagnostics use recorded values; they are not rerolled simulations or proposed rules.
