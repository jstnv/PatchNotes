## Question and hypothesis

Can a legally earned SideStreet Investment capacity of 15.00 support a **Studio-owned** next game from a frozen pre-`BeginProject` quote, transfer $700 once, add five launch Awareness before traits, recoup at most $875 from only that title's raw post-platform net, and award 10.00 future Investment only if the frozen Review and actual sixth-earning-cycle unit criteria both pass? Hypothesis: support may improve development liquidity even when the long-run cash gain is small or negative; the current AI market can change the sales path enough that formula-only screens do not predict the legal campaign result. A same-revision no-support arm from the **identical Studio envelope and RNG** is the control.

All values are candidate trial terms. At `I_p=1500` basis points, `i=floor(I_p/100)=15`; `B=min(300000,50000+2500*i)=87500` cents. SideStreet's 8,000-basis-point cash share gives `raw_cash=70000`; `marketing_points=floor((87500-70000)/3500)=5`; the remainder-preserving `funding_cents=B-3500*marketing_points=70000`. The cash grant is not debt, operating income, work-Contract `publisher_receipt`, sales earned/settled, or direct Bank/Credit profit. The $875 cap includes the imputed $175 marketing value. Review must be at least 7.0 and **actual** earned units at least 1,000 at the end of the title's sixth productive earning cycle, which is release-age Month 3. A miss gives no Investment, does not claw back funding, and cannot pass later. The 20% share freezes after the title's 24th productive earning cycle; later raw sales belong fully to the Studio.

## Exact current-source seams and candidate delta

1. [BeginProject](simulators/publisher-progression-market-v1/project/scripts/simulation/begin_project.gd) prepares the project and market targets before spending one productive cycle. Freeze quote and eligibility against the **pre-cycle** Studio relationship state, then bind one typed deal to that project ID and commit its new `publisher_support_advance` cash transaction atomically with the beginning cycle. The beginning cycle can mature an older title's milestone, but that gain cannot qualify or upgrade the new quote. Reject a second game deal, retrospective acceptance, a stale quote, duplicate commit, insufficient/invalid source identity, or a failed project start without mutation or RNG drift. The new deal should inherit tested commission release-rights and finance seams, not be grafted onto the older Brief-only variant.
2. [PrimitiveAwarenessCalculator](simulators/publisher-progression-market-v1/project/scripts/awareness/primitive_awareness_calculator.gd) currently adds banked Promotion and then trait conversion. Add only this project's frozen five support points before trait conversion, once, alongside ordinary banked Promotion. Preserve both sources separately in the release explanation and saved terms. A failed launch consumes neither support's one-time marketing use nor banked Promotion. Existing title releases and unrelated publisher awards are unchanged.
3. [ReleasedGameSales](simulators/publisher-progression-market-v1/project/scripts/sales/released_game_sales.gd) stores actual per-title `earned_units`, cumulative post-platform `entitlement_cents`, raw `settled_cents`, `total_earned_cycles`, and frozen per-age market factors. Its `is_valid()` reconstructs the raw record. Keep this **raw** record unchanged for market history and Fanbase. Add a separate keyed release-rights ledger for cumulative publisher recoup `R`, Studio-rights earned `E`, and Studio-rights settled `S`. On each earning cycle through age 24, let `N` be that title's new cumulative raw `entitlement_cents`, set `R=min(B,floor(N*2000/10000))`, and commit only `R-R_previous`; after age 24 keep `R` frozen. `E=N-R` at every cycle. On a global settlement boundary, settle the positive difference `E-S_previous` and then set `S=E`; on other cycles `S` is unchanged. Check `0<=R<=B`, nondecreasing `R/E/S`, `S<=E`, and no other title's entitlement or receipt changes. Raw `N` has **already** had the platform retention applied; do not deduct it twice. A settlement may occur on a different calendar parity from the title's age-24 cutoff.
4. [RunState._plan_productive_cycle](simulators/publisher-progression-market-v1/project/scripts/run_state.gd) currently sums every title's raw entitlement and raw settlement deltas into `StudioFinanceLedger.plan`. For supported titles, send the **Studio-rights** deltas above instead. Preflight all titles, market snapshots, recoup ledger changes, Fanbase, finance and cap arithmetic together before committing. Observe the milestone exactly when `total_earned_cycles` becomes 6, using that raw record's `earned_units` and the title's launch Review in tenths; append a single immutable pass/miss event keyed by deal/release/criterion version. `I_p=min(10000,I_p+1000)` only on a pass; `R_p` never changes from support. Duplicate callbacks and cold restore cannot replay an award. Retain the candidate support deal's accepted budget/share/criteria if later balance values change.
5. [StudioFinanceLedger](simulators/publisher-progression-market-v1/project/scripts/finance/studio_finance_ledger.gd) currently puts positive `publisher_receipt` into `other_income_cents`, operating revenue/profit, and profit-derived Credit. Add a distinct positive support funding kind and monthly row/journal field. It must enter cash reconciliation before ordinary due bills and arrears service, but not `other_income_cents`, `operating_revenue_cents`, `net_profit_cents`, Studio sales, or Bank sales underwriting. Its exact journal replay validator and report projection must include the new field. Ordinary on-time bill payment may still indirectly affect Credit; this is a real cash benefit, not a profit credit. Studio sales earned/settled and Bank-qualified sales use `E/S`, not raw `N`; raw units remain in market/Fanbase records. [StudioPhase](simulators/publisher-progression-market-v1/project/scripts/phases/studio_phase.gd) currently labels raw entitlement as Studio revenue, so the candidate presentation must distinguish raw post-platform sales, publisher recoup, Studio rights, upfront support, and cap remaining.

If the commission cannot legally qualify within the declared fresh-route ceiling, report support **BLOCKED**, not a synthetic Investment grant.

One case accepts support with the next legal game at its pre-BeginProject quote; the other declines and self-publishes that same Studio-owned game. Use the same visible-choice policy and development inputs while legal. Do not inject cash, cards, ratings, Investment, market factors, or a checkpoint ID; retain and report a policy rejection or divergence. A later second specialty/seed may be selected only in a newly versioned plan after a legal route is demonstrated. The historical arithmetic screen's favorable scenarios are not legal current-market starting states by themselves.
