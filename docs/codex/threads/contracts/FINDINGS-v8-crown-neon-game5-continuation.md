## Recommendation and limits

**Retain the approved Crown/Neon ACTIVE/TRIAL terms.** The v7 cash advantage persists through Game-5 settlements on this one solvent route, while earlier releases continue to earn sooner and the chain remains two Game-6 cycles behind. The pilot is worth human play; this result does not justify a cap/Promotion retune or a final numerical lock. The separate next design question is whether Neon cash changes survival, borrowing or project access under an **actual active-loan or arrears constraint**. Predeclare one legal constrained foundation before running it; do not infer that from this abundant-cash route. Starwave offers, final publisher balance and player-selected Neon focus remain OPEN.
