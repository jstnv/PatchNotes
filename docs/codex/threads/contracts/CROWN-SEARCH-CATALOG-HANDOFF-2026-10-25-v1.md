## Next study question matrix

**Preparation only, not an executable preregistration.** Exact source, terms, cohort seeds and command/release/time budgets must be frozen before runs. No simulations were launched from this matrix.

| Question | Comparison | Measurements |
|---|---|---|
| Can a newly eligible Studio afford to use Search? | Legal lower-resource and established Crown entry cohorts, identified from recorded play rather than edited money | Search legality/block reasons, cash/arrears/debt, productive cycles, opportunity cost and survival |
| Does short work provide a usable trust route? | Brief-led search, release-led development with a fixed occasional-search rule, no-search control from identical eligible Studio states within each cohort | Attempts/offers/completions, requested/credited R, zero/partial/full results, cycles and releases to approved milestones; report censored/unreached milestones |
| Does understandable planning matter? | Fixed visible greedy versus existing simpler expected-final-grade policy under identical terms | Legal choices, outcome differences, observed zero results; no future draw/RNG inspection, no redraw-policy tuning |
| Can a later cash composite actually be completed? | Only after CC3 terms: legal owned-pool witnesses, then fixed policy cohort | Joint completion/bonus grades, overshoot, reward components and failure causes; no advance/reward sweep without separate scope |

Separate same-source state/RNG-matched comparisons from cross-revision evidence. New Search cadence can change source receipts and RNG trajectories; regenerate legal prefixes if revisions are incompatible. Do not inject unlocks, standing, money, cards or offers. Keep exact equal-state entry checks and Studio-only Continue. Use the documented isolated runner with a unique thread variant/output/profile and immutable ancestry. Record all rejected, incomplete and timed-out cases; reduce reproducible command failures where applicable. Stop at predeclared command/release/time bounds or failed state compatibility, never extend only a losing arm or change seeds for favorable results.

Measure cycles from entry as well as equal-release endpoints: rent/credit costs are part of the career choice. Do not claim a candidate R20 milestone unlocks a canonical Crown commission. Include once-only search debit/offer/settlement and frozen-term persistence checks before interpreting reward outcomes. Human understanding of the provisional preview is a separate playable gate.
