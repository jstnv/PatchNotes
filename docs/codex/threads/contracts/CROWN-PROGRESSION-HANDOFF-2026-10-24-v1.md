## Recommended next experiment, not executed here

Start with the Crown Brief only. Use the working Action/Cult Following seed4417 legal unlock route as a bridge; declare a wider no-trait specialty cohort separately rather than presenting its results as the same starting state. A pre-run plan must choose fixed seeds, policy phase budgets, purchase limits, maximum release/command/cycle horizons and stop-at-R20 behavior. Target three opportunities after Crown discovery as an accessibility question, not a forced result; report insufficient-opportunity cases and delayed unlocks. Freeze controls and candidate mechanics before running, and verify shared pre-intervention state/RNG. Minimum, product and softer spread choices require explicit separate candidate versions if compared in gameplay.

Do not enable a generic publisher loop by merely substituting Crown's ID into SideStreet-only trusted resolvers. The inspected `PublisherRelationshipBrief` rejects non-SideStreet definitions and reconstructs denominator64/limited promise grades; Crown needs its own authored denominator66 and all permitted balance grades, validated from immutable accepted sources. P4 support similarly contains SideStreet-specific amounts/assessment semantics. Generalization must preserve accepted old versions, keep code-owned evaluators and avoid making persisted data choose arbitrary rule functions.

Main unresolved question: can normal visible-card choices earn useful Crown Reputation without excessive releases or cash pressure? Until that is answered, the numeric matrix is a proposal and the shared TODO stays unchanged.
