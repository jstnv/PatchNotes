On the Lease route this crossed Neon's existing 125 threshold (114→126); the ordinary route is a negative control (111→123). No Neon profile was unlocked before either branch. Source inspection shows [`register_release`](../../../patch-notes/scripts/run_state.gd) calls `_refresh_publisher_unlocks()` on that launch, which also creates Neon's first-unlock pending offer; **the pending-offer ID itself was not separately captured in these raw traces**.

Promotion alone added **$202.79** (ordinary) and **$258.74** (Lease) of Game-3 target-title *settled* sales by `L+4`; end-cash gains over cash-only were exactly the same. All six arms had nonnegative cash, zero overdue bills and valid finance ledgers. Trial and cash-only credit matched within each cohort (621 ordinary, 624 Lease). The no-offer arms had more Game-3 sales settled by the matched calendar because their launch was two cycles earlier. Crown trial end cash nevertheless exceeded no-offer by $1,696.36 ordinary and $1,384.58 Lease, including the $1,920 Contract payout and all timing effects. This is **not** a Promotion-only estimate.

## Interpretation and next decision

The **existing approved ordering** is demonstrably active: Crown Promotion can move Neon unlock to the next launch when unpromoted frozen Awareness sits close enough to 125. It does not do so in the 111→123 negative control. This is a conditional threshold result, not a measured frequency, a claim that Neon will always unlock one full game earlier, or a reason to change the threshold. The study stops before any Neon Contract is taken; it does not measure the cash/progression cascade after earlier Neon availability. Neither route had an active Bank loan, arrears or cash block; Fanbase is absent from this main source. The source is hash-pinned dirty `main`, not a committed build.

**Recommendation:** retain the approved Crown 12 / Neon 20 **ACTIVE/TRIAL** package without numerical retune. The next design question is whether the *conditional early Neon offer* produces an excessive Contract chain when actually played, versus the extra two Crown cycles and legal no-offer alternative. That requires one bounded Crown→Neon native continuation with matched calendar and settlement, not a broad advance grid. Keep final balance, Starwave terms and player-selected Neon focus **OPEN**. Do not add a shared TODO item or implementation task from this finding alone.
