## Exact arithmetic

With final Scope S and four Cores h in integer half-units:

`N = 2*min(S,9) + sum(min(hi,8)) + 2*min(min(hi),8)`; denominator66.

`base_cash_cents = floor(150000*N/66)`.

`balance_bp = max(0,10000 - 1250*max(0,max(hi)-min(hi)-4))`.

`bonus_cash_cents = floor(10000*balance_bp/10000)`.

`bonus_promotion = floor(4*balance_bp/10000)`.

Full completion and full balance would pay$1,600 and4 Promotion. Never calculate cash from a rounded displayed completion percentage. A challenge miss cannot reduce earned base completion. A perfect balance grade can coexist with partial work; do not add a hidden minimum-work gate. Scope above9 contributes no extra base completion but does not impose a new penalty.

## Why these candidates

**$1,500 base:** a middle research candidate between the inspected SideStreet$1,200 base and Crown introduction$1,920 cap. This is a judgment about the first pilot's scale, not a simulation-derived optimum. Across six retained two-hand outputs, hypothetical total cash is$863.63–$1,463.63, average$1,195.07. The$1,200 alternative is thinner; the$1,800 alternative offers more cash and stronger incentives to repeat the job. Compare the latter only as a declared sensitivity if later economic testing needs it, rather than raising fees until every salary arrangement survives.

**Separate$100/4 bonus:** preserves a familiar existing challenge shape while letting base work carry most cash. Reusing those constants does not approve their new use. Promotion is banked under existing next-launch rules; it is neither converted to dollars nor included in cash-optimal policy claims. Repeated earning/next-launch consumption needs explicit testing before adoption.

**R5:** makes some earned Crown trust unlock a new work role without requiring a full Brief. In the retained three-Brief histories, five of six routes reach it: one after one Brief, four after two. R10 admits four; R20 admits one. R0 admits all already-discovered Crown Studios and remains the explicit alternative if the design goal becomes immediate liquidity. R5 does not fix the zero-Reputation route, which remains excluded; no pity, hidden exception or free standing is proposed.

**No advance:** preserves the distinction between recurring work and introductory guarantees. It also means the player must afford the Search and first hand before publisher cash arrives. Do not describe this as a way out of any existing arrears block.

## Progression and dependency boundary

Reputation gates **access** to this cash package; its completion does not farm Reputation or Investment. Briefs remain the short trust route; commissioned full-game delivery and supported Studio games retain their distinct Investment role. No Crown commission threshold is approved by selecting R5 for a proposed short job.

Current canonical Search is implemented at SideStreet scope. A legal R5 Crown source depends on approved and accepted Crown Reputation progression; the private Brief prototype is not canonical activation authority. Before exposing this job, review that dependency explicitly and regenerate legal post-R5 prefixes. Do not use the R0 quote samples as already eligible paid-job starting states or splice them into a later gate-crossing route.

Completed or existing pending offers keep their terms. New Search must preserve the current typed receipt, frozen standing, ordinary same-type pending-work rule, repeated-command protection and source/old-save validation. The exact new definition/catalog version belongs to the later adoption packet. Browser copy should show why the job is unavailable before charging a cycle; an eligible current quote is revalidated on commit.

## What this does not promise

A three-cycle job crosses one or two monthly bill dates. At$500 rent and no payroll, five of the six quoted outputs cover even the two-rent case; one does not. With fully active$1,000 monthly payroll, none of those six quotes covers even one rent-plus-payroll bill. Existing sales/cash can help, but were not simulated here. The new employee reopening makes a fresh payroll-aware study necessary. These terms are optional cash work alongside production and sales, not a salary subsidy or guaranteed profitable loop.

Zero challenge does not mean zero cash. Conversely, more bonus does not always mean more cash: Puzzle4521's retained greedy result quotes$1,479.54 plus1 Promotion, while the two-hand balance result quotes$1,463.63 plus4 Promotion. Both are conditional quotes; neither establishes the best paid-work strategy. Test a cash-focused policy and a distinct Promotion-aware policy once terms are approved, with no invented dollar value for Promotion.
