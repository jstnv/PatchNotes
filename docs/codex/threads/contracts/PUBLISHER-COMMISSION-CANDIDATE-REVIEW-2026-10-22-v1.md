## Confirmed candidate behavior and limits

- [PublisherCommissionTrial](simulators/publisher-progression-commission-market-v1/project/scripts/contracts/publisher_commission_trial.gd) lines 11–19 implements the proposed Scope/Review minimum grade and flat-fee salvage arithmetic for tier 1. The constructed verifier checks full $6,000 at Review 6.0, $3,000 at 5.0, $5,700 at 5.9, zero at 4.0, and half-Scope capping. These are arithmetic/mechanics checks, **not** approved balance.
- [BeginProject](simulators/publisher-progression-commission-market-v1/project/scripts/simulation/begin_project.gd) lines 21–40 prepares the project, compares the exact current quote before spending the development cycle, restores RNG on rejection, and binds the deal after a successful cycle. The focused tamper check passes; the legal short route confirms one accepted quote. The development cycle can mature older titles, so a future support implementation must retain this pre-cycle quote boundary.
- [RunState](simulators/publisher-progression-commission-market-v1/project/scripts/run_state.gd) lines 949–965 disables Studio-paid post-launch campaigns for commissioned titles; lines 1399–1411 preserve raw title sales for Fanbase while excluding their earned/settled amounts from Studio finance and Bank inputs. Source inspection supports this path. The focused verifier only checks a planned next cycle, and the legal short case stopped before its first earning cycle.
- Commissioned launch still calls `StudioFanbase.register_release()` at `run_state.gd` lines 873–875 and consumes matching banked Promotion at lines 897–898. The legal short checkpoint had no pending Promotion, and no post-launch publisher-owned Fanbase earning cycle was observed. These behaviors require direct legal/cold verification rather than inference from source alone.

**Recommendation:** keep P3 and the $6,000 / Review 6.0 / R20 / +15 relationship values **PROPOSED**. Carry the confirmed UI/accounting and atomic observer findings into a new private candidate revision; then exercise a legal qualifying commission, committed post-launch earnings, rights/Bank/Fanbase accounting, save/restore, and paired support dependency before preparing adoption. The legal short miss and its replay are retained evidence, not a reason to change seeds, budgets, or grading.
