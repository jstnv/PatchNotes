# Publisher support renewal: executable design proposal

2026-10-23. **PROPOSED P4 structure and terms; private experiment authorized.** Existing P1 is implemented; approved SideStreet P2/P3 adoption is separately queued. This document refines [renewal proposal v4](PUBLISHER-RENEWAL-AND-FAILURE-PROPOSAL-2026-10-22-v4.md), without changing funding, marketing, targets, recoup or approved P2/P3 terms.

## Player contract

Support funds a Studio-owned game and adds the quoted marketing benefit. The publisher recovers only the quoted portion of that title's sales, up to its frozen cap and time limit. A missed promise awards no new Investment; it creates no debt, rating loss or funding clawback in this proposed pilot.

After the previous support assessment finishes, a new successful supported assessment or a full commission for the same publisher can qualify the Studio for another support offer. The relationship can keep producing opportunities at Investment100. A success at the cap must remain a committed result even when its credited Investment is zero.

## Alternatives and recommendation

| Approach | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- |
| Require10 additional Investment after each acceptance | Simple, but blocks renewal at the cap. |
| Clamp that threshold at100 | Allows another offer after a cap-level miss without new success. |
| **Require a new qualifying committed result** | Preserves earned recovery and cap behavior; requires ordered history and explicit provenance. Recommended for private testing. |

## Eligibility and frozen terms

Evaluate from the pre-start Studio state. First support requires the existing candidate Investment15 gate and legal project/transaction checks. Later support requires:

1. The latest support from that publisher has launched and reached its frozen assessment boundary. An unresolved result is neither a pass nor a miss.
2. A qualifying success committed after the latest acceptance and no later than this quote. Qualification is a passed supported assessment or a full same-publisher commission. Briefs, partial commissions, ordinary games and other publishers do not qualify.
3. A later Studio-developed release exists after the previous acceptance. The supported title itself can satisfy this, but assessment must still finish.
4. Investment remains at least15. Cap100 is allowed.

The quote freezes `previous_support_id` and `qualification_event_id`, in addition to existing project, source release, publisher, acceptance cycle, capacity and financial terms. First-support terms contain empty previous/qualification IDs. Repeat support selects the earliest qualifying committed result; native P1 adoption uses its ordinal to break ties. Preview/Back and rejected confirmation preserve that eligibility. A successful acceptance moves the boundary forward, consuming all older potential qualifications together: successes are not banked offer tokens.

A full commission earned while an assessment is unresolved may qualify after that assessment finishes. Results generated by the productive start cycle cannot retroactively improve the accepted quote. Older-title recoup can continue while a new deal runs; only one unresolved supported assessment per Studio–publisher pair is allowed.

## Chronological save acceptance

Verify underlying outcomes against actual frozen release/sales records before trusting them as qualification. Reconstruct Investment in commit order; require exact capacity at each acceptance, valid previous-deal links, correct qualification identity, a unique exact funding receipt, frozen assessment timing and conserved per-title rights. Preserve all prior deals and assessments. Reject inconsistent history atomically; never delete older records or credit later results earlier to make a save pass.

The private historical prototype derives deterministic order from validated cycle, assessment-before-release phase and stable project identity. This follows its zero-cycle launch after productive-cycle sales settlement. It is an experimental adapter, **not a replacement for canonical P1 event ordinals**. Reconciliation must use the current Studio-owned P1 ledger and actual commit order, including Founder/active-rival isolation. The old prototype's scalar ledgers and starter pool are historical and must not be transplanted.

## Player-facing availability reasons

- First access: “Earn more Investment with this publisher to unlock support.”
- Pending assessment: “Your previous supported game is still being assessed.”
- Miss without recovery: “Complete a full commission for this publisher to qualify for another support offer.”
- Qualified: show the successful game/commission that unlocked this offer, the frozen cash and marketing package, targets, assessment timing and title-only recoup cap/expiry.

These are proposed copy directions, not verified UI or human comprehension.
