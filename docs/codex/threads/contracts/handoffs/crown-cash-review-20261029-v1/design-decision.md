## Payroll and arithmetic results

**PASS:**288 independent base-payout cases and864 independent bonus cases, including integer floors, Scope above9, and0/1/4 redraw inputs. **PASS:**48 synthetic current-finance-ledger cases: new/mature hires ×0/1/2 employees ×both parities ×payments$0/$750/$1,500/$1,600. These use actual hire quotes and productive bill transitions, but synthetic$100,000 ledger funding and prescribed final receipts. They establish arithmetic/timing, **not legal R5 careers or achievable Contract grades**. [Source and process evidence](studies/crown-cash-20261028-v1/runs/payroll-fixtures-v2/experiment.log).

At the absolute$1,600 payment ceiling, mature staff and rent alone leave:

| Employees | One monthly bill within Search+two hands | Two monthly bills |
|---|---:|---:|
|0|+$1,100|+$600|
|1|+$100|−$1,400|
|2|−$900|−$3,400|

These are upper bounds before sales, debt, any other expense or hire fees. No Promotion-to-cash conversion is assumed. New hires cost$100 each, wages are$1,000 each/month, and first due is `hire_cycle+2+hire_cycle%2`; grace timing is tested separately.
