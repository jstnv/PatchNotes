## Remaining acceptance gaps

1. No human Studio quote/bind control exists yet; the legal flow used the headless CampaignSession action. Native visibility, affordance and comprehension are untested.
2. `register_release` commits sales/Fanbase metadata and calls `add_cash_cents` before storing the commission outcome, publisher owner, relationship award and consumed Promotion. `add_cash_cents` emits finance/cash signals, so a listener may observe a partial release. Move to one prevalidated release transaction with signals after all state is coherent.
3. The Studio per-title summary and monthly sales report label publisher-owned raw entitlement/settlement as Studio earnings, even though the aggregate finance/HUD excludes them. Keep raw publisher units/net visible, label owner, and show Studio income as zero for those titles.
4. Campaign rejection is generic; show publisher-owned rights clearly at Studio and explain why Studio post-launch campaigns are unavailable.
5. The v7 follow-up covers cold restore, earning/settlement, Bank ledger basis, Fanbase and one-time fee in a legal run. A focused automatic verifier should still assert these rights and aggregate ledger reconciliation at every save/restore boundary; Promotion and UI behavior were not exercised by that continuation.

**Recommendation:** keep v1 as experimental proof of legal access and full commission mechanics. Create a new private v2 candidate for the above repairs, regenerate a legal access prefix on that exact revision, rerun a bounded matched pair and cold-restore continuation. Do not adopt the trial amounts or code into canonical gameplay from this finding alone. Keep the prior source and failed bundles intact.
