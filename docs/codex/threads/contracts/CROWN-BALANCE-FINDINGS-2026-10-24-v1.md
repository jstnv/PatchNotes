## Recommendation

Keep the proposed Crown rollout on hold. Balance-aware selection improves standing in this cohort, but it does not establish dependable access to the proposed R20 commission gate. Test a first-hand policy that considers the three visible cards being retained, then a separate bounded legal-redraw arm, before deciding whether the reward formula needs changing. Preserve current cash Contracts and the approved SideStreet implementation.

If better legal play still stalls, compare a separately declared completion component plus a balance reward against the current minimum(completion,promise) formula. Reusing a difficult optional cash bonus as the sole limiter on Reputation can turn missed balance into zero progression. This is a design concern supported by the observed outcomes, not a ruling on a replacement split, easier threshold or higher award.
