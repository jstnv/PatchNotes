2. At most one pending/active Crown Brief; pending survives later releases and restore, missed opportunities do not stack, completion/dismissal does not immediately reissue. Migrated unlocked Studios receive no historical offers; only the next new release qualifies.
3. All Brief results have zero immediate cash, Promotion and Investment, including zero challenge cash/Promotion caps. Existing Crown cash caps/advance/bonus and all SideStreet results remain unchanged. Briefs do not increment legacy cash counts/Starwave prerequisites.

One global active hand-based Contract, legal departure/game-deal guards and independent pending offers remain.
