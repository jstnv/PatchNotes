# Starwave first playable offer — proposed direction v2

2026-10-08 (America/Los_Angeles). **PROPOSED, not approved or handed off for gameplay.** This develops the [scope choice](STARWAVE-SCOPE-v1.md) after the [v10 timing screen](FINDINGS-v10-starwave-shadow-timing.md). Existing authority already sets the profile gate, one persistent first-unlock offer, three hands, frozen owned Primitive pool and one active Contract; this note proposes the reward role inside that boundary. It does not set a cash amount, advance, formula, Promotion award or implementation task.

## Player proposition

Starwave's first offer should be an optional **longer commission that funds the next project**. The player spends three productive Contract cycles to earn a completion-weighted direct cash payment, with a restrained next-launch Promotion component if it earns one at all. The choice is whether to take a larger commission now or launch the next game sooner. The first slice can use the existing one-time receipt, completing-hand payment and keyed next-launch Promotion mechanics; revenue share, publisher control, exclusivity and larger campaigns remain separate future systems.

This gives Starwave a later-game role beyond the two-hand Crown and Neon offers without making a new publisher-stat currency. It also keeps the deal optional and pending if the player prefers immediate development. The accepted three-hand structure must keep seven candidates, four selected cards per hand, three retained candidates and four replacement draws before each later hand, finite owned Primitive Features and renewable Core Passes. These are **proposed continuation rules for analysis** where current source has no third-hand runtime; they need a card-feasibility screen before any handoff.

## What v10 changes

At the legal post-Neon Starwave-profile checkpoint, the Studio has $13,823.20 and Neon's 18 Promotion banked. Three analysis-only cycles delay Game 4 from cycle 72 to 75 and leave $1,527.26 less cash at matched cycle 79 without a reward. A fixed full $2,240 receipt can overcome that later cash gap on this one route, while lower cumulative sales and three fewer Game-5 work cycles remain. Extra 6 and 14 Promotion stack to 24 and 32 on Game 4; their measured two-settlement cash effects are $111.89 and $258.74 above the same fixed cash with no extra points. [V10](FINDINGS-v10-starwave-shadow-timing.md) did not draw, score or pay a Starwave hand, so these figures do not select a reward.

Screen zero versus a small acceptance bridge only on a separate legal cash-constrained route; this solvent v10 route cannot value a bridge. Avoid a hand-two installment in the first slice because it adds a new transaction and restore path.
3. Present the exact proposed terms, route coverage and limits for a user ruling. Keep the shared To Do List and gameplay untouched until that ruling and an explicit handoff.
