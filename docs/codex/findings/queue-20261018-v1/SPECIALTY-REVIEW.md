## First-game access and optional buying

- Recommended v2: Maps costs its unchanged $450; its owned Levels parent permits immediate zero-cycle optional buying. Before, Maps was free and Levels cost $450. Buying both yields the same final ownership set and $450 total optional spend under either roster.
- Exploration costs $300 with the proposed Levels grant. Under current Strategy/Simulation/Sports, acquiring unowned Exploration requires Levels ($450) first, then Exploration ($300): $750 total. The shared ancestor is counted once.
- No new cost for an already-owned child in Stage 1. All other specialty purchase routes retain the implemented four gates. Simple Story → Dialogue/Character Backstories and all downstream AND gates are unchanged by this proposal.
- No new draw, play, Scope completion or Review guarantee follows from ownership. A cheaper route to Exploration and the two-Core exchange could change first-game outcomes; that balance question is OPEN. All recommended initial Scope totals remain below 30.
