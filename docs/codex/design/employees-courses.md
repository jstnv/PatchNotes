## Employee-assisted Feature research direction — 2026-10-25

**LOCKED qualitative design, unimplemented:** An employee assigned to Feature Store research contributes one guaranteed **half research-cycle** of progress after each successful normal productive cycle. This is progress in the Store queue only: the run/calendar clock still advances by its usual whole cycle. Two assigned employees' half-steps stack to one full research-cycle of progress in that same productive cycle. The Store charges the relevant unpaid research share **when each half-step actually occurs**, with no charge for an absent boost. This develops the earlier selected direction that employees default to research and can advance the Store queue outside its manual Research button. The passive research duty is distinct from the Production Specialist's challenge benefit and grants no free Core score.
