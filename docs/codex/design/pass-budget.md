## Selected formula and unchanged rules

For each Design or Alpha phase independently:

`Total generated Pass Budget = 2 + floor(cumulative printed Feature Core in that phase / 8)`.

- Each phase begins with two budget, including an automatic refresh at the Design-to-Alpha transition. Feature activation immediately advances its phase's cumulative **printed**, not synergy-enhanced, Core. Pass activation spends one. Drawing and redrawing Pass candidates spends none.
- Candidate draws may not exceed the unspent budget after retained Pass candidates are accounted for. Preserve the existing seven-candidate maximum, four-card selected hand, Feature exhaustion, current Pass definitions/eligibility, specialization and Balanced Production, cash/calendar, and phase-specific rules. A pool can be shorter than seven once supply/budget is exhausted; canonical Alpha supports that and the short/empty-pool exits are verified.
- The project requirement stays **30 Scope**. The 35-Scope figure is the total printed Scope of the 24 available Primitive Features, not a requirement or engine capacity. Current player starting ownership remains 12 Scope. The 27-Scope player grant used in one study was a counterfactual, not this decision.
- Existing Review calculation, Action's absolute G40/S26/T40/D26 targets, Bugs/Beta, Store prices/eligibility, Feature/Pass card stats and synergies, Bank/Contracts, and rival roster/capital/quality targets stay unchanged.
