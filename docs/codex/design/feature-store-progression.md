## Locked progression direction / open numbers

**Feature research queue — selected 2026-10-07; implemented locally 2026-10-08.** A Store Feature selected for acquisition enters a research queue instead of the current purchase action automatically consuming a productive cycle and granting ownership. A Research button inside the Feature Store is the direct way to advance queued research. Ordinary actions outside the Store do not themselves advance that queue. In a later Employees phase, employees are assigned to research by default and are the intended way to advance queued research outside the Store. This is a direction for the future employee system, not a change to the current Production Specialist trial or a request to resume its deferred implementation. Queue membership is not ownership or project supply and never guarantees a draw or play. The [Feature Pools thread proposal](../../../patch-notes/design-logs/feature-pools/proposal-003-feature-research-queue.md) and [Store-only handoff](../../../patch-notes/design-logs/feature-pools/handoff-003-feature-research-queue.md) separate this bounded slice from future employee mechanics. The Store and advisory guidance now distinguish down payment, paid amounts, current installment and conditional remaining costs.

**Research payments and time — user ruling, 2026-10-07; implemented locally 2026-10-08.** Queue admission charges **half the Feature's base acquisition cost** as a down payment and advances **zero** calendar cycles. The other half is divided across the Feature's required successful research actions. Each Research action spends one normal productive run/calendar cycle and charges that action's installment; reject it without progress if the player cannot afford the installment and the ordinary productive-cycle preflight. Recompute the existing earned parent-familiarity discount for **each installment at the time of its research action**. More familiarity gained while research is paused reduces only later installments; paid amounts are not retroactively changed. For the user's illustrative $1,500 base/two-action Feature: $750 on queueing, then $375 per action before familiarity discounts. An action at 10% familiarity would cost $337.50 instead of $375. The down payment is based on base cost, not the discounted whole-card quote. No future sale may fund the current installment before that action commits. [Payment ruling and arithmetic boundary](../../../patch-notes/design-logs/feature-pools/decision-003-research-payment-rule.md).

**Duration boundary — user clarification, 2026-10-07:** keep the **existing one-cycle duration** for each currently live later Store and post-start Primitive reserve Feature when converting it to research. The live ledger has no per-node research-duration field. Thus a live $1,500-base Feature would charge $750 to queue and one $750 Research installment before any familiarity discount; 10% earned familiarity at that action makes the installment $675 and the total $1,425. The user's two-action/$375-installment example illustrates arithmetic for a possible future two-action Feature, **not** a new duration for live nodes. Future-node durations and exact employee integration remain **OPEN**; the later half-step direction is recorded immediately below. Current runtime uses the queue; initial zero-cycle starter purchases remain instant.
