## Current rules and implementation

Permanent ownership, purchased-parent branches, familiarity discounts, owned-pool summaries and navigable graphical map are implemented. Required specialty grants initial Primitive roster (see genre-specialties); optional unowned Primitive initial purchases cost exact cash and zero cycles before successful first-project departure. No $4,000/23-Scope starter caps. Post-start Primitive reserve and later nodes now enter a FIFO research queue with a zero-cycle half-base down payment; one paid Research action completes each live node. Ownership/child unlock/calendar/redraw/portfolio sales/rent commit centrally on completion. Browsing/failed/duplicate purchases mutate none of these.

Primitive purchase price=(printed Scope+1)×$150 ($300/$450/$600 for Scope1/2/3). Primitive Design/Alpha play fee=$10×(primary+secondary+2×Scope); Pass$0. Later-class Store Features currently have no locked play fee and runtime skips that charge; do not treat this implementation boundary as approved free future cards.

Familiarity comes from eligible committed project Feature usage once/project/ID; each of up to5 parent credits gives10% discount, capped50%. Credits themselves are not capped5. Some gates require explicit familiarity; ownership alone does not supply credits. Current branch catalog is authored separately from Primitive Contract supply. Do not enroll new/later Features in Contract pools without a ruling.

## Locked progression direction / open numbers

**Feature research queue — selected 2026-10-07; implemented locally 2026-10-08.** A Store Feature selected for acquisition enters a research queue instead of the current purchase action automatically consuming a productive cycle and granting ownership. A Research button inside the Feature Store is the direct way to advance queued research. Ordinary actions outside the Store do not themselves advance that queue. In a later Employees phase, employees are assigned to research by default and are the intended way to advance queued research outside the Store. This is a direction for the future employee system, not a change to the current Production Specialist trial or a request to resume its deferred implementation. Queue membership is not ownership or project supply and never guarantees a draw or play. The [Feature Pools thread proposal](../../../patch-notes/design-logs/feature-pools/proposal-003-feature-research-queue.md) and [Store-only handoff](../../../patch-notes/design-logs/feature-pools/handoff-003-feature-research-queue.md) separate this bounded slice from future employee mechanics. The Store and advisory guidance now distinguish down payment, paid amounts, current installment and conditional remaining costs.

**Research payments and time — user ruling, 2026-10-07; implemented locally 2026-10-08.** Queue admission charges **half the Feature's base acquisition cost** as a down payment and advances **zero** calendar cycles. The other half is divided across the Feature's required successful research actions. Each Research action spends one normal productive run/calendar cycle and charges that action's installment; reject it without progress if the player cannot afford the installment and the ordinary productive-cycle preflight. Recompute the existing earned parent-familiarity discount for **each installment at the time of its research action**. More familiarity gained while research is paused reduces only later installments; paid amounts are not retroactively changed. For the user's illustrative $1,500 base/two-action Feature: $750 on queueing, then $375 per action before familiarity discounts. An action at 10% familiarity would cost $337.50 instead of $375. The down payment is based on base cost, not the discounted whole-card quote. No future sale may fund the current installment before that action commits. [Payment ruling and arithmetic boundary](../../../patch-notes/design-logs/feature-pools/decision-003-research-payment-rule.md).

**Duration boundary — user clarification, 2026-10-07:** keep the **existing one-cycle duration** for each currently live later Store and post-start Primitive reserve Feature when converting it to research. The live ledger has no per-node research-duration field. Thus a live $1,500-base Feature would charge $750 to queue and one $750 Research installment before any familiarity discount; 10% earned familiarity at that action makes the installment $675 and the total $1,425. The user's two-action/$375-installment example illustrates arithmetic for a possible future two-action Feature, **not** a new duration for live nodes. Future-node durations and future employee payment/progress remain **OPEN**. Current runtime uses the queue; initial zero-cycle starter purchases remain instant.

**First-version Store-only queue — user-approved defaults, 2026-10-07; implemented locally 2026-10-08.** Only currently unlocked, unowned Features may be queued; a queued parent cannot satisfy a child's prerequisite. Multiple distinct eligible Features may wait in a visible first-in, first-out queue, with only the head receiving manual research. There is no cancellation or reordering in this version, so show the irreversible down payment clearly before confirmation. Grant ownership, child unlock and future project supply only when that Feature's final installment/research cycle commits. Required specialty grants and initial zero-cycle optional Primitive buying stay unchanged; post-start Primitive reserve uses the queue. Use integer cents: round an indivisible half-cent up into the down payment, split the remaining half into nominal installments with any remainder in the final one, and round each familiarity-discounted installment down to a cent. At zero discount, down payment plus all installments equals base cost exactly. Each committed Studio queue/research action must preserve its identity and payment/progress through the existing checkpoint boundary; rejected actions mutate none of them. Queue state and paid installments are not scheduled Bank/other due bills. The Store should show down payment, current next installment, remaining actions and conditional future cost, rather than reusing the current discounted whole-card quote as a guaranteed total.

New qualifying-release entitlements require actual required Scope, not owned Scope or bare release count. Calendar year is necessary for later-era Features:1984/1990/2000/2010/2020/2026 at cycles96/240/480/720/960/1104. Exact complementary qualifying counts, milestones, prices, multi-parent/three-Core mechanics and platform rules remain unapproved. Existing untagged first-era nodes use current behavior. Year label alone must not turn priority-change time grinding into approved progression.

Candidate Background Music: Sound3/Scope1, Music parent,$950 center-price trial.
