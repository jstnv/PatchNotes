## Current rules and implementation

Permanent ownership, purchased-parent branches, familiarity discounts, owned-pool summaries and navigable graphical map are implemented. Required specialty grants initial Primitive roster (see genre-specialties); optional unowned Primitive initial purchases cost exact cash and zero cycles before successful first-project departure. No $4,000/23-Scope starter caps. Same Primitive reserve after departure costs one productive cycle. Later Store node purchase costs one cycle with ownership/child unlock/calendar/redraw/portfolio sales/rent in the central atomic transaction. Browsing/failed/duplicate purchases mutate none of these.

Primitive purchase price=(printed Scope+1)×$150 ($300/$450/$600 for Scope1/2/3). Primitive Design/Alpha play fee=$10×(primary+secondary+2×Scope); Pass$0. Later-class Store Features currently have no locked play fee and runtime skips that charge; do not treat this implementation boundary as approved free future cards.

Familiarity comes from eligible committed project Feature usage once/project/ID; each of up to5 parent credits gives10% discount, capped50%. Credits themselves are not capped5. Some gates require explicit familiarity; ownership alone does not supply credits. Current branch catalog is authored separately from Primitive Contract supply. Do not enroll new/later Features in Contract pools without a ruling.
